Syllabus

10 Units — 5 Questions Each

हर unit में 5 questions और 10 marks होते हैं। Batch सभी दस units को fixed sequence में cover करता है।

01

Teaching Aptitude

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Teaching, Objectives, Levels of Teaching, Learner Characteristics, Teaching Methods, Evaluation आदि।

 02

Research Aptitude

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Research Types, Methods, Sampling, Hypothesis, Ethics, Research Process आदि।

 03

Comprehension

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Reading, Interpretation और Question-solving approach.

 04

Communication

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Types, Models, Barriers, Classroom Communication और Mass Communication.

 05

Mathematical Reasoning & Aptitude

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Ratio, Percentage, Average, Time & Work, Series और exam-relevant aptitude.

 06

Logical Reasoning

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Arguments, Fallacies, Analogy, Indian Logic और reasoning concepts.

 07

Data Interpretation

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Tables, Graphs, Charts, Percentage और Data-based Questions.

 08

Information & Communication Technology

05 Questions · 10 Marks

Internet, Digital Initiatives, Communication Technology, Cyber Concepts आदि।

 09

 10

Institutions, Policies, Governance, Regulatory Bodies और Indian Higher Education.
