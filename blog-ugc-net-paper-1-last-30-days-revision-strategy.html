UGC NET Paper-1 Last 30 DaysRevision Strategy

Last 30 days mein sahi revision plan follow karke aap maximum marks target kar sakte ho. Yeh practical roadmap hai.

10 Sep 2026 13 min readBy Dr. Shardool Sir
Last 30 Days = Game-Changing PhaseYeh 30 din syllabus complete karne ke nahi hain — yeh revision, mock tests aur weak areas fix karne ke hain. Agar sahi tarike se use kiya jaye toh is phase mein 15–20 marks ka jump aa sakta hai.

UGC NET Paper-1 ke last 30 days bahut decisive hote hain. Zyadatar aspirants is phase mein confuse ho jaate hain — kuch naya padhna shuru kar dete hain, kuch sirf PYQs pe atak jaate hain, aur kuch mocks dena band kar dete hain. Sach yeh hai ki is phase ka ek clear structure hona chahiye.

Is guide mein hum aapko ek week-wise practical roadmap de rahe hain — kaunsa week kis kaam ke liye, kaise revision karni hai, kitne mock dene hain, aur kaunsi galtiyan avoid karni hain. Yeh wahi strategy hai jisse GS Net Academy ke students ne last month mein apna score significantly improve kiya hai.

01

Why Last 30 Days Matter Most

Is phase mein teen cheezein clear ho jaati hain — kahan aap strong ho, kahan weak ho, aur exam hall mein kaisa perform kar rahe ho.

Revision

Short notes se

Mock Tests

2 din ke gap pe

PYQs

Roz 50 questions

No New Material

Last week mein

  • Revision se memory strong hoti hai — last month ka revision normal padhai se 3x effective hota hai.
  • Mock tests se exam temperament build hota hai — same pattern, same pressure.
02

Week-Wise Roadmap (Day 1–30)

30 din ko 4 weeks mein divide karo — har week ka ek clear target rakho.

Week 1 (Day 1–7)

Strong Areas Solidify

  • Unit-1, Unit-3, Unit-4 ka full revision
  • Short notes dobara likho — 1 page per unit
  • Roz 50 PYQs — topic-wise

Week 2 (Day 8–14)

Weak Areas Fix

  • Unit-2, Unit-6, Unit-7 — weak topics identify karo
  • Har weak topic ke 20 practice questions
  • First full-length mock do (alternate day)

Week 3 (Day 15–21)

Mock Heavy Phase

  • Alternate day full-length mock
  • Har mock ka 2-hour analysis — kya galat hua, kyun hua
  • Unit-5, Unit-8, Unit-9, Unit-10 ka revision

Week 4 (Day 22–30)

Final Revision Only

  • Sirf short notes + PYQs — koi naya material nahi
  • Last 2 din — sirf notes + formula sheet
  • Sleep cycle fix karo — exam timing ke hisaab se
03

Daily Routine — Last 30 Days

Last month mein 6–7 hours Paper-1 ke liye kaafi hai. Yeh daily split follow karo:

Revision Blocks

  • Morning 2 hours — new revision (short notes)
  • Afternoon 2 hours — practice questions
  • Evening 2 hours — PYQs + mock analysis

Mock Days

  • Fixed time slot — exam timing ke hisaab se
  • Paper-1 + Paper-2 dono solve karo
  • Mock ke baad — 2 ghante detailed analysis
04

Mock Test Plan — Kitne Aur Kaise

Last 30 din mein minimum 15 full-length mocks dene chahiye. Sirf mock dena kaafi nahi, uska analysis bhi zaroori hai.

Mocks

15+ minimum

Analysis

2 hours per mock

Target

75+ in last 5

Error Log

Maintain karo

Analysis Ka Format

  • Galat questions ki list banao — concept ya silly mistake
  • Unit-wise accuracy note karo (Excel/notebook)
  • Time per section track karo — kahan extra lagta hai
05

Common Mistakes in Last 30 Days

Study Mistakes

  • Naya material padhna — last month mein avoid karo
  • Notes na banane ki galti — revision mein time waste hota hai
  • Weak units ko skip karna — kam se kam 30% time weak areas ko do

Mock & Health Mistakes

  • Sirf mock dena aur analysis skip karna
  • Neend aur khane mein compromise — energy gir jaati hai
  • Last 3 din mein over-stress — relax raho, notes revise karo
06

Final Week — Kya Karein Aur Kya Nahi

  • Karein: Sirf short notes revise karo — morning 2 hrs, evening 2 hrs.
  • Karein: Ek mock alternate day — fixed timing pe, full syllabus.
  • Karein: Sleep cycle fix karo — exam slot ke hisaab se.
  • Na Karein: Naya topic, nayi book ya naya mock series start karna.
  • Na Karein: Group discussions mein zyada time — confusion badhta hai.
  • Na Karein: Social media pe "revision strategy" videos dekhna — apni strategy pe focus karo.
30 Days Left

Last 30 days ko smart banao!

?Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. Last 30 days mein naya topic padhna chahiye?

    Nahi. Last 30 days sirf revision aur practice ke liye hote hain. Agar koi topic bilkul chhoot gaya hai toh sirf uska overview dekho — deep dive mat karo.

  • 2. Kitne mock tests dene chahiye last month mein?

    Minimum 15 full-length mocks. Har mock ka 2-hour analysis zaroori hai. Sirf mock dena aur analysis skip karna waste of time hai.

  • 3. Weak areas pe kitna time dena chahiye?

    Total time ka 30% weak areas ko do. Baaki 70% strong areas + revision + mocks mein. Weak areas ko strong karne se hi jump aata hai.

  • 4. Last week mein kya karein?

    Sirf short notes revise karo, alternate day mock do, aur sleep cycle fix karo. Naya material bilkul nahi. Last 2 din mein sirf notes + formula sheet dekho.
