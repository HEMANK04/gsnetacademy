1. Educational Nature of Services

GS Net Academy is an educational and mentorship platform providing services that may include:

  • Live online classes
  • Recorded learning support
  • PDF notes
  • Previous Year Question discussions
  • Tests and practice questions
  • Revision sessions
  • Mentorship
  • Academic guidance
  • Exam preparation resources
  • Career or academic counselling

Program features may vary depending on the specific course.

Students should review the program description before enrolling.

2. No Affiliation With NTA, UGC or Government Authorities

Unless expressly stated otherwise, GS Net Academy is an independent educational platform.

GS Net Academy is not NTA, UGC, a university, examination authority, or government recruitment agency, and use of examination names is for educational and descriptive purposes.

Official eligibility, examination dates, syllabus, result, certification, admission, and recruitment decisions remain under the authority of the relevant official organisation.

Students should verify critical examination information from official sources.

3. Eligibility and Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for ensuring that:

  • They meet the official eligibility requirements of their target examination or admission process.
  • Information submitted during registration is accurate.
  • Their email and mobile/WhatsApp details are active.
  • They regularly check official examination notifications.
  • They maintain appropriate devices and internet connectivity for online classes.

Enrollment in GS Net Academy does not itself establish eligibility for any examination, admission, fellowship, or employment.

4. Registration Information

Students may be required to provide:

  • Full name
  • Contact number
  • Email
  • Subject
  • Target examination
  • Academic information
  • Preparation level
  • Payment details or transaction reference

False, misleading, impersonated, or fraudulent registration information may result in suspension or cancellation of enrollment.

5. Course Access

Course access is personal to the registered student.

A student must not:

  • Share login credentials
  • Share private Google Meet links
  • Share paid WhatsApp/community access
  • Allow another person to attend using their identity
  • Resell course access

Unauthorised sharing may result in suspension or termination of access.

6. Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise stated, course content provided by GS Net Academy—including:

  • Notes
  • PDFs
  • Slides
  • Videos
  • Class recordings
  • Tests
  • Questions
  • Diagrams
  • Teaching frameworks
  • Graphics
  • Website content
  • Course material

is owned by or licensed to GS Net Academy and is provided for the enrolled student’s personal educational use.

Students must not, without written permission:

  • Upload course videos to YouTube or other websites
  • Share paid PDFs publicly
  • Sell course material
  • Redistribute recordings
  • Create unauthorised Telegram/WhatsApp distribution groups
  • Reproduce substantial proprietary course content commercially
  • Remove branding/watermarks and republish the material

Reasonable personal notes made by students for their own study are permitted.

7. Recording of Classes

Recording a GS Net Academy class by a student using:

  • Screen recorder
  • Mobile camera
  • Audio recorder
  • Third-party software

is not permitted unless specific permission has been given.

GS Net Academy may itself record classes for academic and student-support purposes.

8. Academic Conduct

Students are expected to maintain respectful behaviour with:

  • Faculty
  • Staff
  • Mentors
  • Other students

The following may result in moderation, warning, suspension, or removal:

  • Abuse or harassment
  • Threatening behaviour
  • Spam
  • Deliberate disruption of classes
  • Sharing obscene or unlawful content
  • Impersonation
  • Fraud
  • Unauthorised commercial promotion
  • Repeated violation of class/community rules

Removal for serious misconduct does not automatically create a right to refund, subject to applicable law.

9. WhatsApp and Community Rules

Official learning communities are intended for academic communication.

Students should:

  • Keep messages relevant to the course.
  • Avoid unnecessary personal conversations in academic groups.
  • Avoid political, religious, discriminatory, abusive, or inflammatory content unrelated to the educational purpose.
  • Not privately solicit or spam other students.
  • Not promote unrelated courses or commercial services.
  • Protect other members’ privacy.

GS Net Academy may moderate groups to maintain a productive learning environment.

10. Class Schedule

GS Net Academy aims to conduct classes according to the announced academic schedule.

However, schedules may reasonably change because of:

  • Faculty availability
  • Examination developments
  • Holidays
  • Internet or technology issues
  • Academic requirements
  • Emergencies or unforeseen circumstances

Where reasonably possible, students will be informed of major schedule changes.

Replacement classes, revised schedules, or recordings may be provided as appropriate.

11. Recorded Class Availability

Recorded support, where included, is provided according to the specific program’s terms.

The academy does not guarantee that every live interaction, informal discussion, technical interruption, or incidental portion of every class will necessarily be available as a recording.

12. Study Material

Study material is designed to support examination preparation.

GS Net Academy makes reasonable efforts to maintain accuracy, but:

  • Examination syllabi may change.
  • Government policies may change.
  • Official facts or statistics may be updated.
  • Typographical errors may occasionally occur.

Students should consult official notifications for high-stakes examination requirements and immediately report suspected errors to GS Net Academy.

13. Previous Year Questions

Previous Year Questions may be used for:

  • Academic discussion
  • Examination trend analysis
  • Concept identification
  • Practice

Where questions originate from public examination material, ownership remains subject to the rights of the respective examination authority or lawful copyright owner.

GS Net Academy’s original explanations, analysis, notes, and presentation remain subject to applicable intellectual-property rights.

14. Tests and Performance

Test scores, mock ranks, feedback, and class performance are intended primarily for preparation and self-assessment.

They do not guarantee performance in the actual examination.

15. No Guarantee of NET/JRF or Other Selection

GS Net Academy does not guarantee:

  • NET qualification
  • JRF qualification
  • Assistant Professor eligibility
  • Ph.D. admission
  • Fellowship
  • Employment
  • Rank
  • Particular marks

Results depend on factors including:

  • Student effort
  • Paper-2 preparation
  • Revision
  • Attendance
  • Practice
  • Examination difficulty
  • Cut-offs
  • Category
  • Official examination rules

Our commitment is to provide structured educational support and academic guidance.

16. Student Results and Testimonials

Student results or testimonials displayed on the website or social-media platforms represent individual outcomes.

They should not be interpreted as:

  • Average expected performance
  • Guaranteed performance
  • Guaranteed admission
  • Guaranteed selection

Where photographs, testimonials, or results are published, GS Net Academy should use them with an appropriate basis or permission.

The amount payable for each program will be communicated before enrollment.

Some programs may be:

  • Free

For example, a program may state:

Course Fee: ₹0 Registration & Academic/Logistics Support Contribution: ₹500

The exact amount and inclusions applicable at the time of enrollment will govern that student’s registration.
