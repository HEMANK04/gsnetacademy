UGC NET Paper-1 Time Management StrategyExam Day Plan

Exam hall mein sahi time management se hi marks badhte hain. Yeh complete exam day plan hai.

10 Sep 2026 13 min readBy Dr. Shardool Sir
Time Management = Extra MarksSame knowledge, same preparation — lekin exam hall mein time management se 5–10 marks ka difference aa jaata hai. Yeh plan aapko batata hai ki 180 minute mein Paper-1 + Paper-2 kaise handle karein.

UGC NET mein Paper-1 aur Paper-2 dono ek hi session mein hote hain — total 180 minutes. Paper-1 mein 50 questions (100 marks) aur Paper-2 mein 100 questions (200 marks) hote hain. Zyadatar aspirants Paper-2 pe zyada time de dete hain aur Paper-1 ke last questions guess karte hain — yahi sabse badi galti hai.

Is guide mein hum aapko ek complete exam-day time management plan de rahe hain — kitna time kahan dena hai, kaunsa section pehle karna hai, aur kaise 100% questions attempt karne hain bina time waste kiye.

01

Exam Ka Time Mathematics

Pehle ye samjho ki time ka distribution kya hai:

Total Time

180 minutes

Paper-1

50 Q · 100 marks

Paper-2

100 Q · 200 marks

Ideal Split

45 + 135 min

  • Paper-1 ke liye 45 minutes kaafi hain — 50 questions, ~55 seconds per question.
  • Paper-2 ke liye 135 minutes — 100 questions, ~80 seconds per question.
02

Order of Attempt — Kaunsa Pehle

Sequence bahut important hai. Yeh order follow karo:

Paper-1 Order

  • Teaching Aptitude + Higher Education
  • Research Aptitude + Communication
  • ICT + Environment + People
  • Comprehension (5 Q, passage)
  • Logical + Mathematical Reasoning
  • Data Interpretation (last mein)

Strategy Behind This Order

  • Pehle easy + scoring questions — confidence build hota hai
  • Comprehension ko beech mein rakho — fresh mind chahiye
  • DI aur Reasoning last mein — inme time lagta hai
03

Section-Wise Time Allocation (Paper-1)

Paper-1 ke 45 minutes ko aise divide karo:

General Units (25 Q)

20 minutes

Comprehension (5 Q)

6 minutes

Reasoning (10 Q)

10 minutes

Data Interpretation (5 Q)

6 minutes

Review & Guess

3 minutes

Total

45 minutes

04

Question Selection Strategy

Har question nahi karna. Smartly chuno:

Pehle Attempt Karo

  • Definition-based questions — direct answer aata hai
  • Factual questions — years, names, full forms
  • Easy comprehension passages

Baad Mein Karo

  • Lengthy calculations wale (DI, Mathematical)
  • Complex reasoning questions
  • Passages jahan vocabulary difficult ho
05

Smart Guessing — No Negative Marking

Paper-1 mein negative marking nahi hai — matlab guesswork bhi smartly karo. Yeh rules follow karo:

2 Options Eliminate

50% chance — guess lagao

Ek Bhi Idea Nahi

Guess karo, blank mat chhodo

Extreme Options

Usually galat hote hain — avoid

All of the Above

Frequently correct answer

Longest Option

Often correct — but not always

Educated Guess

Blind guess se 20% better

06

Time Management Mistakes

Paper-1 Mistakes

  • Ek question pe 3+ minute lagana
  • Comprehension pe poora 15 minute kharch karna
  • Last 10 minutes mein 20 questions attempt karna

Overall Exam Mistakes

  • Paper-1 ke baad break na lena
  • Clock pe zyada nazar rakhna — stress badhta hai
  • Answer change karna bina strong reason ke

?Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. Paper-1 ke liye kitna time dena chahiye?

    45 minutes ideal hai — 50 questions ke liye ~55 seconds per question. Agar Paper-1 aata hai toh 40 minute bhi kaafi hain.

  • 2. Paper-1 pehle kare ya Paper-2?

    Paper-2 pehle karo (135 min), phir Paper-1 (45 min). Paper-1 chhota hai aur speed-based hai — isse last mein karna better hai.

  • 3. Kya Comprehension pehle karna chahiye?

    Nahi. Comprehension ko beech mein rakho — 5–6 minute. Bahut pehle kiya toh time waste ho jaata hai.

  • 4. Time khatam ho raha hai, kya karun?

    Last 10 minute mein sirf unattempted questions pe focus karo — sab attempt karo. No negative marking hai, toh blank chhodna bilkul waste hai.
