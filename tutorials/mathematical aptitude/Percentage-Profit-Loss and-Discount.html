Meaning of Percentage — प्रतिशत का अर्थ

Percentage का अर्थ है "per hundred" (प्रति सौ)। यह किसी संख्या को 100 के आधार पर व्यक्त करने का तरीका है।

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Example:

  • 25% means 25 out of 100 = 25/100 = 1/4
  • 50% means 50 out of 100 = 50/100 = 1/2
  • 75% means 75 out of 100 = 75/100 = 3/4

Key Point: Percentage always compares a part to a whole (100).

2

Percentage Formulae — प्रतिशत सूत्र

  • To find X% of Y: (X/100) × Y
  • To find what % of Y is X: (X/Y) × 100
  • To find value after X% increase: Original × (100 + X)/100
  • To find value after X% decrease: Original × (100 − X)/100
  • To find original value before X% increase: New × 100/(100 + X)
  • To find original value before X% decrease: New × 100/(100 − X)

Example 1: What is 20% of 500? → (20/100) × 500 = 100

Example 2: What % is 30 of 150? → (30/150) × 100 = 20%

3

Percentage Increase & Decrease — प्रतिशत वृद्धि एवं कमी

Percentage Change = (Change / Original Value) × 100
  • Percentage Increase: (New − Original) / Original × 100
  • Percentage Decrease: (Original − New) / Original × 100

Important: Always use Original Value as the denominator.

Example 1: A price increases from ₹200 to ₹250. What is the percentage increase?

  • Change = 250 − 200 = 50
  • % Increase = (50/200) × 100 = 25%

Example 2: A price decreases from ₹250 to ₹200. What is the percentage decrease?

  • Change = 250 − 200 = 50
  • % Decrease = (50/250) × 100 = 20%
4

Successive Percentage — क्रमिक प्रतिशत

When two or more percentage changes are applied one after another, we use successive percentage formula.

Net Change = A + B + (A × B)/100

Where A and B are percentage changes (positive for increase, negative for decrease).

Example 1: A number is increased by 10% and then by 20%. What is the net percentage change?

  • Net = 10 + 20 + (10×20)/100 = 30 + 2 = 32% increase

Example 2: A number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net change?

  • Net = 10 + (−10) + (10×−10)/100 = 0 − 1 = −1% (1% decrease)

For three successive changes: Apply formula twice: First combine two, then with the third.

Profit & Loss — लाभ एवं हानि

  • Cost Price (CP) — क्रय मूल्य: वह मूल्य जिस पर कोई वस्तु खरीदी जाती है।
  • Selling Price (SP) — विक्रय मूल्य: वह मूल्य जिस पर कोई वस्तु बेची जाती है।
  • Profit (Gain) — लाभ: SP > CP → Profit = SP − CP
  • Loss — हानि: CP > SP → Loss = CP − SP
Profit = SP − CP (when SP > CP) | Loss = CP − SP (when CP > SP)
6

Profit & Loss Formulae — लाभ-हानि सूत्र

  • If Profit% is given: SP = CP × (100 + Profit%)/100
  • If Loss% is given: SP = CP × (100 − Loss%)/100
  • To find CP when SP and Profit% are given: CP = SP × 100/(100 + Profit%)
  • To find CP when SP and Loss% are given: CP = SP × 100/(100 − Loss%)

Example 1: CP = ₹200, Profit% = 25% → SP = 200 × 125/100 = ₹250

Example 2: CP = ₹200, Loss% = 20% → SP = 200 × 80/100 = ₹160

7

Profit & Loss Percentage — लाभ-हानि प्रतिशत

Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100 | Loss% = (Loss / CP) × 100

Important: Profit/Loss percentage is always calculated on Cost Price (CP), unless otherwise specified.

Example 1: CP = ₹200, SP = ₹250 → Profit = ₹50 → Profit% = (50/200) × 100 = 25%

Example 2: CP = ₹200, SP = ₹160 → Loss = ₹40 → Loss% = (40/200) × 100 = 20%

Example 3: If an article is sold at a profit of 20%, and the profit is ₹40, find CP.

  • 20% of CP = 40 → CP = 40 × 100/20 = ₹200

Meaning of Discount — छूट का अर्थ

  • Marked Price (MP) — अंकित मूल्य: वह मूल्य जो किसी वस्तु पर लिखा/अंकित होता है। (List Price)
  • Discount — छूट: MP पर दी जाने वाली कटौती। Discount = MP − SP
  • Discount% — छूट प्रतिशत: (Discount / MP) × 100
Discount = MP − SP | Discount% = (Discount / MP) × 100

Example: MP = ₹500, Discount% = 20%

  • Discount = 20% of 500 = (20/100) × 500 = ₹100
  • SP = MP − Discount = 500 − 100 = ₹400
9

Discount Formulae — छूट सूत्र

  • SP after Discount: SP = MP × (100 − Discount%)/100
  • MP when SP and Discount% given: MP = SP × 100/(100 − Discount%)
  • Discount when MP and SP given: Discount = MP − SP
  • Discount% when MP and SP given: Discount% = (MP − SP)/MP × 100

Example 1: MP = ₹600, Discount% = 15% → SP = 600 × 85/100 = ₹510

Example 2: SP = ₹850, Discount% = 15% → MP = 850 × 100/85 = ₹1000

10

Successive Discount — क्रमिक छूट

When two or more discounts are applied one after another, they are called successive discounts.

Formula:

Net Discount = A + B − (A × B)/100

Where A and B are discount percentages.

Example 1: Two successive discounts of 10% and 20%. What is the net discount?

  • Net = 10 + 20 − (10×20)/100 = 30 − 2 = 28%

Example 2: MP = ₹1000, discounts of 10% and 20% applied successively.

  • After 10%: SP1 = 1000 × 90/100 = ₹900
  • After 20%: SP2 = 900 × 80/100 = ₹720
  • Net Discount = 1000 − 720 = ₹280 → 28%

Important: Successive discounts are not additive (10% + 20% ≠ 30% discount).

11

Formulae Summary — सूत्र सारांश

  • Percentage: (Part/Whole) × 100
  • Percentage Change: (Change/Original) × 100
  • Successive Percentage: A + B + (A×B)/100
  • Profit: SP − CP (when SP > CP)
  • Loss: CP − SP (when CP > SP)
  • Profit%: (Profit/CP) × 100
  • Loss%: (Loss/CP) × 100
  • SP from Profit%: CP × (100 + P%)/100
  • SP from Loss%: CP × (100 − L%)/100
  • Discount: MP − SP
  • Discount%: (Discount/MP) × 100
  • SP after Discount: MP × (100 − D%)/100
  • Successive Discount: A + B − (A×B)/100
12

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Percentage increase and decrease of same percentage are equal → ❌ 10% increase + 10% decrease = 1% net decrease (not 0%)
  • Trap 2: Profit% = (Profit/SP) × 100 → ❌ Always Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100
  • Trap 3: Discount% = (Discount/SP) × 100 → ❌ Always Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100
  • Trap 4: Successive discounts are additive → ❌ 10% + 20% ≠ 30% (actual = 28%)
  • Trap 5: SP = MP − Discount% → ❌ SP = MP × (100 − D%)/100
  • Trap 6: CP = SP × (100 + P%)/100 → ❌ CP = SP × 100/(100 + P%)

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1 (Percentage)

What is 15% of 200?

  • A. 15
  • B. 20
  • C. 30
  • D. 35
Answer: C. 30

Explanation: (15/100) × 200 = 30.

Question 2 (Percentage Increase/Decrease)

A price increases from ₹80 to ₹100. What is the percentage increase?

  • A. 20%
  • B. 25%
  • C. 30%
  • D. 40%
Answer: B. 25%

Explanation: Change = 20, Original = 80 → (20/80) × 100 = 25%.

Question 3 (Successive Percentage)

A number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net change?

  • A. 0%
  • B. 1% increase
  • C. 1% decrease
  • D. 10% decrease
Answer: C. 1% decrease

Explanation: 10 + (−10) + (10×−10)/100 = 0 − 1 = −1%.

Question 4 (Profit & Loss)

An article is bought for ₹200 and sold for ₹250. What is the profit percentage?

  • A. 20%
  • B. 25%
  • C. 30%
  • D. 40%
Answer: B. 25%

Explanation: Profit = 250−200 = 50; Profit% = (50/200)×100 = 25%.

Question 5 (Profit & Loss)

An article is bought for ₹400 and sold at a loss of 20%. What is the SP?

  • A. ₹320
  • B. ₹360
  • C. ₹400
  • D. ₹480
Answer: A. ₹320

Explanation: SP = 400 × (100−20)/100 = 400 × 80/100 = ₹320.

Question 6 (Discount)

An article marked at ₹500 is sold for ₹400. What is the discount percentage?

  • A. 10%
  • B. 15%
  • C. 20%
  • D. 25%
Answer: C. 20%

Explanation: Discount = 500−400 = 100; Discount% = (100/500)×100 = 20%.

Question 7 (Successive Discount)

Two successive discounts of 10% and 20% are given. What is the net discount percentage?

  • A. 28%
  • B. 30%
  • C. 22%
  • D. 25%
Answer: A. 28%

Explanation: Net = 10 + 20 − (10×20)/100 = 30 − 2 = 28%.

Question 8 (Combined Profit & Discount)

An article is marked at ₹600. After a discount of 20%, it is sold. If the profit is 20%, find the CP.

  • A. ₹360
  • B. ₹400
  • C. ₹450
  • D. ₹480
Answer: B. ₹400

Explanation: SP after discount = 600 × 80/100 = ₹480. Profit% = 20% → CP = SP × 100/(100+20) = 480 × 100/120 = ₹400.

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Percentage = (Part/Whole) × 100 | Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100 | Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100

Successive Percentage:

  • Net = A + B + (A×B)/100 (with signs)

Profit & Loss:

  • Profit = SP − CP | Loss = CP − SP
  • SP = CP × (100+P%)/100 | SP = CP × (100−L%)/100

Discount:

  • Discount = MP − SP | Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100
  • SP = MP × (100−D%)/100
  • Successive Discount = A + B − (A×B)/100

Exam Formula:

Always calculate on the right base: % on CP for profit/loss, % on MP for discount
