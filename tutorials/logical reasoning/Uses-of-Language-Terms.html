Uses of Language — भाषा के उपयोग

Language का उपयोग different purposes के लिए किया जाता है। UGC NET में four main uses of language हैं:

  • Informative — सूचनात्मक: To convey information or facts
  • Expressive — अभिव्यंजक: To express feelings, emotions, attitudes
  • Directive — निर्देशात्मक: To direct, command, or request action
  • Performative/Ceremonial — प्रदर्शनात्मक/आनुष्ठानिक: To perform an action or for ceremonial purposes
2

Informative Use — सूचनात्मक उपयोग

Informative language का उपयोग facts, information, knowledge को convey करने के लिए किया जाता है।

Informative = Conveying Information / Facts

Key Points:

  • It is true or false (can be verified)
  • It is the primary use of language in science and education
  • It is objective and descriptive

Examples:

  • "Delhi is the capital of India."
  • "Water boils at 100°C at sea level."
  • "The sun rises in the east."
  • "The exam will be held on Monday."
3

Expressive Use — अभिव्यंजक उपयोग

Expressive language का उपयोग feelings, emotions, attitudes, and personal experiences को व्यक्त करने के लिए किया जाता है।

Expressive = Expressing Emotions / Feelings / Attitudes

Key Points:

  • It is subjective and personal
  • It is not true or false in the factual sense
  • It conveys the speaker's inner state

Examples:

  • "I am so happy today!"
  • "This movie is boring!"
  • "I feel sad about what happened."
  • "What a beautiful sunset!"
4

Directive Use — निर्देशात्मक उपयोग

Directive language का उपयोग someone to do something के लिए किया जाता है — commands, requests, instructions, advice.

Directive = Commands / Requests / Instructions

Key Points:

  • It aims to influence behaviour
  • It is evaluated as appropriate/inappropriate, not true/false
  • It often uses imperative sentences

Examples:

  • "Please close the door."
  • "Don't touch the wires."
  • "You must submit your assignment by Friday."
  • "Take two tablets daily."
5

Performative/Ceremonial Use — प्रदर्शनात्मक/आनुष्ठानिक उपयोग

Performative language किसी action को perform करता है — saying something is doing something. Ceremonial language rituals, ceremonies, and formal occasions में प्रयुक्त होती है।

Performative = Saying is Doing | Ceremonial = Rituals / Formal Occasions

Key Points:

  • Performative utterances perform an action
  • They are effective if certain conditions are met
  • Ceremonial language is formal and ritualistic

Performative Examples:

  • "I now pronounce you husband and wife."
  • "I name this ship the Queen Mary."
  • "I promise to pay you back."
  • "I apologize for my mistake."

Ceremonial Examples:

  • "In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit..."
  • "We are gathered here today to..."
  • "With this ring, I thee wed."
6

Denotation — अभिधा/संकेतार्थ

Denotation किसी word का literal, dictionary meaning है — वह objective, explicit meaning जो word को refer करता है।

Denotation = Literal / Dictionary Meaning

Key Points:

  • It is objective and universal
  • It is the core meaning of a word
  • It is independent of context

Examples:

  • "Dog" = A four-legged carnivorous mammal (literal)
  • "Home" = A place where one lives
  • "Snake" = A long, legless reptile
  • "Rose" = A prickly bush or shrub with fragrant flowers
7

Connotation — व्यंजना/भावार्थ

Connotation किसी word के associated meanings, emotions, feelings, and cultural associations हैं — जो literal meaning से परे होते हैं।

Connotation = Associated Meaning / Emotional Meaning

Key Points:

  • It is subjective and context-dependent
  • It includes emotional, cultural, social associations
  • Different people may have different connotations for the same word

Examples:

  • "Dog" = Loyalty, friendship, love (positive) OR aggression, threat (negative)
  • "Home" = Comfort, safety, family, belonging (positive)
  • "Snake" = Deceit, danger, evil (negative)
  • "Rose" = Love, beauty, romance (positive)

Positive & Negative Terms — सकारात्मक एवं नकारात्मक पद

  • Positive Terms — सकारात्मक पद: Terms that indicate presence of a quality/attribute. (e.g., honest, brave, good, kind, beautiful, intelligent)
  • Negative Terms — नकारात्मक पद: Terms that indicate absence of a quality/attribute, often marked by prefixes like un-, in-, non-, dis-. (e.g., dishonest, cowardly, bad, unkind, ugly, unintelligent)
Positive = Presence of Quality | Negative = Absence of Quality

Important Notes:

  • Positive and negative do not mean "good" and "bad" in the moral sense
  • They refer to logical opposition — presence vs absence
  • "Atheist" (negative) vs "Theist" (positive)
  • "Non-smoker" (negative) vs "Smoker" (positive)
10

Absolute & Relative Terms — निरपेक्ष एवं सापेक्ष पद

  • Absolute Terms — निरपेक्ष पद: Terms that have no degree or variation — they are either true or false. (e.g., perfect, unique, complete, infinite, immortal, omnipotent)
  • Relative Terms — सापेक्ष पद: Terms that are comparative and depend on context or comparison. (e.g., tall, short, big, small, heavy, light, fast, slow)
Absolute = No Degree (All or Nothing) | Relative = Comparative / Contextual

Examples:

  • Absolute: "perfect" — something is either perfect or not; there are no degrees of perfection.
  • Relative: "tall" — what is tall in one context may be short in another (e.g., tall for a child vs tall for an adult).
11

Concrete & Abstract Terms — मूर्त एवं अमूर्त पद

  • Concrete Terms — मूर्त पद: Terms that refer to physical, tangible, perceptible objects that can be seen, touched, heard, etc. (e.g., table, book, tree, dog, car, mountain)
  • Abstract Terms — अमूर्त पद: Terms that refer to ideas, concepts, qualities, feelings that cannot be perceived directly. (e.g., love, justice, beauty, truth, happiness, democracy, freedom)
Concrete = Physical/Tangible | Abstract = Conceptual/Intangible

Examples:

  • Concrete: "That red apple on the table."
  • Abstract: "Justice must be served."
  • Concrete: "The car is blue."
  • Abstract: "Her kindness was appreciated."
12

Collective Terms — समूहवाची पद

Collective Terms refer to a group/collection of things, people, or objects as a single unit/entity.

Collective = Group as a Single Unit

Key Points:

  • A collective term denotes a collection, not individual members
  • It can be used with singular or plural verbs (depending on whether the group is seen as one unit or as individuals)
  • It refers to the entirety of the group

Examples:

  • "Class" — a group of students
  • "Team" — a group of players
  • "Army" — a group of soldiers
  • "Jury" — a group of jurors
  • "Committee" — a group of members
  • "Audience" — a group of spectators
  • "Crowd" — a group of people

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Informative language = only factual statements → ✅ But it also includes definitions, explanations, and descriptions.
  • Trap 2: Expressive language = only about emotions → ❌ It also includes attitudes, opinions, and personal perspectives.
  • Trap 3: Directive language = only commands → ❌ It includes requests, advice, instructions, and recommendations.
  • Trap 4: Performative language = just speech acts → ✅ But it requires appropriate conditions (e.g., authority) to be effective.
  • Trap 5: Denotation = emotional meaning → ❌ Denotation is the literal/dictionary meaning; connotation is emotional.
  • Trap 6: Positive terms = good; negative terms = bad → ❌ Positive/negative refers to presence/absence of quality, not moral value.
  • Trap 7: Absolute terms = always relative → ❌ Absolute terms have no degree (e.g., perfect, unique).
  • Trap 8: Collective terms = plural always → ❌ Collective terms can be used as singular (the team is...) or plural (the team are...).

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1 (Uses of Language)

"The Earth revolves around the Sun." This is an example of:

  • A. Expressive use
  • B. Informative use
  • C. Directive use
  • D. Performative use
Answer: B. Informative use

Explanation: It conveys a factual statement about the world — informative use.

Question 2 (Uses of Language)

"Please open the window." This is an example of:

  • A. Informative
  • B. Expressive
  • C. Directive
  • D. Ceremonial
Answer: C. Directive

Explanation: It is a request/command directing someone to perform an action.

Question 3 (Denotation & Connotation)

The word "dog" denotes:

  • A. A four-legged carnivorous animal
  • B. Loyalty and friendship
  • C. Aggression and threat
  • D. A feeling of love
Answer: A. A four-legged carnivorous animal

Explanation: Denotation is the literal dictionary meaning of a word.

Question 4 (Positive & Negative Terms)

Which of the following is a negative term?

  • A. Honest
  • B. Brave
  • C. Dishonest
  • D. Kind
Answer: C. Dishonest

Explanation: "Dishonest" indicates the absence of honesty — negative term (logically).

Question 5 (Absolute & Relative Terms)

Which of the following is an absolute term?

  • A. Tall
  • B. Perfect
  • C. Heavy
  • D. Fast
Answer: B. Perfect

Explanation: "Perfect" has no degree — something is either perfect or not. The others are comparative/relative.

Question 6 (Concrete & Abstract Terms)

Which of the following is an abstract term?

  • A. Table
  • B. Justice
  • C. Car
  • D. Tree
Answer: B. Justice

Explanation: "Justice" is a concept — abstract term. Others are concrete, physical objects.

Question 7 (Collective Terms)

Which of the following is a collective term?

  • A. Student
  • B. Class
  • C. Teacher
  • D. Book
Answer: B. Class

Explanation: "Class" refers to a group of students as a single unit — collective term.

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Language Uses = Informative + Expressive + Directive + Performative/Ceremonial

Uses of Language:

  • Informative: Facts, information (True/False)
  • Expressive: Feelings, emotions (Subjective)
  • Directive: Commands, requests (Action-oriented)
  • Performative/Ceremonial: Action/ritual (Saying = Doing)

Terms:

  • Denotation: Literal meaning
  • Connotation: Emotional/associated meaning
  • Positive/Negative: Presence vs absence of quality
  • Absolute/Relative: No degree vs comparative
  • Concrete/Abstract: Physical/tangible vs conceptual/intangible
  • Collective: Group as a single unit

Exam Formula:

Identify Use → Classify Terms → Apply in Context

UGC NET में Uses of Language & Terms के questions identification, classification, और application पर based होते हैं।
