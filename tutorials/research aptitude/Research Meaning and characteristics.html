Research का अर्थ Meaning of Research

Research किसी question, problem या phenomenon की systematic, objective, logical और evidence-based investigation है, जिसका उद्देश्य new knowledge विकसित करना, existing knowledge verify करना या किसी problem का credible solution प्राप्त करना है।

Research = Re-search नहीं, बल्कि Systematic Search for Meaning and Evidence

Research में researcher:

  • Problem identify करता है।
  • Relevant literature पढ़ता है।
  • Research questions या hypothesis बनाता है।
  • Appropriate method चुनता है।
  • Data collect करता है।
  • Data analyse और interpret करता है।
  • Evidence-based conclusion प्रस्तुत करता है।

Exam-Oriented Definition

Research को केवल "बार-बार search करना" या "hypothesis को prove करना" नहीं कहा जा सकता।

DefinitionResearch is a systematic and critical investigation undertaken to answer a question, understand a phenomenon, verify knowledge or provide a credible solution to a problem.

सरल उदाहरण

ExampleGS Net Academy यह जानना चाहती है कि "क्या weekly active-recall quizzes से UGC NET learners की retention improve होती है?" इसके लिए दो learner groups बनाए गए — एक को weekly quizzes, दूसरे को conventional revision कराया गया, और pre-test/post-test scores compare किए गए। यह systematic research है।

3

Objectives of Research शोध के उद्देश्य

Exploration — अन्वेषण

कम understood problem के बारे में preliminary understanding विकसित करना।

ExampleAdult learners MOOCs बीच में क्यों छोड़ते हैं?

Description — वर्णन

किसी population, event या phenomenon की characteristics describe करना।

ExampleUGC NET aspirants के study habits का survey।

Explanation — व्याख्या

Variables के बीच relationship या कारण समझना।

ExampleMotivation और mock-test performance के बीच relationship।

Prediction — पूर्वानुमान

Available evidence के आधार पर future outcome का अनुमान लगाना।

ExampleStudy hours और previous scores से examination success predict करना।

Control

Experimental situations में relevant variables control करके causal relationship examine करना।

Theory Building

Concepts और relationships को integrate करके theoretical explanation विकसित करना।

Problem-Solving

Practical या immediate problem का solution विकसित करना।

4

Characteristics of Good Research अच्छे शोध की विशेषताएँ

Systematic — व्यवस्थित

Research predefined logical process follow करती है।

Problem → Literature → Questions/Hypothesis → Method → Data → Analysis → Conclusion

Logical — तार्किक

Reasoning clear और internally consistent होनी चाहिए। Research में दोनों reasoning processes उपयोगी हैं:

  • Deductive Reasoning: Theory → Hypothesis → Data → Testing
  • Inductive Reasoning: Observations → Patterns → Concepts → Theory

Empirical — अनुभवजन्य

Research observable evidence या systematically collected data पर आधारित होती है। Empirical data हो सकता है: Numerical scores, Interviews, Observations, Documents, Behaviour, Images, Field notes।

Exam TrapEmpirical का अर्थ केवल quantitative data नहीं है। Qualitative observation भी empirical evidence हो सकती है।

Objective — वस्तुनिष्ठ

Researcher personal preference और bias का प्रभाव कम करने का प्रयास करता है। Objectivity improve करने के methods: Clear operational definitions, Standardised tools, Transparent procedures, Multiple researchers, Blind assessment, Triangulation, Reflexivity।

Verifiable — सत्यापन योग्य

दूसरे scholars research procedure और evidence examine कर सकें।

Replicable — पुनरावृत्ति योग्य

Method sufficiently clear हो ताकि study को comparable conditions में repeat किया जा सके।

Exam TrapReplication का अर्थ हर बार identical result आना आवश्यक नहीं है। Context, sample और random variation के कारण differences सम्भव हैं।

Controlled — नियंत्रित

विशेषकर experimental research में extraneous variables के प्रभाव को कम किया जाता है।

Critical

Researcher assumptions, evidence, limitations और alternative explanations को critically examine करता है।

Ethical

Good research में आवश्यक हैं: Informed consent, Voluntary participation, Privacy, Confidentiality, No unnecessary harm, Honest reporting, Proper acknowledgement, Right to withdraw।

Cumulative

New research existing knowledge पर build करती है।

Research starts from existing knowledge and contributes back to knowledge.

Generalisable or Transferable

Quantitative research context-free generalisation का प्रयास कर सकती है। Qualitative research अक्सर context-specific understanding और transferability पर emphasis करती है।

Parsimonious

Explanation unnecessarily complex नहीं होनी चाहिए।

Parsimony = Adequate Explanation with Minimum Unnecessary Complexity
5

What Research Is Not शोध क्या नहीं है?

Research केवल यह नहीं है:

  • Information collect करना
  • Internet search करना
  • पहले से निश्चित belief को prove करना
  • बिना method के opinion देना
  • Sample select करना
  • Data का mechanical presentation
  • दूसरे की material copy करना
  • केवल hypothesis को सही सिद्ध करना
Important PrincipleResearcher का उद्देश्य hypothesis को "prove" करना नहीं, बल्कि evidence के आधार पर उसे test करना है। Result hypothesis को Support, Not support, Modify या Reject कर सकता है।
6

Types of Research शोध के प्रकार

Research को एक ही आधार पर classify नहीं किया जाता। एक study simultaneously Applied, Quantitative, Experimental, Longitudinal हो सकती है। इसलिए पहले classification का basis पहचानें।

Basic / Fundamental / Pure Research — मौलिक शोध

Basic research का उद्देश्य knowledge, concepts, principles या theory को expand करना है। Immediate practical application आवश्यक नहीं है।

Basic Research = Knowledge and Theory Building

Characteristics: Curiosity-driven, Theory-oriented, General principles, Long-term contribution, Immediate field solution आवश्यक नहीं।

ExampleHuman memory में retrieval cues किस प्रकार काम करते हैं?

Exam Keywords: Knowledge for knowledge's sake, Theory building, Basic principles, Generalisation, No immediate application।

Applied Research — अनुप्रयुक्त शोध

Applied research existing knowledge या theory का use करके specific practical problem solve करती है।

Applied Research = Theory Applied to Practical Problem
Exampleकौन-सी spaced-revision strategy UGC NET aspirants की retention सबसे अधिक improve करती है?

Characteristics: Practical problem, Field application, Existing theory का use, Larger applicability की सम्भावना, Solution-oriented।

Basic ResearchApplied Research
Theory developmentPractical application
General knowledgeSpecific problem-solving
Immediate use आवश्यक नहींDirect field relevance
"Why does it happen?""How can it be used?"
Foundation तैयार करती हैFoundation का application करती है
Exam TrapApplied research non-scientific नहीं है। यह भी systematic और empirical हो सकती है।

Action Research — क्रियात्मक शोध

Action Research practitioner द्वारा अपने immediate local problem को solve करने और practice improve करने के लिए की जाती है।

Action Research = Local Problem + Practitioner + Immediate Improvement + Cyclical Reflection

Associated Thinkers: Kurt Lewin — Action Research concept और cyclical approach; Stephen M. Corey — Education में Action Research का प्रमुख association।

Plan → Act → Observe → Reflect → Re-plan
Exampleएक teacher देखता है कि students Research Aptitude में Positivism और Post-positivism confuse करते हैं। वह problem analyse करता है, comparison chart introduce करता है, classroom intervention लागू करता है, test results observe करता है, और strategy पर reflection करके सुधार करता है। यह Action Research है।

Characteristics: Small/local sample, Immediate problem, Practitioner as researcher, Improvement of practice, Flexible and cyclical, Context-specific, Collaborative हो सकती है, Universal generalisation primary objective नहीं।

Teacher improving own classroom practice → Action Research

Applied vs Action Research

Applied ResearchAction Research
Practical problem solve करती हैImmediate local practice improve करती है
Research specialist कर सकता हैPractitioner स्वयं करता है
Larger sample सम्भवUsually small/local group
Broader generalisation सम्भवContext-specific findings

Exam Trapहर practical research Action Research नहीं होती। यदि teacher अपने classroom की immediate problem पर iterative intervention करता है, तो Action Research होगी।

Evaluation Research — मूल्यांकन शोध

Evaluation Research किसी programme, policy, practice या intervention की Merit, Worth, Effectiveness, Efficiency और Impact का systematic assessment करती है।

Evaluation Research = Judging the Worth of a Programme or Practice
ExampleGovernment-sponsored online mentorship programme ने rural learners की NET qualification rate कितना improve किया?

Types: Formative Evaluation (programme के दौरान improvement के लिए), Summative Evaluation (programme complete होने के बाद overall worth/impact judge करने के लिए)।

Exam Keywords: Programme evaluation, Merit and worth, Effectiveness, Impact, Cost-effectiveness, Goal attainment।

Exploratory Research — अन्वेषणात्मक शोध

जब problem के बारे में knowledge limited हो, exploratory research preliminary understanding विकसित करती है।

Methods: Literature review, Expert interview, Focus group, Case study, Pilot study।

ExampleFirst-generation learners AI-based education को किस प्रकार अनुभव करते हैं?
Exploratory = What is happening? What may be important?

Descriptive Research — वर्णनात्मक शोध

Descriptive research existing phenomenon की characteristics को systematically describe करती है।

ExampleUGC NET learners द्वारा प्रतिदिन उपयोग किए जाने वाले digital learning resources का survey।

Characteristics: "What exists?" पर emphasis, Manipulation आवश्यक नहीं, Survey और observation common, Frequency, percentage और trends।

Exam TrapDescriptive research cause-and-effect automatically establish नहीं करती।

Analytical Research — विश्लेषणात्मक शोध

Available facts, data या documents का critical analysis करके relationships और explanations विकसित करना।

Exampleपिछले दस वर्षों के UGC NET results का subject-wise trend analysis।

Explanatory Research — व्याख्यात्मक शोध

Variables के बीच relationship या कारण को explain करने का प्रयास।

Exampleक्यों regular mock-test analysis JRF performance improve करता है?

Quantitative Research — मात्रात्मक शोध

Quantitative Research numerical measurement, structured data और statistical analysis पर आधारित होती है।

Quantitative Research = Numbers + Measurement + Hypothesis Testing + Statistics

Characteristics: Objective measurement, Predefined variables, Structured tools, Larger samples, Statistical analysis, Deductive orientation, Hypothesis testing, Generalisation का प्रयास, Fixed/structured design।

Data-Collection Tools: Structured questionnaire, Achievement test, Scale, Experiment, Structured observation, Secondary numerical data।

Example500 learners के mock-test scores और final NET scores के correlation का study।

Qualitative Research — गुणात्मक शोध

Qualitative Research meanings, experiences, perceptions, processes और social context की detailed understanding विकसित करती है।

Qualitative Research = Meaning + Experience + Context + Words

Characteristics: Natural setting, Researcher as key instrument, Words/narratives/images, Small purposive samples, Flexible/emergent design, Inductive analysis, Context-specific understanding, Participant perspectives, Detailed/thick description, Reflexivity।

Methods: Interview, Focus group, Participant observation, Case study, Ethnography, Phenomenology, Narrative inquiry, Grounded theory।

ExampleJRF-qualified learners के preparation experiences की in-depth interviews द्वारा study।

Quantitative vs Qualitative Research

BasisQuantitativeQualitative
DataNumbersWords, narratives, images
PurposeMeasure, test, predictUnderstand meaning and experience
LogicMostly deductiveMostly inductive
DesignStructured/fixedFlexible/emergent
SampleUsually largerUsually smaller/purposive
SettingControlled हो सकती हैNatural setting
ToolInstrument is centralResearcher is key instrument
AnalysisStatisticalThematic/interpretive
OutputGeneral patternsContext-rich understanding
Quality termsValidity, reliabilityCredibility, dependability, transferability
Exam TrapQualitative Research केवल subjective opinion नहीं है। Quantitative Research automatically completely objective नहीं होती। दोनों systematic और empirical हो सकती हैं।

Mixed-Methods Research — मिश्रित शोध

Mixed Methods quantitative और qualitative approaches को purposeful manner में integrate करती है।

Mixed Methods = Numerical Breadth + Qualitative Depth
Exampleपहले 1,000 learners का motivation survey करना और फिर selected learners के interviews लेकर results का deeper explanation प्राप्त करना।
Exam Trapकेवल दो अलग methods use करना पर्याप्त नहीं; meaningful integration आवश्यक है।

Experimental Research — प्रयोगात्मक शोध

Experimental Research cause-and-effect relationship examine करती है।

Essential Elements: Independent variable manipulation, Dependent variable measurement, Control group, Experimental group, Random assignment जहाँ possible, Extraneous-variable control।

Exampleनई teaching strategy का test-score पर effect examine करना।
Manipulation + Control + Randomisation = True Experiment

Quasi-Experimental Research

Quasi-experimental study में intervention या comparison होता है, लेकिन random assignment या complete control absent हो सकता है।

Exampleदो existing university classes में different teaching methods compare करना।
Exam TrapQuasi-experimental का अर्थ weak या non-scientific research नहीं है; causal inference true experiment की तुलना में अधिक cautious होगी।

Ex-post Facto Research — कार्योत्तर शोध

Ex-post facto research में independent variable पहले ही occur हो चुकी होती है; researcher उसे manipulate नहीं करता।

Ex-post Facto = Effect Already Occurred → Probable Cause Traced
Exampleयह examine करना कि अलग school backgrounds से आने वाले learners की research aptitude में difference है या नहीं।
ExperimentalEx-post Facto
Independent variable manipulatedNo manipulation
Researcher control अधिकControl limited
Prospective design हो सकता हैEvent already occurred
Stronger causal inferenceCausation cautiously inferred

Survey Research

Survey population के attitudes, opinions, behaviour या characteristics से संबंधित data collect करती है।

Cross-Sectional Survey: एक point in time पर data। Longitudinal Survey: Time के different points पर repeated data।

Conceptual and Empirical Research

Conceptual Research: Concepts, theories और abstract ideas पर आधारित। उदाहरण: Academic freedom की theoretical conceptualisation।

Empirical Research: Observation या systematically collected evidence पर आधारित। उदाहरण: University teachers की perceived academic freedom का survey।

Historical Research

Past events, documents और records का systematic study।

Primary Sources: Original letters, Diaries, Official records, Speeches, Contemporary documents। Secondary Sources: Later books, Review articles, Interpretations।

7

Research Paradigm का अर्थ

Research Paradigm reality, knowledge और research method के बारे में basic philosophical assumptions का framework है।

Three Core Questions:

  • Ontology: Reality की nature क्या है?
  • Epistemology: Knowledge कैसे प्राप्त की जा सकती है?
  • Methodology: Reality और knowledge का अध्ययन किस method से किया जाए?
Paradigm = View of Reality + View of Knowledge + Research Approach
8

Positivism प्रत्यक्षवाद

Positivism का प्रमुख association Auguste Comte से है।

Auguste Comte → Positivism

Positivism मानता है कि reliable knowledge observable, measurable और empirically verifiable evidence पर आधारित होनी चाहिए।

Positivism = Objective Reality + Observation + Measurement + Hypothesis Testing + Generalisation

Assumptions:

  • Ontology: एक objective reality researcher से independent exist करती है।
  • Epistemology: Reality को observation और measurement द्वारा जाना जा सकता है।
  • Researcher's Role: Researcher को detached, neutral और value-free रहने का प्रयास करना चाहिए।

Methodology: Quantitative methods, Experiment, Survey, Measurement, Statistical analysis, Hypothesis testing, Deductive reasoning।

Theory → Hypothesis → Operationalisation → Data Collection → Statistical Test → Confirmation/Rejection
ExampleWeekly practice-test frequency और NET score के relationship को 2,000 learners के data से statistically test करना।

Key Terms: Objectivity, Measurement, Quantification, Verification, Prediction, Control, General laws, Cause and effect, Value neutrality।

Limitations of Positivism

  • Human experience को numbers तक reduce करने का risk
  • Context और meaning की उपेक्षा
  • Complete value neutrality difficult
  • Researcher और instrument influence सम्भव
  • Social reality complex और changing हो सकती है
  • Observation theory-free नहीं होती
  • A single method पूरी reality capture नहीं कर सकता
9

Post-Positivism उत्तर-प्रत्यक्षवाद

Post-positivism Positivism को पूरी तरह reject नहीं करती; वह उसकी limitations स्वीकार करके उसे revise करती है।

Post-Positivism = Reality Exists, but Our Knowledge of It Is Partial and Fallible

Main Association: Critical Realism, Karl Popper — Falsification और conjectures/refutations, Fallibilism, Theory revision, Triangulation, Probabilistic conclusions।

Assumptions:

  • Ontology: Reality exist करती है, लेकिन उसे perfectly apprehend करना कठिन है।
  • Epistemology: Observation researcher की theories, assumptions और limitations से प्रभावित हो सकती है।
  • Researcher's Role: Complete objectivity सम्भव नहीं, लेकिन rigorous methods से bias कम किया जा सकता है।

Methodology: Hypothesis testing, Critical testing, Falsification, Multiple methods, Triangulation, Quantitative तथा qualitative evidence, Probabilistic conclusions, Replication और peer criticism।

Theory → Tentative Hypothesis → Critical Test → Evidence → Revision → Further Testing

Falsification

Karl Popper के अनुसार scientific theory को केवल confirming evidence से final truth नहीं माना जा सकता। Theory ऐसी होनी चाहिए जिसे evidence द्वारा गलत सिद्ध करने की possibility हो।

Scientific Claim = Testable + Falsifiable
ExampleClaim: "Weekly quizzes सभी learners के scores improve करती हैं।" "सभी" वाला claim evidence द्वारा false सिद्ध किया जा सकता है। इसलिए testable/falsifiable है।
VerificationFalsification
Supporting evidence खोजनाDisconfirming evidence खोजने का प्रयास
Positivist tendencyPopper/post-positivist association
ConfirmationCritical testing
Final certainty का riskTentative knowledge

Triangulation

एक phenomenon को multiple methods, sources, researchers या theories से study करना।

Types: Data triangulation, Method triangulation, Investigator triangulation, Theory triangulation।

ExampleOnline-learning engagement को LMS data, Survey, Interviews और Classroom observation से study करना।
Triangulation does not guarantee truth; it strengthens credibility through multiple perspectives.

इन दोनों को identical नहीं समझना चाहिए।

Important Exam Traps

  • Research का उद्देश्य hypothesis को हर condition में prove करना नहीं है।
  • Basic Research theory; Applied Research practical application से जुड़ी है।
  • Action Research local, immediate और cyclical होती है।
  • Evaluation Research programme की worth/effectiveness judge करती है।
  • Descriptive Research cause establish नहीं करती।
  • Ex-post Facto में independent variable manipulate नहीं होती।
  • Qualitative data भी empirical हो सकती है।
  • Quantitative Research automatically bias-free नहीं होती।
  • Mixed Methods केवल data के दो forms नहीं, उनका integration है।
  • Positivism objective measurement और quantification पर emphasis देती है।
  • Post-positivism reality को reject नहीं करती; perfect knowledge के claim को reject करती है।
  • Popper verification की तुलना में falsification पर emphasis देते हैं।
  • Complete objectivity post-positivism में ideal है, guaranteed achievement नहीं।
  • Ethnography प्रायः interpretivist tradition से जुड़ी है, लेकिन पुराने NET questions में broad post-positivist label के अंतर्गत पूछी गई है।
  • Action Research findings की universal generalisation primary objective नहीं है।

PYQ 1: UGC NET, 18 December 2018, Shift 1

Question: Which of the following statements offer a suitable definition of research?

1. Research means a repeated search. 2. Research is basically an answer to a question. 3. Research provides an authentic solution to a problem. 4. Research is an endeavour to prove one's hypothesis. 5. Research is a meaning-giving process. 6. Research means drawing a sample from a defined population.

A. (1),(2),(3) only   B. (2),(3),(6) only   C. (1),(4),(6) only   D. (2),(3),(5) only

Answer: D. (2), (3) and (5) only

Research questions के answers खोजती है, problems के credible solutions देती है, और data/evidence को meaning देती है। Hypothesis is tested, not compulsorily proved.

PYQ 2: UGC NET, 25 June 2019, Shift 2

Question: Which research type has the potential for enhancing teaching competencies?

A. Fundamental Research   B. Applied Research   C. Impact Assessment Research   D. Action Research

Answer: D. Action Research

Teacher अपने classroom की immediate problem identify करके intervention करता, result observe करता और practice improve करता है।

PYQ 3: UGC NET, 3 December 2019, Shift 1

Question: In the post-positivistic approach to research, which research types were emphasised?

1. Experimental Research 2. Phenomenological Research 3. Ethnographic Research 4. Ex-post Facto Research 5. Action Research

A. (1),(2),(3) only   B. (2),(3),(4) only   C. (2),(3),(5) only   D. (3),(4),(5) only

Answer: C. (2), (3) and (5) only

Official exam convention में Phenomenology, Ethnography और Action Research को post-positivistic approach से associated माना गया।

PYQ 4: UGC NET, 24 September 2020, Shift 1

Question: Which research procedure will figure under the post-positivistic approach?

A. Normative Survey   B. Experimental Study   C. Ethnographic Study   D. Ex-post Facto Study

Answer: C. Ethnographic Study

Ethnographic study natural setting, culture और participants के perspectives की contextual understanding विकसित करती है।

PYQ 5: UGC NET, 9 October 2020, Shift 1

Question: Which research form primarily aims at putting theoretical advances to use while exploring their potential for generalisability?

A. Fundamental Research   B. Evaluative Research   C. Action Research   D. Applied Research

Answer: D. Applied Research

Applied Research existing theory या fundamental knowledge को real-world problem पर apply करती है, broader applicability के साथ।

PYQ 6: UGC NET, 1 October 2022, Shift 1

Question: Which of the following are included in the Positivist paradigm of research?

1. Quantification 2. Construction of hypotheses 3. Use of objective measures 4. Subjective analysis 5. Rejection of theories

A. (1),(2),(3) only   B. (2),(3),(4) only   C. (3),(4),(5) only   D. (1),(4),(5) only

Answer: A. (1), (2) and (3) only

Positivism quantification, hypothesis construction/testing और objective measurement पर emphasis देता है। Subjective analysis इसकी central feature नहीं है।

December 2026 Expected MCQs

निम्न questions prediction-based practice हैं, actual PYQs नहीं।

Question 1

A researcher develops a theory explaining how retrieval practice affects long-term memory, without seeking an immediate classroom application. This is:

A. Applied Research   B. Basic Research   C. Action Research   D. Evaluation Research

Answer: B. Basic Research

Primary purpose theory और knowledge expansion है; immediate practical solution नहीं।

Question 2

A teacher introduces weekly quizzes, observes results and modifies the strategy to improve the performance of the same class. This is:

A. Historical Research   B. Fundamental Research   C. Action Research   D. Normative Survey

Answer: C. Action Research

Practitioner अपने local problem पर Plan–Act–Observe–Reflect cycle follow कर रहा है।

Question 3

A study assesses whether a government mentorship programme achieved its objectives and provided value for the resources spent. This is:

A. Basic Research   B. Evaluation Research   C. Phenomenological Research   D. Historical Research

Answer: B. Evaluation Research

Programme की effectiveness, merit, worth और resource use judge किया जा रहा है।

Question 4

Which research type studies an effect after the presumed causal variable has already occurred?

A. True Experimental Research   B. Ex-post Facto Research   C. Action Research   D. Ethnographic Research

Answer: B. Ex-post Facto Research

Independent variable already occur हो चुकी होती है; researcher उसे manipulate नहीं करता।

Question 5

Which combination is most characteristic of Positivism?

A. Lived experience, subjectivity and multiple meanings   B. Measurement, objectivity and hypothesis testing   C. Personal narrative, reflexivity and cultural immersion   D. Complete rejection of empirical evidence

Answer: B. Measurement, objectivity and hypothesis testing
Question 6

Post-positivism primarily maintains that:

A. Reality does not exist outside language.   B. Reality exists and can be known with complete certainty.   C. Reality exists, but knowledge of it remains partial and fallible.   D. Scientific testing has no role in research.

Answer: C. Reality exists, but knowledge of it remains partial and fallible.

Post-positivism Critical Realism और fallibilism से जुड़ी है।

Question 7

Which thinker is most closely associated with falsification?

A. Auguste Comte   B. Karl Popper   C. Kurt Lewin   D. Stephen Corey

Answer: B. Karl Popper

Popper के अनुसार scientific propositions को critical tests के माध्यम से falsify करने योग्य होना चाहिए।

Question 8

Which sequence correctly represents Action Research?

A. Theory → Verification → Universal Law → Publication   B. Plan → Act → Observe → Reflect   C. Sample → Patent → Profit → Theory   D. Observe → Punish → Control → Certify

Answer: B. Plan → Act → Observe → Reflect
Question 9

A researcher conducts interviews to understand the lived experiences of first-generation university learners. This is primarily:

A. Quantitative Experimental Research   B. Qualitative Phenomenological Research   C. Normative Survey   D. Ex-post Facto Research

Answer: B. Qualitative Phenomenological Research

Phenomenology किसी phenomenon के lived experience का meaning समझती है।

Question 10

Which statement is incorrect?

A. Quantitative research may use statistical analysis.   B. Qualitative research may use natural settings.   C. Empirical evidence is necessarily numerical.   D. Mixed Methods integrates quantitative and qualitative evidence.

Answer: C. Empirical evidence is necessarily numerical.

Interviews, observations और documents से प्राप्त qualitative evidence भी empirical हो सकती है।

Question 11 (Assertion–Reason)

Assertion: Post-positivists use triangulation to strengthen research conclusions. Reason: A single method may provide only a partial view of complex reality.

A. Both true, Reason correctly explains Assertion   B. Both true, Reason does not explain Assertion   C. Assertion true, Reason false   D. Assertion false, Reason true

Answer: A. Both true, and Reason correctly explains Assertion
Question 12

A researcher uses theory to formulate a hypothesis and then collects data to test it. This reasoning is primarily:

A. Inductive   B. Deductive   C. Intuitive   D. Circular

Answer: B. Deductive Reasoning

Theory → Hypothesis → Data is a deductive sequence.

Research = Systematic + Logical + Empirical + Ethical Inquiry
Basic = Theory and Knowledge  |  Applied = Practical Application

Qualitative = Meaning + Context + Induction
Mixed Methods = Breadth + Depth + Integration

Educational Action Research = Stephen Corey

Final Identification Formula

किसी research question में पहले पूछें:

  • Theory बनानी है? → Basic Research
  • Practical problem solve करना है? → Applied Research
  • Practitioner अपनी local practice सुधार रहा है? → Action Research
  • Programme की worth judge करनी है? → Evaluation Research
  • Variable manipulate हुई है? → Experimental Research
  • Event पहले ही हो चुका है? → Ex-post Facto Research
  • Numbers और statistics हैं? → Quantitative Research
  • Meaning और lived experience है? → Qualitative Research
