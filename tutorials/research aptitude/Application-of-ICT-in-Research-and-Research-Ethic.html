ICT का अर्थ

ICT — Information and Communication Technology से आशय उन digital technologies से है जिनका उपयोग information को: Collect, Store, Process, Analyse, Retrieve, Communicate, Disseminate करने के लिए किया जाता है।

ICT in Research = Search + Collect + Store + Analyse + Communicate + Disseminate

ICT किसी एक research stage तक सीमित नहीं है। इसका उपयोग research problem की पहचान से लेकर findings के publication और preservation तक किया जा सकता है।

3

Research Problem की पहचान में ICT

  • Emerging research topics खोज सकता है
  • Government reports और policy documents देख सकता है
  • Research gaps पहचान सकता है
  • नवीनतम journal articles access कर सकता है
  • International research trends का analysis कर सकता है
  • Relevant experts और research groups खोज सकता है

Example: Researcher "Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education" पर काम करना चाहता है। वह academic databases में recent studies search करके यह पहचानता है कि rural learners पर empirical research सीमित है — यह research gap identification में ICT का उपयोग है।

  • Digital Sources: Google Scholar, Scopus, Web of Science, ERIC, PubMed, JSTOR, Shodhganga, INFLIBNET, Institutional repositories, E-journals, E-books, Government databases

5

Digital Libraries and Repositories

  • Digital Library: Organised digital collection of e-books, articles, theses, reports, multimedia
  • Institutional Repository: Digital archive of institution's scholarly output
  • Shodhganga: Indian universities' theses and dissertations digital repository (maintained by INFLIBNET Centre)
  • Shodhgangotri: Approved research synopses/research-in-progress repository

Exam Trap: Repository में उपलब्ध thesis को बिना citation उपयोग करना plagiarism हो सकता है। Open access का अर्थ copyright-free या attribution-free नहीं होता।

6

Online Data Collection

  • Google Forms/Microsoft Forms, Online questionnaires, Email surveys, Mobile applications, Online interviews, Video conferencing, Digital focus groups, Online experiments, Social-media data, Sensors and wearable devices, LMS logs

Advantages: Wider coverage, automatic data entry, cost saving, real-time responses, remote participants, skip logic, multimedia questions

Limitations: Digital divide, Internet access inequality, self-selection bias, representativeness issues, multiple/false responses, low response rate, privacy/data-security risk, identity verification difficult, informed consent neglect

Exam Point: Online survey का बड़ा sample अपने-आप representative sample नहीं होता।

7

Data Storage and Management

  • Means: Spreadsheets, Relational databases, Encrypted drives, Institutional servers, Cloud storage, Data repositories
  • Good Practices: Password protection, Encryption, Regular backup, Version control, File-naming convention, Access control, Data dictionary, De-identification, Retention & deletion policy
Original Data + Secure Backup + Controlled Access

Exam Trap: Cloud पर data upload करना ही security की guarantee नहीं है। Encryption, permissions और applicable policy आवश्यक हैं।

Quantitative Data Analysis में ICT

  • Tools: Microsoft Excel, SPSS, R, Python, SAS, Stata, Jamovi, PSPP
  • Applications: Data cleaning, Coding, Frequency/percentage, Mean/median/mode, Standard deviation, Correlation, t-test, ANOVA, Chi-square, Regression, Graphs/visualisation

Important Caution: Software statistical test कर सकता है, लेकिन appropriate test चुनने और result interpret करने की responsibility researcher की होती है।

Garbage In → Garbage Out

Qualitative Data Analysis में ICT

Exam Point: Qualitative software researcher की interpretation को replace नहीं करता; वह data organisation और analysis को support करता है।

Research Ethics का अर्थ

Research Ethics वे moral principles, professional standards और guidelines हैं जो research की planning, conduct, data handling, reporting और publication को regulate करते हैं।

Research Ethics = Honesty + Objectivity + Respect + Responsibility + Transparency
  • Participants के rights और welfare की रक्षा
  • Research findings की credibility बनाए रखना
  • Academic integrity सुनिश्चित करना
  • Society और scientific community का trust बनाए रखना
16

Core Principles of Research Ethics

  • Respect for Persons/Autonomy: Participant का voluntary decision right
  • Beneficence: Possible benefits maximised
  • Non-maleficence: Do no harm — physical, psychological, social, economic, reputational harm minimised
  • Justice: Fair distribution of burden and benefit
  • Honesty: Truthful reporting of data, method, findings
  • Objectivity: Control/disclose personal bias and conflict of interest
  • Integrity: Consistency and ethical commitment
  • Accountability: Responsible for decisions, data, reporting
  • Transparency: Clear method, limitations, funding, conflict of interest
  • Confidentiality and Privacy: Protect participant information
17

Informed Consent

Informed Consent = Information + Understanding + Voluntariness + Agreement
  • To be told: Purpose, Procedures, Duration, Risks, Benefits, Confidentiality, Voluntary participation, Withdrawal right, Contact details, Data use
  • Invalid when: Incomplete information, Coercion/undue pressure, Language not understood, Participant not legally capable, Only formal signature without real understanding
  • Vulnerable Participants: Children, Limited decision-making capacity, Prisoners, Economically dependent, Researcher's own students/employees, Seriously ill — additional safeguards needed
18

Privacy, Anonymity and Confidentiality

ConceptMeaning
PrivacyPersonal information पर participant का control
AnonymityIdentity linked/known नहीं
ConfidentialityIdentity ज्ञात, पर protected
De-identificationIdentifiers को remove या code करना

Exam Trap: Anonymity और confidentiality समान नहीं हैं। यदि researcher names जानता है लेकिन उन्हें disclose नहीं करता, तो यह confidentiality है, complete anonymity नहीं।

19

Research Misconduct: FFP

FFP = Fabrication + Falsification + Plagiarism
  • Fabrication: Making up data that was not actually collected. Example: Reporting 200 responses when only 120 collected, creating remaining responses.
  • Falsification: Manipulating, altering or omitting existing data/process. Example: Deleting inconvenient observations to support hypothesis.
  • Plagiarism: Using others' words, ideas, data or work without proper credit.
20

Plagiarism के प्रकार

  • Direct plagiarism
  • Mosaic/patchwork plagiarism
  • Inadequate paraphrasing
  • Idea plagiarism
  • Data/image plagiarism
  • Source plagiarism
  • Accidental plagiarism
  • Self-plagiarism — अपने previously published text/data को बिना disclosure/citation नए work के रूप में प्रस्तुत करना

Prevention: Proper citation, Quotation marks, Genuine paraphrasing with citation, Reference list, Original notes, Figures/tables source, Similarity report careful interpretation

Exam Trap: Similarity और plagiarism समान नहीं हैं। Similarity software matching text दिखाता है; Plagiarism ethical judgement है — context, attribution और intent पर depend करता है। Zero similarity academic quality की guarantee नहीं है।

21

Authorship Ethics

किसी व्यक्ति को author तभी बनाया जाना चाहिए जब उसका research में substantial intellectual contribution हो।

  • Legitimate Contribution: Conception/design, Data analysis/interpretation, Drafting/critical revision, Final approval, Accountability
  • Unethical Authorship: Gift/Honorary (influential person without contribution), Ghost (contributor removed), Guest (prestige addition), Order based on power/position not contribution

Exam Point: केवल departmental head या supervisor होना automatic authorship का पर्याप्त आधार नहीं है।

22

Publication Ethics

  • Duplicate Publication: Same research substantially same form in multiple journals
  • Simultaneous Submission: Same manuscript to multiple journals at once
  • Salami Slicing: Unnecessarily dividing one integrated study into small papers
  • Selective Reporting: Only favourable/significant results reported
  • Citation Manipulation: Adding irrelevant citations to increase count
  • Conflict of Interest: Personal/professional/financial interest affecting research judgement — must be disclosed and managed, not hidden
23

Ethical Review Committee

  • Human participants studies generally require appropriate ethics committee/IEC/IRB approval
  • Committee checks: Risk–benefit balance, Informed consent process, Participant selection, Privacy/confidentiality, Vulnerable groups protection, Data management plan, Compensation and withdrawal rights

Exam Trap: Ethics approval प्राप्त करना researcher की continuing ethical responsibility समाप्त नहीं करता।

24

Research में Deception

  • Limited deception may be justified only when: Scientific justification, Minimal risk, No suitable alternative, Ethics approval obtained, Debriefing provided, Participant has data withdrawal opportunity

Deception सामान्य rule नहीं, exceptional procedure है।

25

ICT and Digital Research Ethics

  • Online informed consent
  • Social-media data privacy
  • Data ownership
  • Cybersecurity
  • Cloud storage jurisdiction
  • Facial/biometric data
  • Re-identification risk
  • Algorithmic bias
  • Digital surveillance
  • Copyright/licensing
  • Terms of service
  • Digital divide
  • Automated decision-making

Publicly Available Data Trap: Social media पर data publicly visible होने का अर्थ हमेशा यह नहीं कि उसे बिना ethical consideration research में उपयोग किया जा सकता है। Context, sensitivity, user expectation, consent, anonymisation और potential harm देखना आवश्यक है।

26

Artificial Intelligence in Research

  • AI can assist: Keyword generation, Literature discovery, Language improvement, Coding suggestions, Data visualisation, Transcription, Programming assistance, Preliminary summaries
  • Ethical Risks: False/hallucinated references, Biased output, Confidential data leakage, Undisclosed AI-generated text, Fabricated interpretation, Copyright concerns, Overdependence, Lack of accountability

Right Approach: Verify AI output, Read and cite original sources, Do NOT upload confidential/unpublished participant data to public AI systems, Follow journal/institution AI-disclosure policy, Do NOT treat AI as author, Avoid fabricated citations/data.

AI may assist research; it cannot replace researcher accountability.
