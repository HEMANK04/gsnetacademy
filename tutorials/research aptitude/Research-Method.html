Meaning of Research Method

Research Method वह व्यवस्थित तरीका है जिसके माध्यम से researcher किसी research problem का अध्ययन करता है, data एकत्र करता है, उसका analysis करता है और conclusion तक पहुँचता है।

Research Method = Research Problem + Data Collection + Analysis + Interpretation

Five major methods:

  • Experimental Research — प्रयोगात्मक अनुसंधान
  • Descriptive Research — वर्णनात्मक अनुसंधान
  • Historical Research — ऐतिहासिक अनुसंधान
  • Qualitative Research — गुणात्मक अनुसंधान
  • Quantitative Research — मात्रात्मक अनुसंधान
2

Experimental Research — प्रयोगात्मक अनुसंधान

Experimental Research = Manipulation + Control + Observation of Effect
  • Researcher manipulates an independent variable to study its effect on a dependent variable
  • Example: Flipped classroom method (IV) → Academic achievement (DV)
VariableMeaning
Independent VariableManipulated by researcher (e.g., teaching method)
Dependent VariableMeasured outcome (e.g., achievement score)
Extraneous VariableOther factor affecting outcome (e.g., prior knowledge)
Control VariableDeliberately kept constant
Confounding VariableChanges with IV and obscures results
  • Key features: Variable manipulation, experimental & control groups, cause–effect relationship, controlled conditions, objective measurement, hypothesis testing, random assignment (where possible), replication
3

Experimental Designs

  • Pre-experimental Design: Minimal control (e.g., one-group pre-test–post-test). Shows improvement but cannot prove it was due to treatment alone.
  • True Experimental Design: Manipulation + Control group + Random assignment
True Experiment = Manipulation + Control + Randomisation
  • Quasi-experimental Design: Manipulation occurs, but random assignment is absent (e.g., intact classroom sections).

Exam Trap: Random selection ≠ Random assignment. Selection is about choosing the sample; assignment is about putting participants into groups.

4

Descriptive Research — वर्णनात्मक अनुसंधान

Descriptive Research asks: "What exists?" or "What is happening?"
  • Describes existing conditions, populations, attitudes, behaviours — without manipulating variables
  • Examples: University students' attitudes toward online learning; teachers' job satisfaction survey; study habits; dropout rate study
  • Key features: Systematic description, natural setting, no manipulation, large samples possible, uses questionnaires/interviews/observation/surveys
  • Types: Survey (cross-sectional/longitudinal), Correlational, Case Study, Developmental Study
Correlation ≠ Causation
5

Historical Research — ऐतिहासिक अनुसंधान

Historical Research = Study of Past through Critical Examination of Sources
  • Examples: Development of higher education in India; National Education Policies comparative analysis; Ancient Indian universities
  • Primary Sources: Original records directly related to the studied period/event (government orders, letters, diaries, speeches, official records, photographs, minutes, manuscripts, eyewitness accounts)
  • Secondary Sources: Interpretations/analyses based on primary sources (textbooks, biographies, review articles, later historical accounts, encyclopaedias)
6

Source Criticism

  • External Criticism — बाह्य आलोचना: Checks authenticity of the source. Is the document genuine? Who wrote it? When? Any later changes?
External Criticism = Is the source genuine?
  • Internal Criticism — आंतरिक आलोचना: Checks accuracy and credibility of the information. Is the author reliable? Biased? An eyewitness?
Internal Criticism = Is the information credible?

Process: Problem selection → Research questions → Source discovery → Primary/secondary data collection → External criticism → Internal criticism → Evidence organisation → Interpretation & report writing

7

Quantitative Research — मात्रात्मक अनुसंधान

Quantitative Research = Numerical Data + Measurement + Statistical Analysis
  • Key features: Numerical data, structured tools, large sample, hypothesis testing, statistical analysis, objectivity, deductive reasoning, generalisation, reliability & validity, replication
Theory → Hypothesis → Data Collection → Statistical Test → Conclusion
  • Data tools: Structured questionnaire, achievement test, rating scale, standardised test, structured observation, experiments
  • Statistical techniques: Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, correlation, t-test, ANOVA, Chi-square, regression
8

Qualitative Research — गुणात्मक अनुसंधान

Qualitative Research = Meaning + Experience + Context + Interpretation
  • Key features: Non-numerical data, natural setting, participant perspectives, flexible/emergent design, small purposive sample, researcher as instrument, inductive reasoning, contextual/holistic understanding, thick description, multiple realities
Observations/Data → Patterns → Categories → Themes → Explanation/Theory
  • Data techniques: In-depth interview, focus-group discussion, participant observation, field notes, documents/diaries, audio-visual material, open-ended questions
  • Sampling: Generally purposive sampling
9

Qualitative Methods

  • Phenomenology: Study of lived experiences (e.g., online examination anxiety experiences)
  • Ethnography: Study of cultural/social group beliefs, practices, behaviour through prolonged field study
  • Grounded Theory: Theory emerges from data — not imposed beforehand
  • Narrative Research: Study of individuals' stories and life experiences
  • Qualitative Case Study: Detailed contextual study of a bounded case (person, institution, programme, event)
Grounded Theory → Theory emerges from data
10

Quantitative vs Qualitative

BasisQuantitativeQualitative
DataNumericalWords, narratives, images
PurposeMeasurement, testing, predictionUnderstanding meaning & experience
ReasoningDeductivePrimarily inductive
DesignPredetermined & structuredFlexible & emergent
SampleLarge, often probability-basedSmall, purposive
SettingControlled or structuredNatural setting
ToolsTests, scales, questionnairesInterviews, observations
AnalysisStatisticalCoding & thematic analysis
Quality focusReliability & validityCredibility & dependability
QUAN = Quantity, Numbers, Measurement, Statistics | QUAL = Quality of Experience, Meaning, Context
11

Mixed-Methods Research

Mixed Methods ≠ merely collecting two types of data | Mixed Methods = Collection + Analysis + Integration
  • Convergent Design: Both types collected simultaneously, findings compared
  • Explanatory Sequential: Quantitative first, then qualitative to explain results
  • Exploratory Sequential: Qualitative exploration first, then quantitative testing
12

How to Identify the Method?

Research QuestionMethod
What is the effect of a treatment?Experimental
What is the current status?Descriptive
Are two variables related?Correlational
What happened in the past?Historical
How much or how many?Quantitative
How do participants experience this?Qualitative
Need both types of understandingMixed methods
13

Important Exam Points

  • Experimental research requires variable manipulation
  • True experiment requires random assignment
  • Quasi-experiment lacks random assignment
  • Descriptive research describes present condition
  • Correlation shows relationship, not causation
  • Historical research uses primary and secondary sources
  • External criticism checks authenticity
  • Internal criticism checks credibility
  • Quantitative = numerical & statistical
  • Qualitative = meaning, experience & context
  • Quantitative is mainly deductive
  • Qualitative is mainly inductive
  • Phenomenology = lived experience
  • Ethnography = culture-sharing group
  • Grounded theory = theory from data
14

Common Exam Traps

  • Trap 1: Descriptive vs Experimental — Descriptive observes; Experimental manipulates
  • Trap 2: Correlation vs Causation — High correlation ≠ cause-effect
  • Trap 3: Primary Source — Not every old document; must be original/direct record
  • Trap 4: External vs Internal — Genuine document = External; Reliable statement = Internal
  • Trap 5: Qualitative is not unscientific — Uses systematic coding, triangulation, member checking
  • Trap 6: Large sample ≠ automatically representative — Appropriate sampling matters
  • Trap 7: Quasi-experiment — Has treatment/manipulation; lacks random assignment
