Author's Purpose — लेखक का उद्देश्य

Author's Purpose वह reason है जिसके लिए author ने text लिखा है। यह बताता है कि author क्या achieve करना चाहता है।

Author's Purpose = Why the author wrote this text

सरल उदाहरण:

  • Text: "Exercise 30 minutes daily for better health."
  • Purpose: To inform/persuade (हर किसी को व्यायाम करना चाहिए)
  • Text: "The best-selling novel of the year."
  • Purpose: To entertain (कहानी सुनाना)
  • Text: "Buy our new product today!"
  • Purpose: To persuade (लोगों को उत्पाद खरीदने के लिए)
2

Types of Author's Purpose — लेखक के उद्देश्य के प्रकार

PurposeMeaningKey Words/SignalsExample
Inform — सूचित करनाFacts, data, information देनाResearch shows, according to, facts, statisticsTextbook, news article
Persuade — प्रेरित/रिझानाReader को कुछ मानने या करने के लिएShould, must, benefits, recommend, believeAdvertisement, editorial
Entertain — मनोरंजन करनाReader का मनोरंजन या भावनात्मक अनुभवStory, humour, imagery, emotional languageNovel, short story, poem
Explain — व्याख्या करनाHow/why किसी concept या process को समझानाBecause, this is why, the reason isInstruction manual, how-to article
Describe — वर्णन करनाकिसी person, place, thing या event का विस्तृत चित्रणDescriptive adjectives, sensory detailsTravelogue, descriptive essay

Exam Point: UGC NET में अक्सर यह पूछा जाता है कि "The author's primary purpose is to..." — आपको passage के overall goal को पहचानना होता है।

3

Author's Tone — लेखक का स्वर

Tone author का attitude या feeling है जो text के माध्यम से व्यक्त होता है। यह words, sentence structure और writing style से पहचाना जाता है।

Tone = Author's Attitude Toward the Subject or Audience

Key Point: Tone को directly stated नहीं किया जाता; इसे word choice, sentence structure और style से infer करना होता है।

Example:

  • "The government has once again failed to address the needs of the people." — Critical tone
  • "The new policy shows great promise for improving education." — Optimistic tone
  • "The situation is extremely concerning and requires immediate attention." — Urgent tone

Tone vs Mood — स्वर vs मनोदशा

BasisToneMood
DefinitionAuthor's attitude toward subjectReader's emotional response
Who feels it?AuthorReader
How to identifyWord choice, sentence structureSetting, imagery, word choice
ExampleAuthor's tone is criticalReader feels sad or angry
Question in Exam"What is the author's tone?""What mood does the passage create?"

Exam Point: UGC NET में अक्सर tone के बारे में पूछा जाता है — आपको author के attitude को पहचानना होता है, न कि reader की emotional response को।

6

Author's Argument — लेखक का तर्क

Argument वह claim या position है जो author किसी issue पर लेता है, जिसे evidence और reasoning के साथ support किया जाता है।

Argument = Claim + Evidence + Reasoning

Example:

  • Claim: "Online education is more effective than traditional classroom learning."
  • Evidence: Studies show higher retention rates; flexible learning allows personalisation; access to diverse resources.
  • Reasoning: Students learn at their own pace; technology enables interactive learning; reduced commute time increases focus.
7

Claims & Evidence — दावा एवं साक्ष्य

  • Claim — दावा: Main argument or position (Thesis statement)
  • Evidence — साक्ष्य: Facts, statistics, examples, expert opinions, case studies, research findings that support the claim
  • Types of Evidence:
    • Facts/Statistics — Data, numbers, research findings
    • Expert Testimony — Quotes from authorities
    • Examples — Real-world cases, illustrations
    • Anecdotes — Personal stories
    • Logical Reasoning — Cause-effect, analogies

Exam Point: UGC NET में यह पूछा जा सकता है कि "Which of the following best supports the author's claim?" — आपको strongest evidence को पहचानना होता है।

8

Reasoning Types — तर्क के प्रकार

  • Deductive Reasoning — निगमनात्मक तर्क: General से specific की ओर। If all A are B and X is A, then X is B.
  • Inductive Reasoning — आगमनात्मक तर्क: Specific से general की ओर। Observing many cases and drawing a general conclusion.
  • Analogical Reasoning — अनुरूपात्मक तर्क: Two things similar in some ways → similar in other ways.
  • Causal Reasoning — कारणात्मक तर्क: X causes Y (or X is the reason for Y).
9

Logical Fallacies — तार्किक भ्रांतियाँ

  • Ad Hominem: Argument को attack करने के बजाय person को attack करना
  • Straw Man: Opponent के argument को misrepresent करके उसे attack करना
  • Appeal to Authority: किसी expert या authority को cite करना जब वह relevant न हो
  • Appeal to Emotion: Logic के बजाय emotions (fear, pity, anger) का उपयोग
  • Hasty Generalisation: बहुत कम evidence के आधार पर generalisation करना
  • False Dilemma: केवल दो options दिखाना जब अधिक options हों
  • Circular Reasoning: Claim को evidence के रूप में उपयोग करना (begging the question)
  • Post Hoc: X happened before Y → X caused Y (false cause)
  • Bandwagon: "Everyone is doing it, so it must be right"
  • Slippery Slope: One action will inevitably lead to a series of negative consequences

Exam Point: UGC NET में fallacy-based questions passage में logical weaknesses को पहचानने से आती हैं।

10

Purpose, Tone & Argument Connection — उद्देश्य, स्वर और तर्क का संबंध

Purpose + Tone + Argument = Complete Understanding of Author's Message
  • Purpose: Author क्यों लिख रहा है (to inform, persuade, entertain)
  • Tone: Author का attitude क्या है (critical, supportive, neutral, etc.)
  • Argument: Author क्या claim कर रहा है और कैसे support कर रहा है

Example:

  • Purpose: To persuade
  • Tone: Urgent and concerned
  • Argument: Climate change requires immediate action by governments
  • Evidence: Rising global temperatures, extreme weather events, scientific consensus
11

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Purpose vs Main Idea — Purpose = why author wrote it; Main Idea = what the passage is about
  • Trap 2: Tone vs Mood — Tone = author's attitude; Mood = reader's feeling
  • Trap 3: Purpose vs Tone — Author can have a critical tone while purpose is to inform or persuade
  • Trap 4: Extreme Tone Words — "Outraged" vs "Critical" — choose the more appropriate one
  • Trap 5: Evidence vs Opinion — Facts vs author's personal views
  • Trap 6: Claim vs Evidence — Claim is what author argues; Evidence supports the claim
  • Trap 7: Fallacy Misidentification — Carefully distinguish between different types of fallacies
  • Trap 8: Neutral Tone — When passage only presents facts without opinion, tone is neutral/objective

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Passage 1: "The government's new education policy is a welcome step towards reforming our outdated education system. By introducing flexible curricula and skill-based learning, it addresses the needs of the 21st century. However, the real challenge lies in its implementation. Without adequate teacher training and infrastructure, even the best policies will fail to achieve their objectives."

Question 1 (Purpose)

What is the author's primary purpose?

  • A. To entertain readers
  • B. To inform about the new policy and express a cautious opinion
  • C. To completely criticize the government
  • D. To describe the history of education
Answer: B. To inform about the new policy and express a cautious opinion

Explanation: Author policy का positive aspect बताता है, लेकिन implementation challenges भी highlight करता है — balanced and informative approach।

Question 2 (Tone)

What is the author's tone in the passage?

  • A. Sarcastic
  • B. Optimistic yet concerned
  • C. Completely neutral
  • D. Angry
Answer: B. Optimistic yet concerned

Explanation: Author policy को "welcome step" कहता है (optimistic), लेकिन "challenge", "without adequate training" जैसे words concern दिखाते हैं।

Question 3 (Argument)

What is the author's main argument?

  • A. The new education policy is completely useless
  • B. The policy is good, but implementation is crucial for success
  • C. Teacher training is unnecessary
  • D. The government should not reform education
Answer: B. The policy is good, but implementation is crucial for success

Explanation: Author policy को support करता है, लेकिन implementation पर focus करता है।


Passage 2: "Despite what the advertisements claim, this product is nothing more than overpriced packaging with minimal actual benefit. Consumers are being misled by flashy marketing campaigns that have no scientific backing. It is time we stop falling for such deceptive practices and demand genuine value for our money."

Question 4 (Purpose)

The author's primary purpose is to:

  • A. Inform about the product's features
  • B. Persuade readers not to buy the product
  • C. Entertain with humorous content
  • D. Describe the history of advertising
Answer: B. Persuade readers not to buy the product

Explanation: Author स्पष्ट रूप से readers को product न खरीदने के लिए persuade कर रहा है ("stop falling for such deceptive practices")।

Question 5 (Tone)

The author's tone in the passage can best be described as:

  • A. Neutral and objective
  • B. Humorous and light-hearted
  • C. Critical and disapproving
  • D. Encouraging and supportive
Answer: C. Critical and disapproving

Explanation: "Nothing more than overpriced packaging", "misled", "deceptive practices" — ये सभी critical और disapproving tone दिखाते हैं।

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Author's Purpose = Why author wrote it
Author's Tone = Author's attitude toward subject
Author's Argument = Claim + Evidence + Reasoning

Purpose Types:

  • Inform — facts/data
  • Persuade — convince/change opinion
  • Entertain — story/emotions
  • Explain — how/why
  • Describe — vivid picture

Tone Words:

  • Formal, Informal, Critical, Optimistic, Pessimistic
  • Sarcastic, Humorous, Serious, Concerned, Neutral
  • Enthusiastic, Sympathetic, Apathetic, Ironic

Argument Structure:

  • Claim = Main position
  • Evidence = Support (facts, examples, expert opinions, statistics)
  • Reasoning = Logic connecting evidence to claim

Common Fallacies:

  • Ad Hominem, Straw Man, Appeal to Emotion, Hasty Generalisation
  • False Dilemma, Circular Reasoning, Post Hoc, Bandwagon

Exam Formula:

Purpose = What author wants to achieve | Tone = How author feels | Argument = What author claims + How it's supported

UGC NET में purpose, tone और argument के questions passage की deep understanding को test करते हैं। Word choice, sentence structure और use of evidence पर ध्यान दें।
