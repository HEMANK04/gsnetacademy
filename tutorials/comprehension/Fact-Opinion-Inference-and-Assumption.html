Fact, Opinion, Inference & Assumption

Complete tutorial: Fact vs Opinion, Inference, Assumption, identification techniques, common errors, practice questions & exam strategy for UGC NET Paper-1.

1

Meaning of Fact — तथ्य का अर्थ

Fact वह objective और verifiable statement है जिसे evidence, data, observation या reliable source के माध्यम से सत्यापित (verify) किया जा सकता है।

Fact = Verifiable + Objective + True/False

सरल उदाहरण:

  • "Delhi भारत की राजधानी है।" ✅ Verifiable
  • "पृथ्वी सूर्य के चारों ओर घूमती है।" ✅ Verifiable
  • "भारत की जनसंख्या 1.4 अरब से अधिक है।" ✅ Verifiable
  • "Water boils at 100°C at sea level." ✅ Verifiable

Key Characteristics:

  • Can be proven true or false
  • Based on evidence/observation
  • Objective (independent of personal feelings)
  • Measurable/observable
  • Same for everyone
2

Meaning of Opinion — राय का अर्थ

Opinion वह subjective statement है जो व्यक्ति के beliefs, feelings, attitudes, values या personal interpretation को व्यक्त करता है। इसे objectively verify नहीं किया जा सकता।

Opinion = Subjective + Personal Belief + Cannot Be Verified

सरल उदाहरण:

  • "Delhi भारत की सबसे अच्छी राजधानी है।" ❌ Subjective (अच्छा/बुरा — personal)
  • "Online education is better than traditional education." ❌ Subjective
  • "Summer is the most pleasant season." ❌ Subjective
  • "This movie is boring." ❌ Subjective

Key Characteristics:

  • Cannot be proven true or false
  • Based on personal feelings/beliefs
  • Subjective (depends on the person)
  • Contains value judgements (good/bad, right/wrong, beautiful/ugly)
  • Often uses words like: should, must, best, worst, better, beautiful, ugly, believe, think, feel
3

Fact vs Opinion — तथ्य vs राय

BasisFactOpinion
VerifiabilityCan be verified (true/false)Cannot be verified
NatureObjectiveSubjective
BasisEvidence, data, observationBeliefs, feelings, values
UniversalitySame for everyoneCan vary from person to person
Key WordsIs, was, are, according to, research showsShould, must, best, worst, believe, think, feel
Example"The Earth revolves around the Sun.""The Earth is the most beautiful planet."

Exam Point: UGC NET में अक्सर यह पूछा जाता है कि "Which of the following is a fact?" या "Which of the following is an opinion?" — आपको objective verifiable statements को subjective statements से अलग करना होता है।

4

Meaning of Inference — अनुमान का अर्थ

Inference text में explicit रूप से न लिखी गई information को clues, evidence और logical reasoning के आधार पर निकालना है।

Inference = Reading Between the Lines = Logical Conclusion from Evidence

Key Points:

  • Inference text पर आधारित होता है
  • Inference logical और evidence-based होता है
  • Inference text में directly नहीं लिखा होता
  • Inference must be supported by text evidence
  • Different readers may make different inferences (but should be logical)

सरल उदाहरण:

Text: "राम ने अपना बैग पैक किया, टिकट चेक किया और स्टेशन की ओर चल दिया।"

Inference: राम यात्रा पर जा रहा है। (text में explicit नहीं, पर clues से logical conclusion)

5

Types of Inference — अनुमान के प्रकार

  • Logical Inference — तार्किक अनुमान: Text के clues और logical reasoning से निकाला गया conclusion।
  • Predictive Inference — पूर्वानुमानात्मक अनुमान: Text के आधार पर भविष्य में क्या हो सकता है, इसका अनुमान।
  • Cause-Effect Inference — कारण-प्रभाव अनुमान: किसी घटना के कारण या प्रभाव का अनुमान।
  • Character Inference — पात्र अनुमान: किसी character के personality, motivation या feelings के बारे में अनुमान।
  • Author's Purpose Inference: Author क्यों लिख रहा है — इसका अनुमान।

Example:

Text: "She checked her watch repeatedly, tapped her foot, and looked at the door."

  • Inference: She is waiting for someone and is impatient.
  • Type: Character inference (personality/feeling)
6

Meaning of Assumption — धारणा का अर्थ

Assumption वह belief या idea है जिसे बिना साक्ष्य (evidence) के सत्य मान लिया जाता है। यह वह unstated belief है जिस पर argument या inference आधारित होता है।

Assumption = Unstated Belief + Taken for Granted + No Direct Evidence

Key Points:

  • Assumption text में directly नहीं लिखा होता
  • Assumption बिना evidence के स्वीकार किया जाता है
  • Assumption implicit होता है (अन्तर्निहित)
  • Assumption necessary हो सकता है (argument को समझने के लिए)
  • Assumption को question किया जा सकता है

सरल उदाहरण:

Statement: "Since she is a doctor, she must be rich."

Assumption: All doctors are rich. (अप्रमाणित belief)

7

Inference vs Assumption — अनुमान vs धारणा

BasisInferenceAssumption
DefinitionLogical conclusion from evidenceUnstated belief taken for granted
BasisText evidence/cluesNo direct evidence (unstated)
NatureLogical reasoningBelief/Supposition
SupportMust be supported by textOften unsupported
QuestionWhat can we conclude?What is taken for granted?
ExampleShe packed bags → She is travelingShe packed bags → She has money to travel

Exam Point: UGC NET में inference और assumption में अंतर करना बहुत महत्वपूर्ण है। Inference evidence-based होता है; Assumption evidence-free होता है।

9

Identification Techniques — पहचानने की तकनीकें

Fact vs Opinion:

  • Fact: Can be checked/verified. Look for numbers, dates, statistics, research findings.
  • Opinion: Personal. Look for value words (best, worst, beautiful, should, must, believe, think, feel).

Inference vs Assumption:

  • Inference: Based on text evidence + logical reasoning. Ask "What can I logically conclude from this?"
  • Assumption: Unstated. Ask "What is the author taking for granted here?"
10

Common Errors — सामान्य त्रुटियाँ

  • Error 1: Opinion को Fact समझ लेना — "The movie was boring" को fact मानना (यह opinion है)
  • Error 2: Fact को Opinion समझ लेना — "Water boils at 100°C" को opinion मानना (यह fact है)
  • Error 3: Assumption को Inference समझ लेना — बिना evidence के conclusion निकालना
  • Error 4: Inference को Assumption समझ लेना — evidence-based conclusion को unsupported मानना
  • Error 5: Over-inference — evidence से अधिक conclusion निकालना
  • Error 6: Under-inference — obvious conclusions को न निकालना
  • Error 7: Making assumptions without evidence
  • Error 8: Confusing author's opinion with fact
11

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Opinion statements that look like facts — "Research shows that..." (if citation is given, may be fact; if not, may be opinion)
  • Trap 2: Facts that sound like opinions — "Most students prefer..." (if survey/data supports, it's fact; if not, opinion)
  • Trap 3: Inference options that are too broad or too narrow
  • Trap 4: Assumption options that are actually inferences
  • Trap 5: Options that mix fact and opinion
  • Trap 6: Extreme inference (not supported by enough evidence)
  • Trap 7: Assuming something without textual support

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Passage: "According to the World Health Organization, more than 2 billion people worldwide do not have access to safe drinking water. This is a serious problem that requires immediate attention. Governments must invest more in water infrastructure to save lives. Clean water is the most basic human right."

Question 1 (Fact)

Which of the following is a fact according to the passage?

  • A. Clean water is the most basic human right
  • B. More than 2 billion people do not have access to safe drinking water
  • C. Governments must invest in water infrastructure
  • D. This is a serious problem
Answer: B. More than 2 billion people do not have access to safe drinking water

Explanation: यह WHO का verified data है — fact है। अन्य options opinions/value judgements हैं।

Question 2 (Opinion)

Which of the following is an opinion expressed in the passage?

  • A. 2 billion people lack safe water
  • B. This is a serious problem
  • C. WHO reported the data
  • D. Water boils at 100°C
Answer: B. This is a serious problem

Explanation: "Serious problem" एक value judgement है — opinion है। Data factual है।

Question 3 (Inference)

What can be inferred from the passage?

  • A. The WHO is the only organisation working on water issues
  • B. Lack of safe water affects health
  • C. All countries have safe water
  • D. Water is cheap
Answer: B. Lack of safe water affects health

Explanation: यह logical inference है — unsafe water से health problems हो सकती हैं, हालाँकि passage में explicit नहीं है।

Question 4 (Assumption)

What assumption underlies the statement "Governments must invest more in water infrastructure"?

  • A. Governments currently invest enough in water
  • B. Water infrastructure requires government investment
  • C. All governments have enough money
  • D. Private companies will not invest
Answer: B. Water infrastructure requires government investment

Explanation: Author यह मान रहा है कि water infrastructure के लिए government investment आवश्यक है — यह assumption है।


Passage 2: "The new education policy has been praised by many experts. It focuses on skill development and practical learning. However, some teachers have expressed concerns about its implementation. They believe that without proper training, the policy may not achieve its goals."

Question 5 (Fact vs Opinion)

Which statement from the passage is a fact?

  • A. The policy has been praised by many experts
  • B. The policy focuses on skill development
  • C. Teachers believe the policy may not succeed
  • D. Implementation will be difficult
Answer: B. The policy focuses on skill development

Explanation: यह policy का verified feature है। अन्य opinions/beliefs हैं।

Question 6 (Inference)

What can be inferred about the author's position?

  • A. The author completely opposes the policy
  • B. The author supports the policy but acknowledges implementation challenges
  • C. The author believes teachers are wrong
  • D. The author has no opinion
Answer: B. The author supports the policy but acknowledges implementation challenges

Explanation: Author policy को "praised" कहता है, लेकिन teachers' concerns भी report करता है — balanced position।

13

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Fact = Verifiable + Objective
Opinion = Subjective + Personal Belief
Inference = Logical Conclusion + Text Evidence
Assumption = Unstated Belief + No Evidence

How to Identify:

  • Fact: Can be verified (numbers, dates, data, research)
  • Opinion: Value words (best, worst, should, must, believe, think, feel)
  • Inference: "Reading between the lines" — logical conclusion from clues
  • Assumption: What is taken for granted — unstated belief

Key Questions:

  • Fact: Is it true/false? Can we check it?
  • Opinion: Does someone believe this? Is it subjective?
  • Inference: What can we logically conclude from the text?
  • Assumption: What is the author taking for granted?

Exam Formula:

Fact = Can be proven | Opinion = Personal belief | Inference = Evidence-based conclusion | Assumption = Unstated belief

UGC NET में fact, opinion, inference और assumption के questions passage की deep understanding और logical reasoning को test करते हैं।
