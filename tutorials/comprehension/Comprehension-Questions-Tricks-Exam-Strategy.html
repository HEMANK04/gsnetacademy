Types of Comprehension Questions — प्रश्नों के प्रकार

UGC NET Paper-1 में Comprehension से निम्न प्रकार के questions पूछे जाते हैं:

  • Factual/Literal Questions — तथ्यात्मक/शाब्दिक प्रश्न: Passage में directly लिखी information पर आधारित
  • Inferential Questions — अनुमानात्मक प्रश्न: Passage में explicit न लिखी, पर logically inferred जानकारी
  • Main Idea Questions — मुख्य विचार: Passage का central point
  • Title Questions — शीर्षक: Passage के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त title
  • Purpose Questions — उद्देश्य: Author क्यों लिख रहा है
  • Tone Questions — स्वर: Author का attitude या feeling
  • Vocabulary Questions — शब्दावली: Unfamiliar word का contextual meaning
  • Pronoun Reference Questions — सर्वनाम संदर्भ: Pronoun किस noun को refer कर रहा है
  • Connector Questions — संयोजक: Connector का function/what it indicates
  • Author's Argument Questions — लेखक का तर्क: Author का claim और उसका support
2

Factual/Literal Questions — तथ्यात्मक/शाब्दिक प्रश्न

Factual Questions = Answer is directly in the passage

Strategy:

  • Question में दिए गए keywords को passage में locate करें
  • Passage के exact or paraphrased portion को पढ़ें
  • उस portion से directly answer निकालें
  • अपना अनुमान या personal opinion न लगाएँ

Example Question: "According to the passage, when was the first education policy introduced?" — Passage में date/event देखें।

Key Words in Questions: According to the passage, states that, mentions, according to, as per the passage, the passage says

Trick: Factual questions के answers passage में directly होते हैं। बस सही location खोजनी है।

3

Inferential Questions — अनुमानात्मक प्रश्न

Inferential Questions = Answer is implied, not directly stated

Strategy:

  • Passage में clues और hints खोजें
  • Logical connection बनाएँ
  • Author ने क्या छुपाया/सुझाया है — यह समझें
  • Evidence-based conclusion निकालें

Example Question: "The author implies that..." — Author ने indirectly क्या कहा है?

Key Words in Questions: Implies, suggests, can be inferred, indicates, it can be concluded, most likely, probably

Trick: Inference केवल passage पर आधारित होना चाहिए। Personal knowledge/opinion नहीं use करें।

4

Main Idea Questions — मुख्य विचार

Main Idea = What the passage is mostly about

Strategy:

  • Passage का central theme पहचानें
  • Passage को एक वाक्य में summarise करने की कोशिश करें
  • Supporting details को ignore करें
  • Passage के introduction और conclusion पर ध्यान दें

Example Question: "What is the main idea of the passage?"

Key Words in Questions: Main idea, central idea, primarily concerned with, mainly discusses, overall theme

Trick: वह option जो all paragraphs को cover करता है — वही main idea है। Only one paragraph/detail वाला option नहीं है।

5

Title Questions — शीर्षक

Suitable Title = Concise summary of main idea

Strategy:

  • Main idea को short phrase में express करें
  • Title not too broad, not too narrow होना चाहिए
  • All key aspects को cover करना चाहिए

Example Question: "Which of the following is the most suitable title for the passage?"

Trick:

  • Too Broad: "Education" (जब passage "Online Education in India" पर हो)
  • Too Narrow: "Teacher Training" (जब passage whole online education system पर हो)
  • Correct: "Online Education in India: Challenges and Opportunities"
6

Purpose & Tone Questions — उद्देश्य और स्वर

Purpose = Why author wrote | Tone = How author feels

Purpose Strategy:

  • Passage का overall goal पहचानें
  • Is author trying to inform, persuade, entertain, or explain?
  • Look at the language and content of the passage

Tone Strategy:

  • Author का attitude पहचानें
  • Look at word choice (positive/negative words, formal/informal language)
  • Consider sentence structure and style

Common Tones: Critical, optimistic, pessimistic, neutral, concerned, sarcastic, enthusiastic, formal, informal, sympathetic, apathetic

7

Vocabulary Questions — शब्दावली

Vocabulary = Word meaning in context, not dictionary meaning

Strategy:

  • Word के आस-पास के context clues देखें
  • Look for synonyms, definitions, examples, contrasts around the word
  • Word को replace करके check करें — क्या sentence का meaning same रहता है?

Example Question: "The word 'lauded' in the passage most nearly means..."

Trick: Word का dictionary meaning विकल्प में हो सकता है, लेकिन contextual meaning को select करें।

8

Pronoun & Connector Questions — सर्वनाम और संयोजक

Pronoun Reference Strategy:

  • Pronoun से immediately पहले के nouns को देखें
  • Number (singular/plural) और gender agreement check करें
  • Logical noun को select करें

Connector Strategy:

  • Connector के function को समझें (contrast, addition, cause-effect, etc.)
  • Passage में connector क्या relationship दिखा रहा है — यह पहचानें

Example Questions:

  • "The pronoun 'they' refers to..."
  • "What does the connector 'however' indicate?"

Passage Reading Techniques — पैसेज पढ़ने की तकनीकें

  • Skim First — पहले स्किम करें: Passage को quickly पढ़ें (2-3 minutes). Focus on: Introduction, first sentence of each paragraph, conclusion.
  • Read Questions — फिर questions पढ़ें: Questions पढ़कर समझें कि क्या खोजना है।
  • Scan — स्कैन करें: Questions में keywords को passage में locate करें।
  • Read Carefully — ध्यान से पढ़ें: उस specific portion को carefully पढ़ें और answer निकालें।

Alternative Approach (Question-First):

  • पहले questions पढ़ें
  • फिर passage scan करें
  • Questions के answers को locate करें
  • यह approach time-saving हो सकती है

Which approach to use?

  • यदि passage short है → Skim first
  • यदि passage long है → Questions first
  • Practice में both approaches try करें और जो faster लगे वो use करें

Elimination Strategies — विकल्पों को हटाने की रणनीतियाँ

  • Too Broad: Option passage से व्यापक है → Eliminate
  • Too Narrow: Option केवल एक detail को cover करता है → Eliminate
  • Contradictory: Option passage से विपरीत है → Eliminate
  • Out of Scope: Option passage में mention नहीं है → Eliminate
  • Half Truth: Option partially correct पर incomplete है → Eliminate
  • Extreme Language: "Always", "Never", "All", "None" वाले options से सावधान → अक्सर incorrect
  • Absolute Statements: "Completely", "Entirely", "Absolutely" — often too extreme
  • Personal Opinion: Option passage से unrelated personal view है → Eliminate

Elimination Formula:

Correct Answer = Only option that is true + relevant + supported by passage
11

Time Management — समय प्रबंधन

  • Total Time: UGC NET Paper-1 में Comprehension के लिए 15-20 minutes
  • Passage Reading: 3-4 minutes (skimming)
  • Questions Solving: 1-1.5 minutes per question
  • Easy Questions First: Factual questions को पहले solve करें (fast)
  • Difficult Questions Later: Inference/main idea questions को बाद में solve करें (more time)
  • Don't Get Stuck: यदि कोई question 1.5 minutes से अधिक लग रहा है → skip and come back later
  • Review: अंत में 2-3 minutes बचाकर review करें
12

Common Errors & How to Avoid — सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं बचाव

  • Error 1: Passage को completely न पढ़ना → Solution: At least skim the passage.
  • Error 2: अपने personal knowledge का उपयोग करना → Solution: Answer हमेशा passage-based होना चाहिए।
  • Error 3: Factual question में inference लगाना → Solution: Factual = direct answer.
  • Error 4: Inference question में direct answer ढूँढना → Solution: Inference = logical conclusion.
  • Error 5: Options को not comparing करना → Solution: Always compare all options before selecting.
  • Error 6: Extreme options को eliminate नहीं करना → Solution: Always check for extreme language.
  • Error 7: Vocabulary question में dictionary meaning select करना → Solution: Contextual meaning is the answer.
  • Error 8: Time management में getting stuck → Solution: Skip and come back.

Quick Tricks & Tips — त्वरित ट्रिक्स एवं टिप्स

  • Trick 1: Main Idea → Introduction & Conclusion पर focus करें
  • Trick 2: Factual Questions → Keywords को passage में locate करें
  • Trick 3: Inference Questions → Clues & Evidence खोजें
  • Trick 4: Title Questions → Main Idea को short phrase में express करें
  • Trick 5: Tone Questions → Word Choice पर ध्यान दें (positive/negative words)
  • Trick 6: Purpose Questions → Is author informing, persuading, or entertaining?
  • Trick 7: Vocabulary Questions → Context Clues देखें (synonyms, examples, contrasts)
  • Trick 8: Pronoun Questions → Immediately preceding nouns को देखें
  • Trick 9: Connector Questions → Function समझें (contrast, addition, cause-effect)
  • Trick 10: Elimination → Too Broad, Too Narrow, Contradictory, Out of Scope को हटाएँ
Smart Reading = Skim + Scan + Eliminate + Select
14

Exam Day Strategy — परीक्षा के दिन की रणनीति

  • Before Exam: Practice at least 5-10 passages before the exam
  • During Exam:
    • पहले easy sections को solve करें (Teaching Aptitude, etc.)
    • Comprehension को middle में solve करें (not first, not last)
    • Don't panic if passage topic is unfamiliar
    • Focus on what the passage says, not your knowledge
  • After Solving:
    • Review any questions you skipped
    • Check for obvious errors
    • Don't change answers without strong reason

Last Minute Tips:

  • Passage को speed reading न करें — सही reading speed रखें
  • Mark key points while reading (if allowed)
  • Extreme options से बचें
  • Context को हमेशा ध्यान में रखें
  • Trust your practice

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Passage: "Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the education sector in unprecedented ways. Personalized learning platforms use AI algorithms to adapt content according to individual student needs. However, the integration of AI in education is not without challenges. Many educators worry about data privacy and the potential loss of human connection in classrooms. Despite these concerns, the potential benefits of AI — such as instant feedback and 24/7 accessibility — cannot be ignored."

Question 1 (Factual)

According to the passage, what do personalized learning platforms use AI algorithms for?

  • A. To replace teachers
  • B. To adapt content according to individual student needs
  • C. To monitor student behaviour
  • D. To create new subjects
Answer: B. To adapt content according to individual student needs

Explanation: Passage में directly लिखा है — "Personalized learning platforms use AI algorithms to adapt content according to individual student needs."

Question 2 (Inference)

What can be inferred about AI in education from the passage?

  • A. AI will completely replace teachers
  • B. AI has both benefits and challenges
  • C. AI is only useful for personalized learning
  • D. All educators support AI
Answer: B. AI has both benefits and challenges

Explanation: Passage mentions benefits (personalized learning, instant feedback) and challenges (data privacy, loss of human connection) — both are discussed.

Question 3 (Main Idea)

What is the main idea of the passage?

  • A. AI is transforming education
  • B. AI in education has benefits and challenges
  • C. Personalized learning is important
  • D. Data privacy is a concern
Answer: B. AI in education has benefits and challenges

Explanation: Passage balances both sides — transformation/benefits (para 1 & 3) and challenges/concerns (para 2).

Question 4 (Suitable Title)

Which of the following is the most suitable title for the passage?

  • A. The Future of AI
  • B. Artificial Intelligence: Transforming Education
  • C. AI in Education: Opportunities and Challenges
  • D. The Benefits of Technology
Answer: C. AI in Education: Opportunities and Challenges

Explanation: Passage both benefits और challenges को cover करता है — यह title सबसे appropriate है।

Question 5 (Tone)

The author's tone in the passage is best described as:

  • A. Completely optimistic
  • B. Completely pessimistic
  • C. Balanced and objective
  • D. Sarcastic
Answer: C. Balanced and objective

Explanation: Author benefits और challenges दोनों present करता है — balanced approach।

Question 6 (Vocabulary)

The word "unprecedented" in the passage most nearly means:

  • A. Ordinary
  • B. Without previous example
  • C. Predictable
  • D. Disappointing
Answer: B. Without previous example

Explanation: Unprecedented = never done or known before — transforming education in ways never seen before।

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Smart Reading = Skim + Scan + Eliminate + Select
Factual = Direct Answer | Inference = Logical Conclusion
Main Idea = Central Point | Title = Concise Summary

Question Types:

  • Factual → Find in text
  • Inference → Connect clues
  • Main Idea → Overall theme
  • Title → Short summary
  • Purpose → Why author wrote
  • Tone → Author's attitude
  • Vocabulary → Contextual meaning
  • Pronoun → Find antecedent
  • Connector → Identify function

Elimination Rules:

  • Too Broad → Eliminate
  • Too Narrow → Eliminate
  • Contradictory → Eliminate
  • Out of Scope → Eliminate
  • Half Truth → Eliminate
  • Extreme Language → Eliminate

Time Management:

  • Skim: 3-4 minutes
  • Questions: 1-1.5 minutes each
  • Easy first, difficult later
  • Review at the end

Exam Formula:

Comprehension Success = Smart Reading + Elimination + Time Management + Practice
