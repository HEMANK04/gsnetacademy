Vocabulary in Context, Pronoun & Connectors

Complete tutorial: Vocabulary in Context, Pronoun Reference, Connectors (coordinators, subordinators, transitions, conjunctive adverbs) for UGC NET Paper-1.

Vocabulary in Context — संदर्भ में शब्दावली

Vocabulary in Context का अर्थ है किसी unfamiliar word का अर्थ surrounding words, sentences और passage के context से समझना।

Vocabulary in Context = Understanding Word Meaning from Surrounding Text

Key Point: किसी शब्द का सामान्य अर्थ passage में उसके context से अलग हो सकता है। आपको word का dictionary meaning नहीं, बल्कि passage में उसका अर्थ पहचानना होता है।

सरल उदाहरण:

  • Sentence: "The teacher's acerbic remarks made the students uncomfortable."
  • Context: Remarks made students uncomfortable → Acerbic = sharp/harsh
  • Sentence: "She was elated when she received the scholarship."
  • Context: Received scholarship (positive event) → Elated = extremely happy
2

Types of Context Clues — संदर्भ सुराग के प्रकार

  • Definition/Explanation — परिभाषा/व्याख्या: Word को directly define या explain किया जाता है। Example: "The altruistic act — helping others without expecting anything in return — was appreciated by all."
  • Synonym — पर्यायवाची: Similar word का उपयोग। Example: "The arduous journey was difficult and exhausting."
  • Antonym — विलोम: Opposite word का उपयोग। Example: "Unlike her gregarious sister, she was shy and reserved."
  • Example — उदाहरण: Examples word के meaning को समझाते हैं। Example: "She had many culinary skills, such as baking, grilling, and sautéing."
  • Inference — अनुमान: पूरे sentence/passage से meaning infer करना। Example: "The mellifluous voice of the singer captivated everyone in the hall."
  • Contrast — विरोधाभास: Opposite idea से meaning समझना। Example: "The politician was loquacious, but his opponent was silent and reserved."
  • Cause-Effect — कारण-प्रभाव: कारण/प्रभाव से meaning समझना। Example: "The exigent situation required immediate action."
3

Pronoun Reference — सर्वनाम संदर्भ

Pronoun Reference यह identify करना है कि एक pronoun (he, she, it, they, them, this, that, etc.) किस noun (antecedent) को refer कर रहा है।

Pronoun → Refers to → Antecedent (the noun it replaces)

Key Point: UGC NET में pronoun reference questions passage की coherence और clarity को test करते हैं।

सरल उदाहरण:

  • Sentence: "Shyam lost his book. He was very upset."
  • Pronoun: He → Refers to: Shyam
  • Sentence: "The students submitted their assignments. They were well-prepared."
  • Pronoun: They → Refers to: The students
4

Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement — सर्वनाम-पूर्वपद संगति

  • Number Agreement: Singular pronoun → singular antecedent; Plural pronoun → plural antecedent
  • Gender Agreement: He/him/his → masculine; She/her/hers → feminine; They/them/theirs → gender-neutral/plural
  • Person Agreement: I/we (first person), You (second person), He/she/they (third person)

Example:

  • Correct: "Every student must submit his/her assignment."
  • Correct (Gender-neutral): "Every student must submit their assignment." (accepted in modern usage)
  • Incorrect: "Every student must submit their assignment." (traditionally considered incorrect; now widely accepted)
  • Correct: "The team celebrated its victory." (collective noun → singular)

Ambiguous Reference:

  • Ambiguous: "Ravi told Shyam that he was selected." → कौन selected हुआ — Ravi या Shyam? (Unclear)
  • Clear: "Ravi told Shyam, 'I was selected.'" (Clear)
5

Connectors — कनेक्टर्स (संयोजक)

Connectors वे words या phrases हैं जो sentences, clauses, paragraphs और ideas को logically connect करते हैं।

Connectors = Link Ideas + Show Relationships + Improve Coherence

Why Connectors Matter in UGC NET:

  • Passage की flow और structure समझने में मदद करते हैं
  • Author के argument और reasoning को समझने में मदद करते हैं
  • Questions passage में missing connector या incorrect connector के बारे में भी पूछ सकते हैं
6

Coordinating Conjunctions — समानयोजक संयोजक

FANBOYS — For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So

  • For — कारण/explanation (I was tired, for I had worked all day.)
  • And — addition (She likes tea and coffee.)
  • Nor — negative addition (He didn't go, nor did he call.)
  • But — contrast (She is smart, but lazy.)
  • Or — alternative/choice (You can stay or leave.)
  • Yet — contrast/despite (He is wealthy, yet unhappy.)
  • So — result/consequence (It was raining, so we stayed inside.)
7

Subordinating Conjunctions — अधीनयोजक संयोजक

  • Time: when, while, after, before, since, until, as soon as, whenever
  • Reason/Cause: because, since, as, now that
  • Contrast/Concession: although, though, even though, while, whereas
  • Condition: if, unless, provided that, as long as
  • Purpose: so that, in order that
  • Result/Effect: so...that, such...that

Example:

  • "Although it was raining, we went for a walk." (contrast)
  • "She left early because she was tired." (reason)
  • "Unless you study, you will not pass." (condition)
8

Transitional Words & Phrases — संक्रमणात्मक शब्द

  • Addition: additionally, moreover, furthermore, in addition, also, besides
  • Contrast: however, nevertheless, on the other hand, in contrast, conversely, although
  • Cause/Effect: therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence, accordingly
  • Sequence/Order: first, next, then, finally, subsequently, meanwhile
  • Example: for example, for instance, such as, to illustrate
  • Conclusion/Summary: in conclusion, to conclude, in summary, overall, ultimately
  • Emphasis: indeed, in fact, certainly, undoubtedly

Common Errors — सामान्य त्रुटियाँ

  • Error 1: Unfamiliar word का dictionary meaning लगाना — context का अर्थ अलग हो सकता है
  • Error 2: Pronoun reference में ambiguity — pronoun किस noun को refer कर रहा है, unclear होना
  • Error 3: Pronoun-antecedent agreement mismatch — singular/plural mismatch
  • Error 4: Incorrect connector — contrast के लिए addition connector का use
  • Error 5: Misunderstanding connector function — connector का function समझना ज़रूरी
  • Error 6: Overlooking context — passage का overall context ignore करना
  • Error 7: Confusing similar connectors — "however" vs "moreover"
11

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Vocabulary questions — options में common meaning और contextual meaning दोनों हो सकते हैं। Contextual meaning को choose करें।
  • Trap 2: Pronoun reference — options में multiple nouns हो सकते हैं। Most logical and nearest noun को select करें।
  • Trap 3: Connectors — options में different function connectors हो सकते हैं। Passage का logical relationship पहचानें।
  • Trap 4: Ambiguous pronoun — pronoun का antecedent clear न हो तो grammatically incorrect या confusing माना जाता है।
  • Trap 5: Misleading context — context clue जो misleading हो सकता है, उसे ignore करें और overall passage meaning देखें।
  • Trap 6: False synonyms — options में similar meaning वाले words हो सकते हैं, लेकिन only one fits the context।

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Passage 1: "The new education policy has been lauded by many experts. However, its implementation remains a challenge. While the policy is comprehensive, it requires significant resources. Teachers, who are the key stakeholders, need proper training. Therefore, the government must invest in professional development programmes."

Question 1 (Vocabulary)

In the passage, the word "lauded" most nearly means:

  • A. Criticised
  • B. Praised
  • C. Ignored
  • D. Described
Answer: B. Praised

Explanation: Context से "lauded" का positive meaning है — praised या commended।

Question 2 (Vocabulary)

The word "comprehensive" in the passage means:

  • A. Narrow
  • B. Limited
  • C. Complete and thorough
  • D. Confusing
Answer: C. Complete and thorough

Explanation: Context से comprehensive = व्यापक, पूर्ण, thorough।

Question 3 (Pronoun Reference)

In the passage, the pronoun "who" refers to:

  • A. The government
  • B. Teachers
  • C. Experts
  • D. Resources
Answer: B. Teachers

Explanation: "Teachers, who are the key stakeholders" — who teachers को refer करता है।

Question 4 (Connector)

What does the connector "Therefore" indicate in the passage?

  • A. Contrast
  • B. Addition
  • C. Cause and effect / conclusion
  • D. Example
Answer: C. Cause and effect / conclusion

Explanation: Therefore = परिणाम/निष्कर्ष — teachers को training चाहिए, इसलिए government invest करे।


Passage 2: "Online learning has become increasingly popular. However, it has its limitations. Students often struggle with lack of motivation because there is no face-to-face interaction. Moreover, technical issues can disrupt learning. Although online education offers flexibility, it cannot completely replace traditional classrooms."

Question 5 (Connector)

The connector "However" indicates:

  • A. Addition
  • B. Contrast
  • C. Cause
  • D. Sequence
Answer: B. Contrast

Explanation: Online learning popular है, but limitations हैं — contrast।

Question 6 (Connector)

The connector "because" indicates:

  • A. Contrast
  • B. Cause/Reason
  • C. Addition
  • D. Example
Answer: B. Cause/Reason

Explanation: Students struggle because there is no face-to-face interaction — reason।

Question 7 (Pronoun Reference)

In the passage, what does the connector "Although" indicate?

  • A. Cause
  • B. Contrast/Concession
  • C. Addition
  • D. Effect
Answer: B. Contrast/Concession

Explanation: Although = contrast/concession — flexibility है, but replace नहीं कर सकता।

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Vocabulary in Context = Word Meaning from Surrounding Text
Pronoun → Refers to → Antecedent
Connectors = Link Ideas + Show Relationships

Context Clues:

  • Definition, Synonym, Antonym, Example, Inference, Contrast, Cause-Effect

Pronoun Reference:

  • Pronoun must agree with antecedent (number, gender)
  • Ambiguous reference = unclear meaning

Connectors by Function:

  • Addition: and, also, moreover, furthermore, in addition
  • Contrast: but, however, although, yet, nevertheless, on the other hand
  • Cause/Reason: because, since, as
  • Effect/Result: so, therefore, thus, hence, consequently
  • Condition: if, unless, provided that
  • Purpose: so that, in order that
  • Example: for example, for instance, such as
  • Time/Sequence: then, next, finally, meanwhile
  • Conclusion: in conclusion, to conclude, overall

Exam Formula:

Vocabulary = Context | Pronoun = Antecedent | Connector = Relationship

UGC NET में vocabulary, pronoun और connector के questions passage की deep understanding और logical structure को test करते हैं।
