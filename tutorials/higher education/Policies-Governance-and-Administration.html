Policies, Governance & Administration

Complete tutorial: NEP 2020, NEP 1968, NEP 1986, UGC, AICTE, NAAC, NBA, NIRF, NRF, RUSA, HECI, NETF, and other key policies and governance frameworks in Indian Higher Education.

NEP 2020 — राष्ट्रीय शिक्षा नीति 2020

National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 is the first education policy of the 21st century in India — replacing NEP 1986.

NEP 2020 = 5+3+3+4 Structure + Multidisciplinary + Digital + Research + Flexibility
  • Vision: To make India a global knowledge superpower
  • School Structure: 5+3+3+4 (instead of 10+2)
  • Higher Education: Multidisciplinary universities, flexible curricula, multiple entry-exit options
  • Academic Bank of Credit (ABC): Digital credit account for students
  • National Research Foundation (NRF): To promote research and innovation
  • Higher Education Commission of India (HECI): Single regulatory body (replacing UGC)
  • Multiple Entry-Exit: Certificate (1 year), Diploma (2 years), Degree (3-4 years)
  • Mother Tongue/Regional Language: As medium of instruction (where possible)
  • Technology Integration: Online courses, digital classrooms, AI, virtual labs
  • Education for all by 2030: Universal access to education
2

NEP 1968 — राष्ट्रीय शिक्षा नीति 1968

National Education Policy (NEP) 1968 was the first comprehensive education policy after independence — based on the Kothari Commission (1964-66).

  • Chairman: Dr. Daulat Singh Kothari
  • Key Features:
    • Common School System
    • Three-language formula (Hindi, English, Regional language)
    • Investment of 6% of GDP in education
    • Vocationalisation of education
    • Teacher training and improvement
    • Democratic and secular values
    • Education for national integration
  • Significance: It laid the foundation for modern Indian education.
3

NEP 1986 — राष्ट्रीय शिक्षा नीति 1986

National Education Policy (NEP) 1986 replaced NEP 1968 — focused on quality, access, and equity in education.

  • Key Features:
    • National System of Education (10+2+3 structure)
    • Operation Blackboard (primary education)
    • Navodaya Vidyalayas (model schools)
    • Vocationalisation of education
    • Adult education and literacy
    • Medium of instruction: mother tongue
    • Research and development focus
  • POA 1992 (Programme of Action): Implemented NEP 1986
  • Significance: Focused on access and quality improvement.

UGC — विश्वविद्यालय अनुदान आयोग

University Grants Commission (UGC) is the statutory body for coordination, determination and maintenance of standards of university education in India.

  • Established: 1956 (Statutory body under UGC Act, 1956)
  • Headquarters: New Delhi
  • Functions: Grant-in-aid, Recognition (2(f) and 12(B)), Curriculum development, UGC-NET, Research promotion
  • Key Schemes: SAP, DSA, UPE, CPEPA, etc.
6

AICTE — अखिल भारतीय तकनीकी शिक्षा परिषद

All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) is the statutory body for planning, coordination, and regulation of technical education in India.

  • Established: 1987 (Statutory body under AICTE Act, 1987)
  • Headquarters: New Delhi
  • Functions: Approval of technical institutions, Curriculum development, Quality assurance
7

NAAC — राष्ट्रीय मूल्यांकन एवं प्रत्यायन परिषद

National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) is an autonomous body that assesses and accredits higher education institutions (HEIs) in India.

  • Established: 1994 (Autonomous body of UGC)
  • Headquarters: Bengaluru
  • Grading: A++ (3.51-4.00), A+ (3.26-3.50), A (3.01-3.25), B++ (2.76-3.00), B+ (2.51-2.75), B (2.01-2.50), C (1.51-2.00), D (≤1.50)
  • NAAC Grade is valid for 5 years.
8

NBA — राष्ट्रीय प्रत्यायन बोर्ड

National Board of Accreditation (NBA) is an autonomous body that accredits technical programmes (engineering, management, pharmacy, etc.) in India.

  • Established: 1994 (under AICTE)
  • Headquarters: New Delhi
  • Outcome-Based Accreditation: Focus on PO, PEO, CO
  • NBA is a signatory to the Washington Accord.
9

NIRF — राष्ट्रीय संस्थागत रैंकिंग फ्रेमवर्क

National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) is a methodology adopted by the Ministry of Education (MoE) to rank higher education institutions in India.

  • Launched: 2015
  • Parameters (5): TLR, RP, GO, OI, PR
  • Categories: Overall, Universities, Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Medical, Law, Architecture, Dental, Research, Innovation, Agriculture, etc.
  • NIRF ranking is published annually by the MoE.
10

NRF — राष्ट्रीय अनुसंधान फाउंडेशन

National Research Foundation (NRF) is a proposed body to promote research and innovation in India, as recommended by NEP 2020.

  • Purpose: To foster a culture of research and innovation
  • Functions: Funding for research projects, Interdisciplinary research, Industry-academia collaboration, Supporting early-career researchers
11

RUSA — राष्ट्रीय उच्चतर शिक्षा अभियान

Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (RUSA) is a centrally sponsored scheme for the development of higher education in India.

  • Launched: 2013
  • Purpose: Strategic funding to state universities and colleges
  • Key Areas: Infrastructure development, Faculty improvement, Research promotion, Quality enhancement
  • RUSA focuses on equity, access, and quality in higher education.
12

HECI — उच्च शिक्षा आयोग

Higher Education Commission of India (HECI) is the proposed single regulatory body for higher education in India, as recommended by NEP 2020.

  • Purpose: To replace UGC and AICTE (as single regulator)
  • Functions: Regulation of higher education, Academic standards, Quality assurance, Coordination
  • NEP 2020 proposes HECI as the umbrella regulator for all higher education (except medical and legal).
13

NETF — राष्ट्रीय शैक्षिक प्रौद्योगिकी मंच

National Educational Technology Forum (NETF) is a proposed platform for the use of technology in education, as recommended by NEP 2020.

  • Purpose: To promote technology integration in education
  • Functions: Sharing best practices, Pilot studies, Research on educational technology, Knowledge exchange
  • NETF will support online and digital learning initiatives.
14

Governance Structure — शासन संरचना

Governance Structure of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in India:

  • Chancellor: Honorary head (usually Governor of the state)
  • Vice-Chancellor (VC): Chief executive officer of the university
  • Pro-Vice-Chancellor: Deputy to VC
  • Registrar: Administrative head
  • Finance Officer: Financial management
  • Controller of Examinations (CoE): Examination management
  • Academic Council: Academic matters
  • Executive Council/Syndicate: Executive body
  • Board of Studies (BoS): Curriculum development
  • Finance Committee: Financial matters

Autonomous Colleges: Have degree-awarding powers; follow UGC regulations.

Affiliated Colleges: Affiliated to a university; follow university curriculum and regulations.

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: NEP 2020 = 10+2 structure → ❌ NEP 2020 introduced 5+3+3+4.
  • Trap 2: NEP 1968 was based on Radhakrishnan Commission → ❌ It was based on Kothari Commission.
  • Trap 3: UGC was established in 1986 → ❌ UGC was established in 1956.
  • Trap 4: AICTE regulates all higher education → ❌ AICTE regulates technical education only.
  • Trap 5: NAAC = NBA → ❌ NAAC accredits institutions; NBA accredits technical programmes.
  • Trap 6: NIRF = conducted by UGC → ❌ NIRF is conducted by the Ministry of Education.
  • Trap 7: NRF = existing body → ❌ NRF is proposed (NEP 2020).
  • Trap 8: HECI = UGC → ❌ HECI is proposed to replace UGC (as single regulator).

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

NEP 2020 introduced which school structure?

  • A. 10+2
  • B. 5+3+3+4
  • C. 8+4
  • D. 6+3+3
Answer: B. 5+3+3+4

Explanation: NEP 2020 replaced the 10+2 structure with the 5+3+3+4 structure.

Question 2

Which education policy was based on the Kothari Commission?

  • A. NEP 1968
  • B. NEP 1986
  • C. NEP 2020
  • D. NEP 1992
Answer: A. NEP 1968

Explanation: NEP 1968 was based on the Kothari Commission (1964-66).

Question 3

Which body is responsible for ranking higher education institutions in India?

  • A. UGC
  • B. AICTE
  • C. NIRF
  • D. NAAC
Answer: C. NIRF

Explanation: NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework) ranks HEIs in India.

Question 4

What is the full form of RUSA?

  • A. Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan
  • B. Rashtriya University Shiksha Abhiyan
  • C. Rural University Support Abhiyan
  • D. Regional University Support Abhiyan
Answer: A. Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan

Explanation: RUSA is a centrally sponsored scheme for higher education development.

Question 5

HECI is proposed to replace which body?

  • A. NAAC
  • B. NIRF
  • C. UGC
  • D. NBA
Answer: C. UGC

Explanation: HECI (Higher Education Commission of India) is proposed to replace UGC as the single regulator for higher education.

Question 6

Which of the following is NOT a regulatory body for higher education?

  • A. UGC
  • B. AICTE
  • C. NIRF
  • D. NCTE
Answer: C. NIRF

Explanation: NIRF is a ranking framework, not a regulatory body. UGC, AICTE, and NCTE are regulatory bodies.
