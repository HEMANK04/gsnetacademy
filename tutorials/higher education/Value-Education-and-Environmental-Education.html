Value Education — मूल्य शिक्षा

Value Education वह शिक्षा है जो learners में moral, ethical, social, cultural, and spiritual values को विकसित करती है — जिससे वे responsible and compassionate citizens बन सकें।

Value Education = Moral Development + Ethical Conduct + Social Responsibility + Character Building

Meaning:

  • Value education is the process of inculcating values in learners.
  • It helps in the all-round development of personality.
  • It develops critical thinking, empathy, and social responsibility.
  • It is an integral part of holistic education.
2

Types of Values — मूल्यों के प्रकार

  • Moral Values — नैतिक मूल्य: Honesty, truthfulness, integrity, respect, justice
  • Social Values — सामाजिक मूल्य: Tolerance, cooperation, equality, social justice
  • Cultural Values — सांस्कृतिक मूल्य: Heritage, traditions, respect for diversity
  • Spiritual Values — आध्यात्मिक मूल्य: Compassion, forgiveness, self-reflection
  • Aesthetic Values — सौंदर्य मूल्य: Appreciation of beauty, art, nature
  • Intellectual Values — बौद्धिक मूल्य: Curiosity, critical thinking, open-mindedness
  • Democratic Values — लोकतांत्रिक मूल्य: Freedom, equality, fraternity, justice
  • Environmental Values — पर्यावरणीय मूल्य: Respect for nature, sustainability, conservation
3

Sources of Values — मूल्यों के स्रोत

  • Family — परिवार: First and most important source of values
  • School and Teachers — स्कूल और शिक्षक: Formal education and role models
  • Peers — साथी/मित्र: Social interaction and peer influence
  • Religion and Spirituality — धर्म और अध्यात्म: Religious teachings and moral codes
  • Society and Culture — समाज और संस्कृति: Traditions, norms, customs
  • Media — मीडिया: Television, social media, books, films
  • Constitution and Law — संविधान और कानून: Constitutional values (justice, liberty, equality, fraternity)
  • Personal Experience — व्यक्तिगत अनुभव: Life experiences and reflections
4

Role of Education in Value Inculcation — मूल्य निर्माण में शिक्षा की भूमिका

  • Formal Curriculum: Including value education in the curriculum
  • Co-curricular Activities: Drama, debates, community service, NCC, NSS
  • Teacher as Role Model: Teachers demonstrate values through their actions
  • School Environment: Creating a value-based school culture
  • Storytelling and Literature: Using stories, biographies, and literature to teach values
  • Value-based Discussions: Classroom discussions on moral and ethical issues
  • Experiential Learning: Learning through experience — community service, field visits
  • Integration with Subjects: Values integrated across all subjects
5

Value Education in NEP 2020 — NEP 2020 में मूल्य शिक्षा

  • Holistic Education: Emphasis on holistic, multidisciplinary, and value-based education
  • Core Values: Respect for diversity, human rights, social responsibility, and environmental consciousness
  • Ethical and Moral Values: Honesty, integrity, compassion, empathy, and tolerance
  • Democratic Values: Constitutional values — justice, liberty, equality, fraternity
  • Experiential Learning: Community service, internships, and field visits to inculcate values
  • Teacher Training: Training teachers to integrate values into teaching
  • Cultural Values: Promotion of Indian heritage, languages, and arts

Environmental Education — पर्यावरणीय शिक्षा

Environmental Education (EE) वह शिक्षा है जो learners को environmental awareness, knowledge, attitudes, skills, and participation विकसित करने में मदद करती है — ताकि वे environmental issues को समझ सकें और solve कर सकें।

Environmental Education = Awareness + Knowledge + Attitude + Skills + Participation

Definition (UNESCO, 1970): Environmental education is the process of recognising values and clarifying concepts in order to develop skills and attitudes necessary to understand and appreciate the inter-relatedness among man, his culture, and his biophysical surroundings.

7

Objectives of Environmental Education — EE के उद्देश्य

IUCN/UNESCO Objectives of EE (1977, Tbilisi Conference):

  • Awareness — जागरूकता: Develop awareness and sensitivity to the environment
  • Knowledge — ज्ञान: Acquire knowledge of environmental issues and concepts
  • Attitudes — अभिवृत्ति: Develop positive attitudes towards the environment
  • Skills — कौशल: Develop skills to identify and solve environmental problems
  • Participation — भागीदारी: Encourage active participation in environmental protection
  • Evaluation — मूल्यांकन: Ability to evaluate environmental measures

Key Principles (Tbilisi Declaration, 1977):

  • EE is lifelong, interdisciplinary, and holistic
  • EE should be integrated into all subjects
  • EE should focus on real environmental issues
  • EE should develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills
8

Concept of Sustainability — सततता/स्थिरता की अवधारणा

Sustainability का अर्थ है present needs को पूरा करना without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs (Brundtland Commission, 1987).

  • Three Pillars of Sustainability:
    • Environmental — पर्यावरणीय: Conservation of natural resources, reducing pollution, protecting biodiversity
    • Social — सामाजिक: Equity, justice, human rights, quality of life
    • Economic — आर्थिक: Sustainable economic growth, resource efficiency, green economy
  • Education for Sustainable Development (ESD): Education that promotes knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes for a sustainable future
  • SDGs: Goal 4.7 — Education for Sustainable Development and Global Citizenship
9

Environmental Education in India — भारत में पर्यावरणीय शिक्षा

  • Supreme Court Order (1991): Environmental education made compulsory in schools and colleges (1991 Supreme Court directive)
  • Environmental Studies (EVS): Compulsory subject in primary school
  • UGC Circular (2003): Environmental studies made compulsory at the undergraduate level
  • National Curriculum Framework (NCF) 2005: Emphasises environmental education across curriculum
  • NEP 2020: Emphasises environmental awareness, sustainability, and climate action in education
10

Value Education & Environmental Education — मूल्य शिक्षा और पर्यावरणीय शिक्षा

  • Shared Goals: Both aim to develop responsible, ethical, and aware citizens
  • Environmental Values: Respect for nature, conservation, sustainability — these are values
  • Interconnectedness: Environmental education is a form of value education (values of sustainability)
  • Holistic Development: Both contribute to holistic development of learners
  • NEP 2020: Both are emphasised — value education and environmental education are integral parts of the curriculum
11

Environmental Education in NEP 2020 — NEP 2020 में पर्यावरणीय शिक्षा

  • Climate Change and Environmental Awareness: Emphasis on climate change, sustainability, and environmental protection
  • Integration Across Subjects: EE integrated into all subjects and disciplines
  • Experiential Learning: Field visits, projects, and community engagement
  • SDGs Alignment: Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) — aligned with SDG 4.7
  • Green Initiatives: Promotion of green practices in educational institutions
12

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Value education = only religious education → ❌ Value education includes moral, social, cultural, spiritual, and democratic values.
  • Trap 2: Environmental education = only science → ❌ EE is interdisciplinary — includes social, economic, and ethical dimensions.
  • Trap 3: Sustainability = only environmental protection → ❌ Sustainability includes environmental, social, and economic dimensions.
  • Trap 4: Tbilisi Conference = 1972 → ❌ Tbilisi Conference was 1977 (Stockholm 1972).
  • Trap 5: Environmental education in India = 1990 → ❌ Supreme Court order was 1991; UGC circular was 2003.
  • Trap 6: Values are only taught through religion → ❌ Values come from family, school, society, peers, media, and personal experience.
  • Trap 7: Value education is not part of NEP 2020 → ❌ NEP 2020 strongly emphasises value education and holistic development.

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

Which of the following is NOT a type of value?

  • A. Moral values
  • B. Environmental values
  • C. Market values
  • D. Democratic values
Answer: C. Market values

Explanation: Market values are economic concepts; moral, environmental, and democratic values are types of values in education.

Question 2

Environmental education was made compulsory in India by which order?

  • A. UGC Order, 2000
  • B. Supreme Court Order, 1991
  • C. NEP 2020
  • D. Kothari Commission
Answer: B. Supreme Court Order, 1991

Explanation: The Supreme Court of India ordered environmental education to be made compulsory in schools and colleges in 1991.

Question 3

The Tbilisi Conference on Environmental Education was held in:

  • A. 1972
  • B. 1977
  • C. 1987
  • D. 1992
Answer: B. 1977

Explanation: The Tbilisi Conference (1977) was the first major conference on Environmental Education, organised by UNESCO.

Question 4

Which of the following is NOT a pillar of sustainability?

  • A. Environmental
  • B. Social
  • C. Economic
  • D. Political
Answer: D. Political

Explanation: The three pillars of sustainability are Environmental, Social, and Economic.

Question 5

Value education in NEP 2020 emphasises all EXCEPT:

  • A. Human rights
  • B. Environmental consciousness
  • C. Competition
  • D. Social responsibility
Answer: C. Competition

Explanation: NEP 2020 emphasises values like human rights, environmental consciousness, and social responsibility — not competition.

Question 6

What are the five objectives of Environmental Education as per IUCN/UNESCO?

  • A. Awareness, Knowledge, Attitude, Skills, Participation
  • B. Reading, Writing, Arithmetic, Reasoning, Science
  • C. Memory, Recall, Recognition, Application, Analysis
  • D. Moral, Social, Spiritual, Cultural, Aesthetic
Answer: A. Awareness, Knowledge, Attitude, Skills, Participation

Explanation: The five objectives of EE are Awareness, Knowledge, Attitude, Skills, and Participation (IUCN/UNESCO, Tbilisi 1977).
