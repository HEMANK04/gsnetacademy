Learner's Characteristics: Adolescent & Adult Learners & Individual Differences

Meaning of Learner

Learner वह व्यक्ति है जो experience, instruction, observation, practice, interaction और reflection के माध्यम से अपने knowledge, skills, attitudes या behaviour में comparatively lasting change लाता है।

Learning = Experience + Interaction + Practice + Reflection + Change

Learner केवल information का passive receiver नहीं है। वह: नई information को prior knowledge से जोड़ता है, questions पूछता है, meaning construct करता है, experiences से सीखता है, feedback के आधार पर सुधार करता है, social और cultural environment से प्रभावित होता है।

2

Nature of Learner

  • Active — participate, question, apply, reflect
  • Unique — ability, interest, aptitude, motivation, pace, background
  • Goal-Oriented — examination, employment, skill, promotion
  • Social — influenced by teacher, peers, family, culture
  • Emotional — confidence, anxiety, interest, fear, self-esteem
  • Developing — characteristics change with age, experience
  • Constructs Knowledge — connects new information to existing knowledge
  • Requires Feedback — timely and constructive feedback improves performance
3

Learner's Characteristics

Learner's Characteristics वे विशेषताएँ हैं जो निर्धारित करती हैं कि learner: कैसे सीखता है (Academic), कैसे सोचता है (Cognitive), दूसरों से कैसे interact करता है (Social), emotions को कैसे manage करता है (Emotional), अन्य learners से क्यों अलग है (Individual Differences)।

Learner Characteristics = Academic + Cognitive + Social + Emotional + Individual Differences
4

Academic Characteristics

Academic characteristics यह बताती हैं कि learner formal learning situation में कैसे पढ़ता, समझता और perform करता है।

  • Prior knowledge
  • Academic readiness
  • Learning pace
  • Study habits
  • Attention and concentration
  • Achievement level
  • Subject interest
  • Language proficiency
  • Learning strategies
  • Self-regulation
  • Examination anxiety
  • Feedback requirements
New Learning = Prior Knowledge + New Experience
5

Cognitive Characteristics

Cognitive characteristics learner की mental processes से संबंधित हैं: Perception, Attention, Memory, Understanding, Reasoning, Imagination, Problem-solving, Decision-making, Critical thinking, Creativity, Metacognition.

Metacognition = Planning + Monitoring + Evaluating
6

Social Characteristics

Social characteristics बताती हैं कि learner दूसरों के साथ कैसे relate और interact करता है।

  • Peer interaction
  • Cooperation
  • Competition
  • Communication
  • Leadership
  • Group participation
  • Social acceptance
  • Belongingness
  • Cultural identity
  • Teacher–learner relationship
7

Emotional Characteristics

Emotional characteristics learner के feelings, self-concept, emotional regulation और motivational state से संबंधित हैं।

  • Self-esteem
  • Confidence
  • Anxiety
  • Fear of failure
  • Emotional stability
  • Sensitivity
  • Motivation
  • Frustration tolerance
  • Emotional regulation
  • Sense of security

Fear may produce temporary compliance, but it does not ensure meaningful learning.

8

Cognitive and Non-Cognitive Characteristics

  • Cognitive: Memory, Reasoning, Perception, Problem-solving, Understanding, Intelligence, Critical thinking
  • Non-Cognitive: Interest, Motivation, Attitude, Values, Beliefs, Personality, Self-concept, Emotional regulation

Exam Trap: Non-cognitive का अर्थ unimportant या irrational नहीं है। Motivation और self-efficacy academic performance पर गहरा प्रभाव डाल सकते हैं।

9

Adolescent Learner

Adolescence childhood और adulthood के बीच transition period है। इसमें rapid physical, cognitive, emotional और social changes होते हैं।

Adolescent Learner = Development + Identity + Peer Influence + Emotional Change + Emerging Abstract Thinking
10

Adolescent Learner — Academic Characteristics

  • Interest-Based Learning (career, exam, identity linked)
  • Need for Guidance and Autonomy
  • Achievement Orientation (marks, rank, peer comparison)
  • Developing Study Habits
  • Fluctuating Attention
  • Fear of Failure
Adolescent = Need for Autonomy + Need for Guidance
11

Adolescent Learner — Cognitive Characteristics

  • Abstract thinking
  • Logical reasoning
  • Hypothetical thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Critical thinking
  • Metacognition
  • Multiple perspectives
  • Future-oriented thinking

Piaget: Adolescence generally associated with Formal Operational Stage — hypothesis, abstract concepts, systematic reasoning, "what if?" situations.

Exam Trap: सभी adolescents automatically समान स्तर की formal reasoning नहीं दिखाते। Development experience, education, task familiarity और environment से प्रभावित होता है।

12

Adolescent Learner — Social Characteristics

  • Peer group influence high
  • Acceptance and belongingness important
  • Friendship and group identity strong
  • Independence increases
  • Social approval affects behaviour
  • Cooperation with competition
  • Leadership and group participation develop
  • Questioning of parental/teacher authority

Exam Point: Peer influence हमेशा negative नहीं होता। Positive peer group motivation, cooperation, academic engagement support कर सकता है।

13

Adolescent Learner — Emotional Characteristics

  • Emotional sensitivity increases
  • Mood variation
  • Self-consciousness increases
  • Sensitivity to criticism
  • Identity and self-esteem questions
  • Recognition and appreciation motivate
  • Tension between independence and guidance
  • Fear of failure and social comparison

Erikson: Adolescence conflict — Identity vs Role Confusion (Who am I? What are my values? What is my future role?)

14

Adolescent Learner — Teacher's Role

  • Avoid public humiliation
  • Give respectful and constructive feedback
  • Create safe classroom environment
  • Use peer collaboration positively
  • Show career-related relevance
  • Provide appropriate autonomy opportunities
  • Emphasise individual progress
  • Reduce comparison among learners
  • Give critical and abstract thinking tasks
  • Offer counselling and emotional support
15

Adult Learner

Adult learners के पास सामान्यतः अधिक life experience, स्पष्ट goals और practical learning needs होती हैं। Adult learning approach — Andragogy.

Adult Learner = Self-Direction + Experience + Relevance + Problem-Centred Learning + Internal Motivation

Thinker: Malcolm Knowles → Andragogy (Adult Learning) | Alexander Kapp → Early use of term Andragogy

16

Knowles' Assumptions of Adult Learning

  • Need to Know: "Why should I learn this?"
  • Self-Concept: Self-directed, want participation in learning decisions
  • Experience: Previous personal/professional experience is a resource
  • Readiness to Learn: Ready for things relevant to current roles/problems
  • Orientation to Learning: Problem/task/life-centred, not just subject-centred
  • Motivation: Internal (self-esteem, satisfaction, growth) + External (salary, promotion, certification)
17

Adult Learner — Academic Characteristics

  • Goal-Oriented (employment, promotion, skill improvement)
  • Relevance-Oriented ("How is this useful?")
  • Problem-Centred Learning
  • Self-Directed Learning
  • Immediate Application
  • Flexible Learning Requirements (schedule, modular content, recorded resources)
18

Adult Learner — Cognitive Characteristics

  • Experience-based understanding
  • Practical reasoning
  • Problem-solving orientation
  • Critical evaluation
  • Connecting new knowledge to prior experience
  • Better retention of meaningful content
  • Multiple perspectives
  • Reflective judgement

Important: Adult learning capacity समाप्त नहीं होती; learning speed, strategy और required support अलग हो सकते हैं।

19

Adult Learner — Social Characteristics

  • Multiple family and professional roles
  • Workplace experience
  • Expectation of respect and equality
  • Collaborative learning
  • Experience-sharing
  • Diverse professional backgrounds
  • Peer learning
  • Professional networking
  • Time constraints due to social responsibilities
20

Adult Learner — Emotional Characteristics

  • Self-respect and dignity important
  • Anxiety from previous academic failure possible
  • Hesitation with new technology
  • Need respectful feedback
  • Practical success motivates
  • Fear of appearing incompetent
  • Internal motivation
  • Professional identity

Teacher's Approach: Avoid patronising behaviour, use experience as resource, give choice, practical examples, problem-based tasks, immediate feedback, flexible delivery, respectful interaction.

21

23

Individual Differences

Individual Differences = Variations among learners in abilities, interests, personality, experience and development
  • Intelligence
  • Aptitude
  • Interest
  • Motivation
  • Personality
  • Prior knowledge
  • Learning pace
  • Achievement
  • Language
  • Culture
  • Socio-economic background
  • Physical and sensory abilities
  • Emotional development
  • Learning opportunities
  • Health
  • Self-efficacy
24

Sources of Individual Differences

Individual Differences = Heredity × Environment × Experience
  • Heredity: Biological/genetic factors, physical characteristics, predispositions, maturation pattern
  • Environment: Family, school, nutrition, culture, language, peer group, socio-economic conditions, learning opportunities
  • Experience: Previous education, success/failure, practice, professional exposure, social interaction, technology use
25

Intelligence, Aptitude, Interest and Achievement

ConceptMeaning
IntelligenceGeneral learning, reasoning and adaptation potential
AptitudeSpecific field future learning potential
InterestPreference towards an activity or subject
AchievementPreviously acquired learning or demonstrated performance
MotivationForce that initiates and sustains behaviour
Aptitude predicts potential; Achievement reflects acquired performance.
26

Educational Implications of Individual Differences

  • Diagnose prior knowledge (pre-test, questioning, concept map)
  • Differentiate instruction (content, process, product, support, pace)
  • Use multiple methods (lecture, discussion, demonstration, project, visual aids, cooperative learning, digital resources)
  • Provide flexible support (remedial, enrichment, extra practice, advanced tasks, peer support)
  • Use varied assessment (written, oral, project, portfolio, practical, observation)
  • Build inclusive classroom (diversity, not deficiency)
  • Avoid unfair comparison (individual progress + objective standards)
27

Equality vs Equity

  • Equality: सभी learners को same resource देना।
  • Equity: Learner की आवश्यकता के अनुसार appropriate support देना।

Example: सभी learners को same printed notes = Equality; visually impaired learner को accessible digital text/audio = Equity.

Equity does not lower standards; it removes irrelevant barriers.

Common Exam Traps

Misleading StatementCorrect Understanding
All adolescents are emotionally mature.Emotional regulation is still developing.
Peer influence is always negative.It can be positive or negative.
Every adolescent reasons abstractly at the same level.Development varies by learner and context.
Adults do not require guidance.They want respectful facilitation and support.
Adult learning is only self-directed.Degree of self-direction varies.
Adults have only internal motivation.Both internal and external motives are possible.
Adult experience is always correct.Experience is useful but can also bring biases.
Slow learner necessarily has low intelligence.Pace is affected by many factors.
Intelligence and achievement are identical.Potential and acquired performance are different.
Individual differences can be eliminated by education.Education addresses them; they are natural.
Same teaching ensures fairness.Different learners may need different support.
Andragogy term was coined by Knowles.Early use by Kapp; Knowles popularised framework.

Verified PYQs

PYQ 1: UGC NET, 24 Nov 2021, Shift 2

Adolescent characteristics: Increasing independence, rapid physiological changes, increased peer-group focus. "Completely emotionally mature" and "always rational" are NOT correct.

PYQ 2: UGC NET, 4 Dec 2021, Shift 1

Adult learner characteristics: Self-control, ability to act rationally, learning to be independent of guidance. Rapid growth and intense peer focus are adolescent.

PYQ 3: UGC NET, 5 Dec 2021, Shift 1

Understanding individual differences helps teacher in: designing course material, planning training programmes, designing teaching strategies.

PYQ 4: UGC NET, 20 June 2019, Shift 1

Social characteristic of learner: Ability to relate oneself with others.

PYQ 5: UGC NET, 22 Oct 2022, Shift 2

Self-directed and responsible learner — Andragogical Model.

PYQ 6: UGC NET, 21 Oct 2022, Shift 1

Adult learner characteristic NOT true: Rote learning.

PYQ 7: UGC NET, 16 June 2023, Shift 1

Individual differences that do NOT generally affect learning: Height, Blood group, Complexion.

PYQ 8: UGC NET, 13 March 2023, Shift 2

Intellectual characteristics of adolescents: Development of imagination, increase in span of attention, development of thinking and reasoning.

PYQ 9: UGC NET, 26 June 2025, Shift 2

Adolescent characteristics: Increased emotional sensitivity, strong need for social belonging, development of abstract thinking.

One-Page Revision

Learner Characteristics = Academic + Cognitive + Social + Emotional + Individual Differences
  • Academic: Prior Knowledge + Readiness + Study Habits + Achievement
  • Cognitive: Attention + Memory + Reasoning + Problem-Solving + Metacognition
  • Social: Interaction + Cooperation + Peer Influence + Belongingness
  • Emotional: Confidence + Anxiety + Self-Esteem + Motivation
  • Adolescent: Identity + Peer Influence + Emerging Abstract Thinking + Emotional Change
  • Piaget: Formal Operational Stage | Erikson: Identity vs Role Confusion
  • Adult Learner: Self-Direction + Experience + Relevance + Application + Internal Motivation
  • Knowles: Andragogy | Kapp: Early use of term Andragogy
  • Individual Differences: Heredity × Environment × Experience
  • Intelligence: Learning Potential | Aptitude: Specific Future Potential | Achievement: Acquired Performance
  • Equality: Same Support | Equity: Need-Based Appropriate Support

Final Exam Approach: किसी characteristic को classify करने के लिए पूछें: पढ़ने और performance से संबंधित? → Academic | Thinking और mental process से? → Cognitive | दूसरों से interaction से? → Social | Feelings, anxiety, self-esteem से? → Emotional | Learners के बीच variation? → Individual Difference
