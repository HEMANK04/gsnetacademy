Meaning of Teaching Method

Teaching Method से आशय उस व्यवस्थित प्रक्रिया से है जिसके माध्यम से teacher निर्धारित learning objectives प्राप्त करने के लिए content, activities, resources, communication और assessment को organise करता है।

Teaching Method = Objectives + Content + Learner Needs + Learning Activities + Assessment

कोई एक method प्रत्येक subject, learner और situation के लिए best नहीं होता।

Right Method = Objective + Learner + Content + Class Size + Resources + Context
2

Classification of Teaching Methods

  • A. Participation basis: Teacher-centred vs Learner-centred
  • B. Delivery Mode: Offline/Face-to-face, Online, Blended/Hybrid
  • C. Communication Timing: Synchronous vs Asynchronous
3

Teacher-Centred Method

Teacher-centred = Teacher Control + Content Delivery + Uniform Pace + Learner Reception
  • Teacher is primary source of content
  • Controls classroom activities
  • Determines teaching pace
  • Explains content
  • Expects prescribed responses from learners
  • Learner role is comparatively receptive

Methods: Lecture, Explanation, Demonstration, Direct instruction, Expository method, Chalk and talk, Teacher-led presentation, Drill and practice

4

Lecture Method

व्याख्यान विधि — Teacher systematically oral presentation through information/explanation.

  • When useful: Large class, limited time, new topic introduction, expert knowledge, overview, theoretical subjects
  • Advantages: Large number of learners, economical, systematic content, expert insights
  • Limitations: Learner passive, individual differences ignored, limited critical thinking, one-way communication risk

Exam Trap: Lecture method is not always ineffective. Short, interactive, well-structured lecture with examples, questions, feedback can be effective.

5

Demonstration Method

Teacher performs a process/experiment/skill; learner observes.

Examples: Lab experiment, statistical software operation, teaching skill demo, machine operation, art technique.

Important: If teacher only demonstrates → teacher-directed. If learners practice, discuss, get feedback → becomes learner-centred.

6

Direct Instruction

  • State objective
  • Explain concept/skill
  • Present model
  • Guided practice
  • Provide feedback
  • Independent practice

This is structured teaching — not merely passive lecture.

7

Characteristics of Teacher-Centred Teaching

  • Teacher dominant position
  • Emphasis on content and syllabus
  • Same content and pace for all
  • More verbal presentation
  • Limited learner participation
  • External discipline
  • Teacher-controlled assessment
  • Objective/recall-based exams
  • Mainly teacher-to-learner communication
  • Knowledge transmission orientation
8

Learner-Centred Method

Learner-centred = Active Participation + Choice + Experience + Collaboration + Reflection + Responsibility
  • Learner is active participant
  • Teacher role: Facilitator, Guide, Mentor, Resource person, Learning designer
  • Learner constructs meaning through activity, experience, interaction
9

Learner-Centred Methods

  • Discussion, Project, Problem-solving, Inquiry, Discovery learning, Case study
  • Seminar, Tutorial, Workshop, Cooperative learning, Collaborative learning
  • Peer teaching, Role-playing, Simulation, Brainstorming, Experiential learning
  • Flipped classroom, Research-based learning
10

Discussion Method

Structured exchange on an issue, concept or problem.

  • Develops: Communication, listening, reasoning, multiple perspectives, critical thinking, respect for disagreement
11

Project Method

Learners plan, collect data, execute and present on a meaningful task/problem.

Project = Purpose + Planning + Execution + Evaluation

Associated with William H. Kilpatrick, influenced by John Dewey's pragmatist philosophy.

  • Advantages: Learning by doing, collaboration, real-life application, research skills, responsibility, creativity
  • Limitations: Time-consuming, unequal participation, difficult assessment, not all concepts efficient
12

Problem-Solving Method

Problem Identification → Hypothesis → Evidence → Analysis → Solution → Evaluation
13

Case Study Method

  • Real/realistic situation description given
  • Learner: identifies problem, understands stakeholders, compares alternatives, justifies decision
  • Useful in management, law, education, medicine, public policy
14

Cooperative Learning

Structured small groups working towards common objective.

  • Key Elements: Positive interdependence, Individual accountability, Face-to-face interaction, Social skills, Group processing

Exam Trap: Just seating students in groups is NOT cooperative learning. Common goal AND individual accountability are required.

15

Collaborative Learning

Learners construct knowledge together. More flexible structure than cooperative learning.

More structuredRelatively flexible
Teacher may assign rolesGroup negotiates roles

16

Seminar & Tutorial

  • Seminar: Presentation on selected topic followed by questions and discussion. Suitable for higher education, research presentation, academic communication
  • Tutorial: Small group/individualised interaction focusing on specific difficulties, doubts, progress
17

Flipped Classroom

Traditional: Lecture in class → Homework at home

Flipped: Content before class → Discussion, application, problem-solving in class

Example: Students watch SWAYAM video at home; solve case study in class.

Exam Trap: Just sending recorded video is NOT flipped classroom. In-class active learning with pre-class content is required.

19

Offline Teaching

ऑफलाइन अथवा प्रत्यक्ष शिक्षण — Teacher and learner in physical learning environment.

  • Examples: Classroom lecture, Laboratory, Face-to-face seminar, Field visit, Workshop, Tutorial, Library-based learning
  • Advantages: Direct human interaction, immediate doubt resolution, body language easy, hands-on work, socialisation, discipline
  • Limitations: Time/location restrictions, infrastructure required, individualisation hard in large classes, limited reach
20

Online Teaching

ऑनलाइन शिक्षण — Internet and digital technologies for teaching-learning.

  • Examples: Live Meet/Zoom class, LMS, SWAYAM, Recorded lecture, Online quiz, Discussion forum, Virtual lab, Webinar, E-content
  • Characteristics: Geographic flexibility, digital content, synchronous/asynchronous, multimedia, online assessment, wider reach
21

Synchronous and Asynchronous Learning

  • Synchronous (समकालिक): Same time participation. Examples: Live online class, webinar, real-time chat, video conference. Advantages: Immediate interaction, instant feedback, social presence. Limitations: Fixed time, stable Internet required, time-zone issues.
  • Asynchronous (असमकालिक): Same-time presence not required. Examples: Recorded lecture, e-mail, discussion board, downloadable notes, self-paced MOOC. Advantages: Time flexibility, self-paced, repeatable. Limitations: Delayed feedback, isolation risk, high self-discipline required.

23

Blended Learning

Blended Learning = Offline Interaction + Online Flexibility

Face-to-face and online learning integrated in planned manner.

Example: SWAYAM video/reading at home; classroom discussion/problem-solving; online quiz; face-to-face feedback.

Exam Trap: Uploading classroom lecture recording is NOT meaningful blended learning. Purposeful integration of both modes is required.

24

MOOC

Massive Open Online Course

  • Massive: Large number of learners
  • Open: Relatively open access (but "open" ≠ always free)
  • Online: Digital platform delivery
  • Course: Structured content, activities, assessment, learning outcomes

Examples: SWAYAM, NPTEL, Coursera, edX, FutureLearn

  • Advantages: Wider access, flexibility, lifelong learning, self-paced, affordable, repeat viewing
  • Limitations: Digital divide, low completion, isolation, limited personal interaction, self-regulation needed, assessment credibility
25

SWAYAM

Study Webs of Active Learning for Young Aspiring Minds

Government of India's national online learning platform for access, equity and quality.

Four Quadrants:

SWAYAM = Watch + Read + Test + Discuss
  • Quadrant 1 (Watch): Video lecture, demonstrations, audio-video, animations
  • Quadrant 2 (Read): E-text, downloadable notes, references, transcripts
  • Quadrant 3 (Test): Quiz, MCQ, assignment, practice test
  • Quadrant 4 (Discuss): Doubt clarification, learner interaction, academic discussion, instructor support

Applications in Higher Education

  • Wider Access: Remote and working learners
  • Supplementary Learning: University teaching with SWAYAM videos, readings, quizzes
  • Credit Learning: Approved SWAYAM courses under UGC framework
  • Faculty Development: Professional knowledge, digital pedagogy, subject updates
  • Flipped Classroom: Pre-class online + in-class application
  • Research Training: Research methodology, statistical software, academic writing, research ethics, data analysis, literature review
  • Inclusive & Lifelong Learning: Working professionals, adult learners, geographically isolated

Important Exam Points

  • Teacher-centred: teacher primary decision-maker
  • Learner-centred: teacher facilitator
  • Project work and group discussion → learner-centred
  • Lecture method → generally teacher-centred
  • Online is NOT automatically learner-centred
  • Offline is NOT automatically teacher-centred
  • Synchronous = same time; Asynchronous = own time
  • MOOC = Massive Open Online Course
  • SWAYAM = Indian online MOOC platform
  • SWAYAM four quadrants: Video, Reading, Self-Assessment, Discussion
  • SWAYAM Prabha = DTH educational broadcasting
  • NPTEL = Engineering; CEC = UG; UGC = Non-technical PG; IIM Bangalore = Management; NITTTR = Teacher Training
  • Blended = planned offline + online integration
  • Flipped = content before class + application in class
  • "Open" ≠ every course certificate/exam is free
