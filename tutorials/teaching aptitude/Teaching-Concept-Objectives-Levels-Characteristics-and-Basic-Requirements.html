Teaching: Concept, Objectives, Levels, Characteristics & Basic Requirements

Complete tutorial: concept, objectives, three levels (Memory, Understanding, Reflective), characteristics, teacher-learner roles, requirements & PYQs.

Concept of Teaching

शिक्षण की अवधारणा — Teaching एक planned, purposeful and interactive process है, जिसके माध्यम से teacher learner के knowledge, understanding, skills, attitudes और behaviour में desirable change लाने का प्रयास करता है।

Teaching = Planned Interaction + Learning Facilitation + Behavioural Change + Development

शिक्षण केवल information देना या lecture deliver करना नहीं है। Effective teaching में teacher: learning objectives निर्धारित करता है, learner की needs समझता है, content व method चुनता है, environment तैयार करता है, learner को actively participate कराता है, feedback और assessment देता है।

उदाहरण: केवल "भारत का संविधान 26 जनवरी 1950 को लागू हुआ" बताना information transmission है। परंतु "संविधान को 26 नवम्बर 1949 को अपनाने के बाद 26 जनवरी 1950 को ही क्यों लागू किया गया?" — यह meaningful teaching है।

2

Teaching, Training, Instruction & Indoctrination

ConceptFocus
Teaching — शिक्षणKnowledge, understanding, skills, values & reflection
Instruction — अनुदेशनContent delivery, directions
Training — प्रशिक्षणSkill proficiency through practice
Indoctrination — मतारोपणFixed belief without critical examination
Conditioning — अनुबंधनBehaviour formation via stimulus-response
Teaching ≠ Training (Training is skill-specific; teaching develops knowledge, understanding, attitude, judgement & reflection)
3

Nature & Characteristics of Teaching

  • Purposeful — clear objectives
  • Interactive — two-way communication
  • Triadic — Teacher + Learner + Subject Matter
  • Art and Science — creative & evidence-based
  • Social — influenced by society, culture
  • Developmental — cognitive, affective, skill
  • Dynamic & Flexible — adapts to situation
  • Continuous — planning → teaching → assessment → feedback → improvement
  • Learner-oriented — active participation, meaning-making
4

Objectives of Teaching

  • Acquisition of Knowledge
  • Development of Understanding
  • Application of Knowledge
  • Development of Skills
  • Critical & Reflective Thinking
  • Attitude & Value Development
  • Independent Learning
Best Teaching gradually reduces unnecessary learner dependence on the teacher.
5

Domains of Teaching Objectives

  • Cognitive — Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyse → Evaluate → Create (Bloom's revised)
  • Affective — Receiving → Responding → Valuing → Organisation → Characterisation
  • Psychomotor — physical, technical, performance skills

Effective teaching addresses all three domains, not only cognitive.

6

Levels of Teaching

तीन मुख्य levels: Memory → Understanding → Reflective (thoughtfulness और learner independence बढ़ती है)

Herbart → Memory Level  |  H.C. Morrison → Understanding Level  |  Hunt → Reflective Level
7

Memory Level of Teaching

Thinker: John Friedrich Herbart

Memory Level = Facts + Repetition + Recall + Recognition + Retention
  • Teacher-centred, subject-centred, learner passive
  • Rote learning, drill, external motivation
  • Recall-based evaluation (objective, fill-in, matching)
  • Useful for basics, formulas, vocabulary, primary classes
  • Limitation: no guarantee of understanding, critical thinking weak
8

Understanding Level of Teaching

Thinker: H.C. Morrison

Understanding Level = Meaning + Relationship + Explanation + Generalisation + Application
  • Learner understands relationships among facts & ideas
  • Teacher as organiser & explainer; learner active participant
  • Positive & negative examples used
  • Morrison cycle: Exploration → Presentation → Assimilation → Organisation → Recitation
  • Evaluation: explanation, comparison, interpretation, application
9

Reflective Level of Teaching

Thinker: Hunt

Reflective Level = Problem + Inquiry + Evidence + Critical Thinking + Independent Solution
  • Highest & most thoughtful level
  • Problem-centred, learner-centred, inquiry-oriented
  • Teacher as facilitator; learner as independent investigator
  • Open, democratic, interactive classroom
  • Evaluation: case study, project, open-ended, debate, portfolio

11

Autonomous Development Level

Morris L. Bigge द्वारा उल्लेखित। Learner को अधिकतम freedom, self-judgement, self-direction; formal teaching minimal. Exam caution: Standard Paper-1 main emphasis on three levels — Memory, Understanding, Reflective. Autonomous Development can appear as distractor.

12

Basic Requirements of Effective Teaching

  • Clear Objectives (specific, observable, assessable)
  • Subject Knowledge (concepts, principles, applications)
  • Pedagogical Knowledge (what, why, whom, how, assess)
  • Learner Knowledge (age, ability, prior knowledge, interest, etc.)
  • Communication Skills (clarity, language, questioning, feedback)
  • Suitable Learning Environment (safe, inclusive, democratic)
  • Appropriate Teaching Methods (based on objective, content, learner)
  • Teaching-Learning Resources (ICT, models, cases, etc.)
  • Assessment & Feedback (diagnostic, formative, summative, self, peer)
  • Reflective Practice (self-evaluation after lesson)
13

Important Exam Points

  • Teaching is purposeful & interactive.
  • Memory Level → Herbart; Understanding → Morrison; Reflective → Hunt.
  • Memory: recall, recognition, retention.
  • Understanding: relationships among facts & ideas.
  • Reflective: problem-centred, learner-centred.
  • Reflective has most active & interactive communication.
  • Effective teaching needs subject knowledge + communication + pedagogy.
  • Rigid lesson plan is NOT necessary condition.
  • Higher levels do not eliminate lower levels; they build on them.
  • Teaching objectives cover cognitive, affective, psychomotor domains.
14

Common Exam Traps

  • Trap 1: Understanding Level is NOT highest — Reflective is highest.
  • Trap 2: Herbart→Memory, Morrison→Understanding, Hunt→Reflective (don't interchange).
  • Trap 3: Memory is teacher-centred; Reflective is learner-centred.
  • Trap 4: Explaining relationship = Understanding; unfamiliar problem justified solution = Reflective.
  • Trap 5: Only formula = Memory; meaning & relationship = Understanding; complex problem with decision = Reflective.
  • Trap 6: Reflective level teacher is NOT absent; role changes to facilitator.
  • Trap 7: Excellent subject knowledge alone is NOT sufficient; pedagogy, communication, learner understanding, feedback also required.

Verified PYQs

PYQ 1: UGC NET, 9 Oct 2020, Shift 2

Question: Facts and information get focused in which level of teaching?
Answer: Memory Level

PYQ 2: UGC NET, 5 Nov 2020, Shift 1

Answer: Understanding Level focuses on seeking relationships among facts and ideas.

PYQ 3: UGC NET, 2 Dec 2019, Shift 1

Answer: Reflective Level: helping students explore and explain problems and their solutions.

PYQ 4: UGC NET, 17 Oct 2020, Shift 1

Answer: Reflective Level has highly active and interactive communication.

PYQ 5: UGC NET, 3 Dec 2019, Shift 1

Answer: Lower-level learning outcomes (recall, recognition) are focused in Memory Level.

PYQ 6: UGC NET, 13 June 2023, Shift 2

Answer: Reflective Level involves highest-order thinking skills.

Final Revision Formula

Teaching = Objective + Interaction + Facilitation + Assessment + Development
Memory = Remember and Reproduce  |  Understanding = Explain and Relate  |  Reflective = Analyse, Decide and Solve
Herbart → Memory → Recall  |  Morrison → Understanding → Relationship  |  Hunt → Reflective → Problem-solving

Classroom Identification Rule: बताता है → Memory, समझाता है → Understanding, तर्क देकर समाधान बनाता है → Reflective
