Environmental Pollution — पर्यावरणीय प्रदूषण

Environmental Pollution environment में harmful substances (pollutants) का introduction है जो living organisms और natural systems को negatively impact करता है।

Pollution = Contaminant + Environment + Adverse Effect

Types of Pollution:

  • Air Pollution — वायु प्रदूषण
  • Water Pollution — जल प्रदूषण
  • Soil/Land Pollution — मृदा प्रदूषण
  • Noise Pollution — ध्वनि प्रदूषण
  • Thermal Pollution — तापीय प्रदूषण
  • Radiation Pollution — विकिरण प्रदूषण
2

Air Pollution — वायु प्रदूषण

Air Pollution atmosphere में harmful gases, particulate matter और biological molecules का presence है।

Major Pollutants:

  • Primary Pollutants: Directly emitted — CO, SO₂, NOx, PM2.5, PM10, VOCs
  • Secondary Pollutants: Formed from reactions — Ozone (O₃), Smog, Acid rain

Sources:

  • Fossil fuel burning (vehicles, industries)
  • Industrial emissions
  • Agricultural activities
  • Deforestation
  • Natural sources (volcanoes, dust storms)

Effects:

  • Respiratory diseases (asthma, bronchitis)
  • Global warming and climate change
  • Acid rain (damages buildings, forests, water bodies)
  • Ozone layer depletion
3

Water Pollution — जल प्रदूषण

Water Pollution water bodies (rivers, lakes, oceans, groundwater) में contaminants का introduction है जो water quality degrade करता है।

Sources:

  • Industrial effluents (chemicals, heavy metals)
  • Agricultural runoff (fertilizers, pesticides, manure)
  • Domestic sewage (organic matter, pathogens)
  • Oil spills
  • Plastic waste

Effects:

  • Waterborne diseases (cholera, typhoid, dysentery)
  • Eutrophication (excess nutrients → algal blooms)
  • Bioaccumulation and biomagnification
  • Loss of aquatic biodiversity
4

Soil Pollution — मृदा प्रदूषण

Soil Pollution soil में toxic chemicals, heavy metals, pesticides और waste का accumulation है जो soil quality degrade करता है।

Sources:

  • Industrial waste dumping
  • Excessive use of fertilisers and pesticides
  • Mining activities
  • Urban waste disposal
  • Oil spills and chemical leaks

Effects:

  • Reduced soil fertility
  • Contamination of groundwater
  • Toxicity to plants and animals
  • Health risks to humans (through food chain)
5

Noise Pollution — ध्वनि प्रदूषण

Noise Pollution unwanted or excessive sound है जो human health और environment को adversely affect करता है।

Sources:

  • Transportation (road, rail, air)
  • Industrial activities
  • Construction
  • Entertainment (loudspeakers, music)
  • Household appliances

Measured in: Decibels (dB)

Effects:

  • Hearing loss
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Sleep disturbance
  • Cardiovascular problems
  • Reduced concentration

Permissible Limits:

  • Residential area: 55 dB (day), 45 dB (night)
  • Industrial area: 75 dB (day), 70 dB (night)
  • Commercial area: 65 dB (day), 55 dB (night)
  • Silent zone: 50 dB (day), 40 dB (night)

Solid Waste — ठोस अपशिष्ट

Solid Waste वे unwanted or discarded solid materials हैं जो human activities से उत्पन्न होते हैं।

Types:

  • Municipal Solid Waste (MSW): Household, commercial, institutional waste
  • Industrial Solid Waste: Factory waste, construction debris
  • Agricultural Waste: Crop residues, animal manure
  • Hazardous Waste: Toxic, flammable, reactive, corrosive
  • Biomedical Waste: Hospital and healthcare waste
  • E-waste: Electronic and electrical waste
7

Biomedical Waste — जैव-चिकित्सा अपशिष्ट

Biomedical Waste healthcare activities (hospitals, clinics, laboratories, research) से उत्पन्न होने वाला waste है — which may be infectious, hazardous, or toxic.

Types:

  • Infectious Waste: Blood, body fluids, cultures, sharps
  • Pathological Waste: Tissues, organs, body parts
  • Sharp Waste: Needles, syringes, scalpels
  • Pharmaceutical Waste: Expired medicines
  • Chemical Waste: Laboratory chemicals
  • Radioactive Waste: Radioactive materials

Disposal Methods:

  • Incineration
  • Autoclaving (steam sterilisation)
  • Microwave treatment
  • Chemical disinfection
  • Landfilling (after treatment)
8

E-waste — ई-अपशिष्ट

E-waste (Electronic Waste) discarded electrical and electronic equipment (EEEE) है — which contains hazardous and valuable materials.

Examples:

  • Computers, laptops, mobile phones
  • TVs, monitors, refrigerators
  • Printers, scanners, copiers
  • Batteries, chargers
  • Wires, cables, circuit boards

Hazardous Components:

  • Lead, Mercury, Cadmium
  • Arsenic, Chromium
  • Brominated flame retardants
  • PVC plastics

Recycling/Management:

  • Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)
  • Dismantling and segregation
  • Recovery of precious metals (gold, silver, copper)
  • Safe disposal of hazardous components

Waste Management Hierarchy — अपशिष्ट प्रबंधन पदानुक्रम

Waste Management Hierarchy waste management का priority order है — from most preferred to least preferred.

Waste Hierarchy = Prevent → Reduce → Reuse → Recycle → Recover → Disposal
  • Prevention — निवारण: Avoid generating waste (most preferred)
  • Reduction — कमी: Reduce the amount of waste produced
  • Reuse — पुन:उपयोग: Use items again without processing
  • Recycle — पुनर्चक्रण: Process waste into new products
  • Recovery — पुन:प्राप्ति: Energy recovery (incineration with energy recovery)
  • Disposal — निपटान: Landfilling, incineration without recovery (least preferred)

Key Principle: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R's)

BOD, COD & DO — BOD, COD और DO

  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) — जैव-रासायनिक ऑक्सीजन माँग: Oxygen की वह मात्रा जो microorganisms organic matter को decompose करने के लिए consume करते हैं। Higher BOD = more organic pollution.
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand) — रासायनिक ऑक्सीजन माँग: Oxygen की वह मात्रा जो chemical oxidation के लिए required है। COD > BOD (because chemical oxidation is more complete).
  • DO (Dissolved Oxygen) — विघटित ऑक्सीजन: Water में dissolved oxygen की मात्रा। Higher DO = clean water. Lower DO = polluted water.

Key Relationships:

  • High BOD → Low DO → Polluted water
  • Low BOD → High DO → Clean water
  • COD > BOD (always)
11

Eutrophication — सुपोषण

Eutrophication water body में excess nutrients (especially nitrogen and phosphorus) के accumulation से algal blooms की process है — leading to oxygen depletion and death of aquatic life.

Eutrophication = Excess Nutrients → Algal Bloom → Oxygen Depletion → Death of Aquatic Life

Stages:

  • Nutrient enrichment (from fertilisers, sewage)
  • Algal bloom (excessive growth of algae)
  • Algae die and decompose
  • Decomposition consumes oxygen (BOD increases)
  • DO decreases → fish and other organisms die

Causes:

  • Agricultural runoff (fertilisers, manure)
  • Domestic sewage
  • Industrial effluents
  • Detergents (phosphates)
12

Biomagnification — जैव-आवर्धन

Biomagnification (Bioamplification) toxic substances (especially pesticides, heavy metals) का concentration increasing होता है as they move up the food chain.

Biomagnification = Increasing Toxin Concentration at Higher Trophic Levels

Key Points:

  • Starts at the lowest trophic level (producers)
  • Concentration increases at each trophic level
  • Top consumers have the highest concentration
  • DDT and Mercury are classic examples

Example (DDT):

  • Water → DDT concentration: 0.003 ppm
  • Phytoplankton → 0.04 ppm
  • Zooplankton → 0.16 ppm
  • Small fish → 0.5 ppm
  • Large fish → 2.0 ppm
  • Birds (top consumer) → 25 ppm

Difference from Bioaccumulation:

  • Bioaccumulation: Toxin accumulation within a single organism over time
  • Biomagnification: Toxin concentration increases along the food chain

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: COD = BOD → ❌ COD is always higher than BOD.
  • Trap 2: High DO = polluted water → ❌ High DO = clean water; Low DO = polluted water.
  • Trap 3: Eutrophication = good for aquatic life → ❌ Eutrophication leads to oxygen depletion and death of aquatic life.
  • Trap 4: Biomagnification = bioaccumulation → ❌ Biomagnification is along the food chain; bioaccumulation is within an organism.
  • Trap 5: Waste hierarchy = Recycle > Reduce → ❌ Reduce is higher priority than Recycle.
  • Trap 6: E-waste = non-hazardous → ❌ E-waste contains hazardous materials (lead, mercury, cadmium).
  • Trap 7: Noise pollution measured in Hertz → ❌ Measured in Decibels (dB).

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

Which of the following indicates higher organic pollution in water?

  • A. High DO
  • B. Low BOD
  • C. High BOD
  • D. Low COD
Answer: C. High BOD

Explanation: High BOD means more organic matter is being decomposed, indicating higher organic pollution.

Question 2

Eutrophication is caused primarily by:

  • A. Excess oxygen
  • B. Excess nutrients (N and P)
  • C. Excess CO₂
  • D. Excess sunlight
Answer: B. Excess nutrients (N and P)

Explanation: Eutrophication is caused by excess nutrients (nitrogen and phosphorus) from fertilisers and sewage.

Question 3

In the waste management hierarchy, which is the most preferred option?

  • A. Recycling
  • B. Disposal
  • C. Prevention
  • D. Energy recovery
Answer: C. Prevention

Explanation: Prevention (avoiding waste generation) is the most preferred option in the waste hierarchy.

Question 4

The increase in concentration of toxic substances along the food chain is called:

  • A. Bioaccumulation
  • B. Biomagnification
  • C. Eutrophication
  • D. Biodegradation
Answer: B. Biomagnification

Explanation: Biomagnification is the increase in toxin concentration at higher trophic levels along the food chain.

Question 5

Which of the following is NOT a component of biomedical waste?

  • A. Used needles
  • B. Blood samples
  • C. Municipal solid waste
  • D. Pathological waste
Answer: C. Municipal solid waste

Explanation: Municipal solid waste is household/commercial waste, not biomedical waste. Used needles, blood samples, and pathological waste are biomedical waste.

Question 6

Which hazardous metal is commonly found in e-waste?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Calcium
  • C. Lead
  • D. Iron
Answer: C. Lead

Explanation: Lead, Mercury, Cadmium, and Arsenic are hazardous metals commonly found in e-waste.
