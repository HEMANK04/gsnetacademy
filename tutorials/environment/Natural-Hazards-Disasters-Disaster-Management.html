Natural Hazards, Disasters & Disaster Management

Complete tutorial: Hazard vs Disaster, Disaster Risk, Geological Hazards, Flood, Cyclone & Drought, Earthquake & Tsunami, Landslide & Heatwave, Disaster Management Cycle, Prevention, Mitigation, Preparedness, Response, Recovery & Rehabilitation.

Hazard vs Disaster — आपदा जोखिम vs आपदा

  • Hazard — आपदा जोखिम/खतरा: A potential threat or event that could cause harm. (e.g., an earthquake fault line)
  • Disaster — आपदा: An actual event that causes widespread damage, loss, and disruption. (e.g., an earthquake that destroys a city)
HazardDisaster
Potential threatActual event
May or may not occurHas occurred
Example: A dormant volcanoExample: Volcanic eruption
Exists regardless of human presenceCauses damage to people/property
Hazard (Potential) + Vulnerability + Exposure = Disaster (Actual)
2

Disaster Risk — आपदा जोखिम

Disaster Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure

Disaster Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure
  • Hazard — आपदा जोखिम/खतरा: Natural event (earthquake, flood, cyclone)
  • Vulnerability — संवेदनशीलता/भेद्यता: How susceptible a community is to damage (poverty, poor infrastructure, lack of awareness)
  • Exposure — जोखिम/एक्सपोजर: How many people/properties are in the hazard zone

Key Point: Reducing vulnerability and exposure can reduce disaster risk even if the hazard cannot be prevented.

3

Geological Hazards — भूगर्भीय आपदा जोखिम

Geological Hazards Earth's geological processes से उत्पन्न होने वाले hazards हैं — tectonic, volcanic, mass movement.

  • Earthquake — भूकंप: Sudden shaking of the ground due to tectonic plate movement
  • Tsunami — सुनामी: Large ocean waves caused by underwater earthquakes
  • Volcano — ज्वालामुखी: Eruption of magma, ash, and gases
  • Landslide — भूस्खलन: Downward movement of rock/soil on slopes
  • Avalanche — हिमस्खलन: Rapid flow of snow/ice down a slope
4

Flood, Cyclone & Drought — बाढ़, चक्रवात एवं सूखा

  • Flood — बाढ़: Overflow of water onto normally dry land — caused by heavy rain, river overflow, coastal storm surges.
  • Cyclone — चक्रवात: Rotating storm system with low pressure — also called hurricanes/typhoons. Causes strong winds, heavy rain, storm surges.
  • Drought — सूखा: Prolonged period of abnormally low rainfall — leading to water shortage, crop failure, food insecurity.
5

Earthquake & Tsunami — भूकंप एवं सुनामी

  • Earthquake — भूकंप: Caused by tectonic plate movement (fault lines). Measured on Richter Scale (magnitude). Effects: ground shaking, building collapse, landslides, fires, tsunamis.
  • Tsunami — सुनामी: Series of large ocean waves caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, or landslides. Travel at high speed across oceans; cause massive coastal destruction.

Key Points:

  • Richter Scale measures earthquake magnitude (logarithmic: each unit = 10x increase in amplitude)
  • Earthquake early warning systems can provide precious seconds/minutes of warning
  • Tsunami warning systems monitor seismic activity and ocean buoys
6

Landslide & Heatwave — भूस्खलन एवं लू

  • Landslide — भूस्खलन: Movement of rock, earth, or debris down a slope. Causes: heavy rain, earthquakes, deforestation, construction on slopes. Effects: damage to infrastructure, loss of life, road blockages.
  • Heatwave — लू/ताप लहर: Prolonged period of excessively hot weather (temperatures above normal). Effects: heat exhaustion, heatstroke, increased mortality, crop damage, increased energy demand.
7

Disaster Management Cycle — आपदा प्रबंधन चक्र

Disaster Management Cycle disaster management के phases का continuous cycle है — from pre-disaster to during-disaster to post-disaster.

Disaster Management Cycle = Prevention → Mitigation → Preparedness → Response → Recovery → Rehabilitation

Three Phases:

  • Pre-Disaster: Prevention, Mitigation, Preparedness (before the disaster)
  • During-Disaster: Response (during the disaster)
  • Post-Disaster: Recovery, Rehabilitation (after the disaster)
8

Prevention — रोकथाम

Prevention disasters को completely avoid करने की कोशिश है — eliminating the hazard or stopping it from occurring.

Prevention = Stop the Disaster from Happening

Examples:

  • Building dams to prevent floods
  • Stopping construction in high-risk zones
  • Enforcing building codes in seismic zones
  • Protecting forests to prevent landslides

Note: Complete prevention is not always possible for natural hazards (earthquakes, cyclones).

9

Mitigation — शमन

Mitigation disaster के impacts को reduce करना है — even if the hazard cannot be prevented.

Mitigation = Reduce the Impact of the Disaster

Examples:

  • Earthquake-resistant building design
  • Flood shelters and embankments
  • Cyclone-resistant housing
  • Drought-resistant crops
  • Afforestation to prevent landslides
  • Building codes and land-use planning
10

Preparedness — तैयारी

Preparedness disaster से पहले pre-planning and preparation है — to ensure effective response when a disaster strikes.

Preparedness = Be Ready Before the Disaster

Examples:

  • Early warning systems
  • Emergency evacuation plans and drills
  • Stockpiling emergency supplies (food, water, medicine)
  • Training for first responders (police, fire, medical)
  • Public awareness campaigns
  • Emergency communication systems
11

Response — प्रतिक्रिया

Response disaster के during and immediately after की actions हैं — saving lives, providing emergency assistance.

Response = Immediate Actions During/After Disaster

Examples:

  • Search and rescue operations
  • Medical assistance (first aid, hospitals)
  • Evacuation and shelter provision
  • Food, water, and supply distribution
  • Emergency communication
  • Damage assessment (initial)
  • Coordination of relief efforts
12

Recovery & Rehabilitation — पुनर्वास एवं पुनर्निर्माण

  • Recovery — पुनर्प्राप्ति: Affected communities को return to normalcy लाना — restoring essential services, infrastructure, economy.
  • Rehabilitation — पुनर्वास: Long-term rebuilding — houses, infrastructure, livelihoods, psychological support.

Examples:

  • Rebuilding damaged houses, schools, hospitals
  • Restoring roads, bridges, power, water supply
  • Economic recovery (jobs, agriculture)
  • Psychological counselling
  • Building back better (using mitigation measures)
  • Community rebuilding and social support

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: Hazard and disaster are the same → ❌ Hazard = potential; Disaster = actual event.
  • Trap 2: Disaster Risk = Hazard only → ❌ Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure.
  • Trap 3: Prevention = Mitigation → ❌ Prevention = avoid; Mitigation = reduce impact.
  • Trap 4: Preparedness = Response → ❌ Preparedness is before; Response is during/after.
  • Trap 5: Recovery = Rehabilitation → ❌ Recovery = return to normal; Rehabilitation = long-term rebuild.
  • Trap 6: Mitigation happens after the disaster → ❌ Mitigation is pre-disaster (reducing future impact).
  • Trap 7: Response happens after recovery → ❌ Response is during/immediately after; Recovery comes after response.

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

An earthquake fault line is an example of:

  • A. Disaster
  • B. Hazard
  • C. Response
  • D. Recovery
Answer: B. Hazard

Explanation: An earthquake fault line is a potential threat (hazard), not an actual event (disaster).

Question 2

Disaster Risk = Hazard × _____ × Exposure

  • A. Vulnerability
  • B. Response
  • C. Recovery
  • D. Preparedness
Answer: A. Vulnerability

Explanation: Disaster Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure.

Question 3

Building earthquake-resistant buildings is an example of:

  • A. Prevention
  • B. Mitigation
  • C. Response
  • D. Recovery
Answer: B. Mitigation

Explanation: Earthquake-resistant buildings reduce the impact of earthquakes — this is mitigation (pre-disaster).

Question 4

Search and rescue operations during a disaster are part of:

  • A. Preparedness
  • B. Mitigation
  • C. Response
  • D. Rehabilitation
Answer: C. Response

Explanation: Search and rescue is an immediate action taken during/after a disaster — response phase.

Question 5

Which phase involves rebuilding houses and restoring livelihoods after a disaster?

  • A. Prevention
  • B. Preparedness
  • C. Response
  • D. Rehabilitation
Answer: D. Rehabilitation

Explanation: Rehabilitation is the long-term rebuilding phase after a disaster — houses, livelihoods, infrastructure.

Question 6

An early warning system for cyclones is part of:

  • A. Prevention
  • B. Preparedness
  • C. Mitigation
  • D. Recovery
Answer: B. Preparedness

Explanation: Early warning systems help communities prepare for an impending disaster — preparedness phase.

One-Page Revision — एक पेज में पुनरावृत्ति

Hazard = Potential | Disaster = Actual Event
Disaster Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure

Types of Hazards:

  • Geological: Earthquake, Tsunami, Volcano, Landslide
  • Hydrological: Flood, Cyclone, Storm surge
  • Climatic: Drought, Heatwave, Cold wave

Disaster Management Cycle:

  • Prevention → Mitigation → Preparedness → Response → Recovery → Rehabilitation

Pre-Disaster:

  • Prevention (avoid), Mitigation (reduce impact), Preparedness (plan/train)

During-Disaster:

  • Response (save lives, emergency assistance)

Post-Disaster:

  • Recovery (restore normalcy), Rehabilitation (long-term rebuild)

Key Measures:

  • Earthquake: Seismic-resistant buildings, early warning
  • Flood: Dams, embankments, early warning
  • Cyclone: Shelters, early warning, evacuation
  • Drought: Water conservation, drought-resistant crops
  • Landslide: Afforestation, slope stabilisation
  • Heatwave: Early warning, cooling centres

Exam Formula:

Disaster Management = Pre-Disaster + During-Disaster + Post-Disaster
