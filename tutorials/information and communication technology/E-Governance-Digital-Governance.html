E-Governance — ई-गवर्नेंस

E-Governance = Government services में ICT का उपयोग करके administration, service delivery, transparency, participation और accountability को improve करना।

E-Governance = ICT + Public Services + Transparency + Accountability

Key Concepts:

  • E-Governance: Use of ICT in government processes and service delivery
  • Digital Governance: Broader concept including digital infrastructure, policies, and citizen engagement

Objectives: Faster services, Transparency, Reduced paperwork, Better record management, Citizen participation, Reduced transaction costs, Monitoring and accountability, Wider accessibility

2

Types of E-Governance Interaction — G2C, G2G, G2B, G2E

  • G2C (Government to Citizen): Services from government to citizens. Examples: Online certificates, tax services, passport services, utility bill payments.
  • G2G (Government to Government): Inter-departmental and inter-government data exchange. Examples: Record sharing between ministries, central-state coordination.
  • G2B (Government to Business): Services from government to businesses. Examples: E-tendering, online licences, GST registration, company registration.
  • G2E (Government to Employee): Services from government to employees. Examples: Payroll, service portal, attendance, leave management, pension.

Governance Models — गवर्नेंस मॉडल

  • Information Model — सूचना मॉडल: Government information online उपलब्ध कराना। Citizens can access information without interaction.
  • Interaction Model — अंतःक्रिया मॉडल: Citizen feedback, queries and communication. Two-way communication.
  • Transaction Model — लेन-देन मॉडल: Online payment, application, certificate or licence. Complete transactions online.
  • Transformation Model — परिवर्तन मॉडल: Departments और services का integrated digital restructuring. Full digital transformation.
  • Participation Model — भागीदारी मॉडल: Online consultation and citizen involvement in governance. Participatory decision-making.

Good Digital Governance Principles — सुशासन के सिद्धांत

  • Transparency — पारदर्शिता: Open and accessible information
  • Accountability — उत्तरदायित्व: Responsibility for actions and decisions
  • Accessibility — पहुँच: Services available to all citizens
  • Responsiveness — प्रतिक्रियाशीलता: Timely response to citizen needs
  • Inclusion — समावेश: All citizens included regardless of digital literacy
  • Efficiency — दक्षता: Cost-effective and streamlined processes
  • Security — सुरक्षा: Data and system protection
  • Privacy — गोपनीयता: Protection of personal information
  • Interoperability — अंतर-संचालनीयता: Systems work together seamlessly
  • Reliability — विश्वसनीयता: Consistent and dependable services
  • Citizen-centricity — नागरिक-केंद्रित: Focus on citizen needs
  • Grievance Redressal — शिकायत निवारण: Effective complaint handling

Digital India — डिजिटल इंडिया

Digital India: Digitally empowered society and knowledge economy के लिए flagship programme।

Vision Areas:

  • Digital Infrastructure as a Utility: High-speed Internet, digital identity, mobile phones
  • Governance and Services on Demand: Integrated services, digital documents, cashless transactions
  • Digital Empowerment of Citizens: Digital literacy, universal access to digital resources

Key Initiatives under Digital India: DigiLocker, MyGov, UMANG, BharatNet, Common Service Centres, Aadhaar, e-Office, GeM

8

UMANG — UMANG

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance): एक unified application जो multiple government services को एक platform पर उपलब्ध कराता है।

  • Citizens can access various government services through one app
  • Single sign-on for multiple services
  • Available on mobile and web
  • Services: Aadhaar, passport, PAN, EPFO, etc.
9

DigiLocker — डिजीलॉकर

DigiLocker: Personal digital documents और certificates को store और access करने की platform।

  • 10 GB free storage
  • Issued documents are legally valid
  • Shareable and verifiable
  • Eliminates physical documents
  • Integrated with Aadhaar
10

Aadhaar — आधार

Aadhaar: India's digital identity/authentication framework — 12-digit unique identity number.

  • Biometric and demographic data
  • Identity verification and authentication
  • Used for: subsidies (DBT), bank accounts, SIM cards, passport, etc.
  • UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India) manages Aadhaar
11

GeM (Government e-Marketplace) — GeM

GeM: Government procurement और selling के लिए online platform.

  • Transparent and efficient public procurement
  • Direct purchase and bidding
  • Multiple categories of products and services
  • Reduces manual procurement delays
12

e-Office — ई-ऑफिस

e-Office: Digital government-office processes को implement करने वाली initiative।

  • Paperless office
  • Digital file movement
  • Electronic records management
  • Improved efficiency and transparency
13

MyGov — मायगोव

MyGov: Citizen engagement and participation platform — citizens और government के बीच सीधा interaction।

  • Online discussions
  • Citizen feedback and suggestions
  • Idea sharing and innovation
  • Volunteering opportunities
14

BharatNet — भारतनेट

BharatNet: Broadband connectivity infrastructure for rural India.

  • High-speed optical fibre network
  • Connecting gram panchayats
  • Bridging the digital divide
  • Enabling digital services in rural areas
15

Common Service Centres (CSC) — CSC

Common Service Centres (CSC): Assisted digital public services के लिए access points.

  • Digital service delivery in rural/remote areas
  • CSC operators provide assisted access
  • Services: certificates, banking, insurance, utility payments, etc.
  • Important for digital inclusion
16

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) — DBT

Direct Benefit Transfer: Subsidies और benefits को directly bank accounts में transfer करना।

  • Reduces leakages and corruption
  • Using Aadhaar for identification
  • Real-time transfer
  • Examples: LPG subsidy, scholarships, welfare schemes
17

e-Hospital — ई-हॉस्पिटल

e-Hospital: Digital hospital-related services के लिए initiative।

  • Online appointment booking
  • Patient records management
  • Digital prescriptions
  • Hospital management system
18

GSTN (Goods and Services Tax Network) — GSTN

GSTN: Digital GST-related infrastructure — GST registration, return filing, and compliance platform.

  • Centralised IT platform for GST
  • Connects taxpayers, states, and central government
  • Returns, invoices, payments
19

National Scholarship Portal — National Scholarship Portal

National Scholarship Portal: Scholarship services की unified platform — application, processing और disbursement के लिए।

  • Multiple scholarships under one portal
  • Online application and tracking
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Transparent process

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: E-Governance = only citizen services → ❌ It includes G2G, G2B, and G2E also.
  • Trap 2: ICT Governance = E-Governance → ❌ ICT Governance is broader organisational concept; E-Governance is about government services.
  • Trap 3: G2C = Government to Business → ❌ G2C is Government to Citizen; G2B is Government to Business.
  • Trap 4: Aadhaar = only identity card → ❌ Aadhaar is a digital identity/authentication framework.
  • Trap 5: DigiLocker = only document storage → ❌ It includes document sharing and verification also.
  • Trap 6: UMANG = only one service → ❌ UMANG is a unified platform for multiple services.
  • Trap 7: BharatNet = urban connectivity → ❌ BharatNet is for rural broadband connectivity.

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

Citizen applying online for a certificate is an example of:

  • A. G2G
  • B. G2C
  • C. G2B
  • D. G2E
Answer: B. G2C

Explanation: G2C (Government to Citizen) involves services from government to citizens.

Question 2

Which initiative provides a unified platform for multiple government services?

  • A. DigiLocker
  • B. UMANG
  • C. MyGov
  • D. GeM
Answer: B. UMANG

Explanation: UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) provides access to multiple government services.

Question 3

Which initiative is associated with digital documents and certificates?

  • A. Aadhaar
  • B. DigiLocker
  • C. GeM
  • D. BharatNet
Answer: B. DigiLocker

Explanation: DigiLocker provides storage and access to personal digital documents and certificates.

Question 4

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is associated with:

  • A. Online education
  • B. Government procurement
  • C. Digital identity
  • D. Broadband connectivity
Answer: B. Government procurement

Explanation: GeM is a platform for transparent and efficient government procurement.

Question 5

Which initiative provides broadband connectivity to rural areas?

  • A. UMANG
  • B. BharatNet
  • C. MyGov
  • D. e-Office
Answer: B. BharatNet

Explanation: BharatNet provides broadband connectivity infrastructure to rural gram panchayats.

Question 6

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) is associated with:

  • A. Online education
  • B. Transferring subsidies directly to bank accounts
  • C. Digital identity
  • D. Government procurement
Answer: B. Transferring subsidies directly to bank accounts

Explanation: DBT transfers subsidies and benefits directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts, reducing leakages.
