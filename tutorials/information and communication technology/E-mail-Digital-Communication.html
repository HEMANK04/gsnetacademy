E-mail & Digital Communication

Complete tutorial: E-mail, digital communication, e-mail protocols (SMTP, POP3, IMAP), CC & BCC, e-mail etiquette, video conferencing, synchronous/asynchronous communication, VoIP, instant messaging.

E-mail — ईमेल

E-mail (Electronic Mail) digital messages को network के माध्यम से send/receive करने की system है।

E-mail = Electronic Message + Network + Recipient

Key Features:

  • Text messages with attachments
  • Global reach
  • Asynchronous communication
  • Can be stored and forwarded
  • Multiple recipients possible (To, CC, BCC)

Advantages: Fast, economical, permanent record, attachments, global reach

Limitations: No immediate feedback, spam, phishing, attachment size limits

2

E-mail Structure — ईमेल संरचना

  • To — मुख्य प्राप्तकर्ता: Primary recipient(s) of the email.
  • CC (Carbon Copy) — कार्बन कॉपी: Additional recipients whose addresses are visible to all.
  • BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) — ब्लाइंड कार्बन कॉपी: Additional recipients whose addresses are not visible to other recipients.
  • Subject — विषय: Concise summary of the email's purpose.
  • Body — मुख्य भाग: The main content of the email.
  • Attachment — संलग्नक: File(s) sent along with the email.
  • Signature — हस्ताक्षर: Sender's identification/contact information (automated).
3

CC & BCC — CC एवं BCC

  • CC (Carbon Copy):
    • Recipients are visible to all other recipients
    • Used when everyone should know who received the email
    • Example: Project team members
  • BCC (Blind Carbon Copy):
    • Recipients are hidden from other recipients
    • Used to protect privacy or when recipients don't need to see each other
    • Example: Sending a bulk email to many people

Exam Point: BCC recipients are hidden from all other recipients, including those in To and CC.

4

E-mail Protocols — ईमेल प्रोटोकॉल

  • SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol): Used for sending e-mail. Port 25.
  • POP3 (Post Office Protocol Version 3): Used for receiving/downloading e-mail from server to client. Port 110.
  • IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol): Used for accessing and synchronising e-mail on the server. Port 143.
SMTP Sends | POP3 Pulls/Downloads | IMAP Synchronises
5

SMTP — SMTP

SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol): Protocol for sending e-mail from client to server and between servers.

  • Port: 25 (default)
  • Function: Outgoing mail transfer
  • Direction: Sender → Server → Recipient's Server
  • Works with: POP3 or IMAP for receiving

Key Point: SMTP is used only for sending e-mail, not for receiving.

6

POP3 — POP3

POP3 (Post Office Protocol Version 3): Protocol for downloading/retrieving e-mail from server to client.

  • Port: 110
  • Function: Mail retrieval and download
  • Direction: Server → Client
  • Default behavior: Downloads and removes from server (can be configured to keep a copy)
  • Best for: Single device access; offline access

Limitation: Limited synchronisation across multiple devices.

7

IMAP — IMAP

IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol): Protocol for accessing and managing e-mail on the server.

  • Port: 143
  • Function: Mail management and synchronisation
  • Direction: Server → Client (with server storage)
  • Keeps messages on server — syncs across multiple devices
  • Best for: Multiple device access; synchronisation

Advantage: Same inbox, folders, and read/unread status across all devices.

8

Exam Point: IMAP is preferred when using multiple devices; POP3 is suitable for single-device offline access.

E-mail Etiquette — ईमेल शिष्टाचार

  • Clear Subject Line: Use descriptive, concise subjects.
  • Professional Greeting & Closing: Formal greeting and sign-off.
  • Use of CC & BCC Appropriately: Use CC for transparency, BCC for privacy.
  • Reply All: Use only when necessary.
  • Concise and Clear Language: Be brief and to the point.
  • Avoid ALL CAPS: It is considered shouting.
  • Check Spelling & Grammar: Proofread before sending.
  • Avoid Excessive Attachments: Compress or link large files.
  • Timely Response: Reply within a reasonable time.
  • Professional Tone: Maintain professionalism even in casual communication.
10

Digital Communication — डिजिटल संचार

Digital Communication digital technologies के माध्यम से information, ideas और messages का exchange है।

Forms of Digital Communication:

  • E-mail: Asynchronous written messages
  • Instant Messaging: Real-time text chat (WhatsApp, Telegram)
  • Video Conferencing: Real-time audio-visual communication (Zoom, Meet)
  • Voice over IP (VoIP): Internet-based voice calls (Skype)
  • Social Media: Public/semi-public communication platforms
  • Discussion Forums: Asynchronous group discussions
  • Collaboration Tools: Shared documents and workspaces
11

Synchronous vs Asynchronous — समकालिक vs असमकालिक

  • Synchronous Communication — समकालिक: Participants interact in real-time. Examples: Live video call, chat, webinar, phone call.
  • Asynchronous Communication — असमकालिक: Participants do NOT need to be present at the same time. Examples: E-mail, recorded lecture, discussion forum, SMS.

Exam Point: Video conferencing = synchronous; E-mail = asynchronous.

12

Video Conferencing — वीडियो कॉन्फ्रेंसिंग

Video Conferencing: Real-time audio and visual communication between two or more participants in different locations.

Examples: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Cisco Webex, Skype.

Essential Requirements:

  • Computer/mobile device
  • Microphone and speaker/headphones
  • Camera (webcam)
  • Stable Internet connection
  • Conferencing software/platform

Advantages in Education:

  • Remote learning and teaching
  • Guest lectures from anywhere
  • Collaboration and group work
  • Screen sharing and demonstration
  • Recording for revision

Limitations:

  • Bandwidth issues
  • Digital divide
  • Screen fatigue
  • Reduced non-verbal cues
  • Privacy concerns
13

VoIP — VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol)

VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol): Internet-based voice calls using IP networks instead of traditional telephone lines.

  • Examples: Skype, WhatsApp calls, Google Voice, Zoom audio
  • Function: Converts voice to digital packets and transmits over the Internet
  • Advantages: Lower cost, flexibility, additional features (video, conferencing)
  • Protocol: SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) commonly used
14

Instant Messaging — इंस्टेंट मैसेजिंग

Instant Messaging (IM): Real-time text communication between users over the Internet.

Examples: WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Facebook Messenger, Slack, Google Chat.

Features:

  • Real-time text messages
  • File sharing
  • Group chats
  • Voice and video calls
  • End-to-end encryption (some platforms)

Comparison with E-mail:

  • IM = Synchronous (real-time)
  • E-mail = Asynchronous (delayed)
15

Collaboration Tools — सहयोग उपकरण

Collaboration Tools teams को एक साथ काम करने, documents share करने और communicate करने की सुविधा देते हैं।

  • Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Meet
  • Microsoft 365: Word Online, Excel Online, Teams, OneDrive
  • Slack: Team communication and collaboration
  • Notion: Note-taking and project management
  • Trello: Project management and task tracking
  • Zoom/Meet: Video conferencing
16

Digital Communication Ethics — डिजिटल संचार नैतिकता

  • Privacy: Respect others' privacy; don't share personal information without consent.
  • Consent: Obtain consent before adding to groups or sharing content.
  • Respectful Language: Use polite and respectful language.
  • Cyberbullying: Never engage in or encourage harassment.
  • Fake Information: Verify before sharing; avoid spreading misinformation.
  • Professional Conduct: Maintain professional behaviour in work/educational contexts.
  • Data Protection: Secure sensitive information shared digitally.
  • Intellectual Property: Respect copyright and give credit for shared content.
17

Common Exam Traps — सामान्य परीक्षा जाल

  • Trap 1: SMTP = receiving e-mail → ❌ SMTP sends e-mail; POP3/IMAP retrieve/access it.
  • Trap 2: POP3 = syncs across devices → ❌ IMAP syncs; POP3 downloads to one device.
  • Trap 3: IMAP = downloads and removes from server → ❌ IMAP keeps messages on server; POP3 often removes.
  • Trap 4: BCC recipients are visible to CC recipients → ❌ BCC recipients are hidden from everyone.
  • Trap 5: Video conferencing = asynchronous → ❌ Video conferencing is synchronous.
  • Trap 6: E-mail = synchronous → ❌ E-mail is asynchronous.
  • Trap 7: VoIP = video calling → ❌ VoIP is primarily voice; can include video but not necessarily.

Practice Questions — अभ्यास प्रश्न

Question 1

Which protocol is used to send e-mail?

  • A. POP3
  • B. IMAP
  • C. SMTP
  • D. FTP
Answer: C. SMTP

Explanation: SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is used for sending e-mail.

Question 2

Which protocol is best for accessing e-mail across multiple devices with full synchronisation?

  • A. POP3
  • B. IMAP
  • C. SMTP
  • D. HTTP
Answer: B. IMAP

Explanation: IMAP keeps messages on the server and synchronises across all devices.

Question 3

In an e-mail, BCC recipients are:

  • A. Visible to all recipients
  • B. Visible only to CC recipients
  • C. Hidden from all other recipients
  • D. Visible only to the sender
Answer: C. Hidden from all other recipients

Explanation: BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) recipients are hidden from all other recipients.

Question 4

Which of the following is an example of synchronous communication?

  • A. E-mail
  • B. Recorded lecture
  • C. Video conferencing
  • D. Discussion forum
Answer: C. Video conferencing

Explanation: Video conferencing is real-time (synchronous) communication. E-mail, recorded lectures, and discussion forums are asynchronous.

Question 5

What is the default port for SMTP?

  • A. 25
  • B. 110
  • C. 143
  • D. 80
Answer: A. 25

Explanation: SMTP uses port 25. POP3 uses 110, IMAP uses 143, HTTP uses 80.

Question 6

Which of the following is an example of VoIP service?

  • A. E-mail
  • B. WhatsApp call
  • C. SMS
  • D. Discussion forum
Answer: B. WhatsApp call

Explanation: VoIP (Voice over IP) services include WhatsApp calls, Skype, Google Voice, etc. E-mail, SMS, and forums are not VoIP.
