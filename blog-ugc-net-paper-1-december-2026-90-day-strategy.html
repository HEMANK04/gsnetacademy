UGC NET Paper-1 December 2026:90-Day Complete Preparation Strategy

A practical, week-wise plan to help you complete Paper-1 & practice enough mocks to crack UGC NET with a top score. Follow consistently – Success is certain!

26 Aug 2026 12 min readBy Er. R.P. Singh
Target December 2026?This 90-day plan will help you complete Paper-1 & practice enough mocks to crack UGC NET with a top score. Follow consistently — Success is certain!

UGC NET is one of the most trusted exams for aspiring Assistant Professors and JRF researchers in India. Paper-1 is qualifying in nature but scoring well here gives you a huge advantage in the final merit. If you are targeting UGC NET December 2026, the next 90 days are the golden period to build concepts, improve accuracy, and strengthen your exam temperament.

Here's a step-by-step, reliable and proven 90-day strategy to help you crack Paper-1 with confidence.

01

Understand the Exam First

A clear understanding of the format is crucial to build a winning strategy:

Total Questions

50 MCQs (All Compulsory)

Total Marks

100 Marks

Duration

3 Hours (with Paper 2)

Mode

Computer Based Test (CBT)

  • Marking Scheme: 2 marks per question, No negative marking.
  • Language: English and Hindi
02

Know the Syllabus Areas

Paper-1 covers 10 core units. The recent trends show that NTA is emphasizing conceptual clarity and analytical skills over rote memorization. The units are:

Teaching Aptitude
Research Aptitude
Comprehension
Communication
Mathematical Reasoning & Aptitude
Logical Reasoning
Data Interpretation
Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
People, Development & Environment
Higher Education System
03

90-Day Preparation Timeline

A phased approach is the most effective way to cover the vast syllabus and ensure proper revision.

Days 1–30

Foundation Phase

  • Focus on building core concepts for all 10 units
  • Understand concepts thoroughly
  • Make short, crisp, point-based notes

Days 31–60

Practice Phase

  • Complete the syllabus
  • Start solving subject-wise questions
  • Take sectional tests to improve speed and accuracy

Days 61–90

Revision + Mock Phase

  • Spend this time exclusively on full-length mock tests
  • Analyze your mistakes
  • Focus on your weak areas
04

Daily Study Plan (6–7 Hours)

A consistent 6–7 hour routine is essential to cover both Paper 1 and your main subject (Paper 2). It is crucial to prepare both papers simultaneously.

Theory Study – 3 hrs
Practice – 2 hrs
Revision – 1 hr
  • Theory Study (3 hours): Dive deep into concepts. Give priority to units with high weightage like Teaching and Research Aptitude. Pay special attention to assertion-reasoning questions.
  • Practice (2 hours): Tackle 15–20 MCQs per unit and practice at least 10 data interpretation sets daily to master calculative questions.
  • Revision (1 hour): Dedicate the last hour to reviewing what you learned that day to ensure long-term memory retention.
05

Key Preparation Tips

  • Use Authentic Study Material: Stick to one or two standard books for theory.
  • Practice PYQs: Attempt previous year questions to get a feel for the exam's difficulty and pattern.
  • Learn the Pomodoro Technique: Use a 50-minute study cycle followed by a 10-minute break to maintain focus during extended study sessions.
  • Stay Consistent: Avoid unnecessary sources and stick to one plan.
06

Recommended Book: UGC NET Paper-1

“NTA UGC NET Paper 1: Teaching and Research Aptitude”

by K.V.S. Madaan

For a comprehensive guide covering the complete syllabus. This book is known for its clear explanations of complex topics and includes plenty of practice questions to solidify your concepts.

?Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. Is 3 months enough for UGC NET Paper-1?

    Yes. Paper-1 has a fixed and limited syllabus of 10 units. With a consistent 6–7 hour daily routine split across theory, practice and revision, 90 days is enough to cover every unit and still get a full revision plus mock phase.

  • 2. How many mock tests should I attempt?

    Days 61–90 should be spent mainly on full-length mocks. Attempt them regularly, and more importantly analyse every mock — your mistakes tell you exactly which units still need work.

  • 3. Which topics are scoring in Paper-1?

    Teaching Aptitude and Research Aptitude carry high weightage and are conceptual, so they reward understanding. Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning and Mathematical Reasoning are practice-based and become almost guaranteed marks once you solve them daily.

  • 4. Is there negative marking in Paper-1?

    No. Every correct answer gives +2 marks and there is no negative marking, so you should attempt all 50 questions.
