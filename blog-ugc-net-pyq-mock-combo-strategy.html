UGC NET Paper-1 PYQ + Mock Combo Strategy

PYQ aur Mock dono ko milakar use karne se preparation next level pe chali jaati hai. Yeh proven combo strategy hai.

10 Sep 2026 13 min readBy Dr. Shardool Sir
PYQ + Mock = Best ComboPYQ aapko batate hain ki kya poocha jaata hai. Mocks batate hain ki aap kitna perform kar rahe ho. Dono ko milakar use karne se preparation mein 2x improvement aati hai.

UGC NET Paper-1 ki preparation mein do sabse powerful tools hain — PYQs (Previous Year Questions) aur Mock Tests. Zyadatar aspirants inhe alag-alag use karte hain. Lekin asli power inke combination mein hai.

Is guide mein hum aapko ek complete PYQ + Mock Combo Strategy de rahe hain — PYQ kaise use karein, mock kaise integrate karein, aur dono se milkar maximum improvement kaise nikalein. Yeh wahi approach hai jisse GS Net Academy ke students ne last 60 din mein apna score significantly improve kiya hai.

01

Why This Combo Works?

PYQ aur Mock dono ke apne faayde hain — jab dono saath use hote hain toh results multiply ho jaate hain.

PYQ Se Kya Milta Hai

  • Exam pattern clear hota hai
  • Important topics identify hote hain
  • Question framing style samajh aata hai
  • Repetition trend pata chalta hai

Mock Se Kya Milta Hai

  • Real exam jaisa pressure handle karna
  • Speed aur accuracy check hoti hai
  • Time management improve hota hai
  • Weak areas reveal hote hain
02

PYQ Use Karne Ka Sahi Tarika

PYQ sirf solve karna kaafi nahi. Unka systematic use zaroori hai. Yeh 4-step method follow karo:

Step 1: Topic-Wise Solve

Days 1–15

  • Har unit ke last 10 saal ke PYQs solve karo
  • Repeated topics highlight karo
  • Ek PYQ notebook maintain karo

Step 2: PYQ Error Log

Days 16–25

  • PYQ mein galat hue questions alag note karo
  • Har galti ka reason likho
  • Ye weak areas ka master list ban jaata hai

Step 3: Mock Integration

Days 26–45

  • Alternate day full-length mock
  • Mock mein bhi wahi PYQ patterns dikhenge
  • Mock analysis mein PYQ pattern match karo

Step 4: PYQ + Mock Final Round

Days 46–60

  • Sirf error log + PYQ notes revise
  • Last 10 mocks — full syllabus
  • Repeated PYQ patterns pe extra focus
03

Weekly Combo Plan

Har week mein PYQ aur Mock ka balance aise rakho:

Monday

Topic-wise PYQ (30 Q)

Tuesday

PYQ analysis + Notes update

Wednesday

Full-length Mock

Thursday

Mock analysis (2 hours)

Friday

Weak units pe PYQ practice

Saturday

Sectional mock + PYQ revision

Sunday

Rest + Error log revision

Weekly Target

150 PYQs + 2 Mocks

Monthly Target

600 PYQs + 8 Mocks

04

PYQ Analysis — Kya Dhoondhna Hai

PYQ solve karne se zyada important hai unka analysis. Yeh cheezein dhoondho:

Pattern Analysis

  • Kaunsa unit har saal aata hai
  • Kaunsa topic repeat hota hai
  • Question framing style

Content Analysis

  • Difficulty level ka trend
  • Naye topics ka addition
  • Current affairs ka weightage
05

PYQ Aur Mock Ko Integrate Kaise Karein

Dono ko alag-alag mat dekho. Yeh integration rules follow karo:

Rule 1

Mock ke 70% questions PYQ-style hote hain — dono ek jaise treat karo

Rule 2

Mock analysis mein PYQ pattern match karo — kitne familiar the

Rule 3

PYQ mein galat hone wala topic, mock mein bhi galat hota hai

Rule 4

Ek common error log banao — PYQ + Mock dono ka

Rule 5

Weekly ek "PYQ + Mock review" session rakho

Rule 6

Last week mein sirf PYQ + Error log revise karo

06

Common Mistakes

PYQ Mistakes

  • PYQ ratna, samajhna nahi
  • Sirf recent years dekhna, purane skip karna
  • PYQ analysis skip karna

Combo Mistakes

  • PYQ aur Mock ko alag-alag dekhna
  • Ek common error log na banana
  • Weekly review skip karna

?Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. Kitne saal ke PYQ solve karne chahiye?

    Kam se kam 10 saal ke PYQs. Purane PYQs bhi important hain — kuch topics repeat hote hain. Recent 5 saal ke PYQs sabse zyada valuable hain.

  • 2. PYQ pehle ya Mock pehle?

    Pehle topic-wise PYQ solve karo (concept build karne ke liye), phir mock do (performance check karne ke liye). Dono parallel chalte rehne chahiye.

  • 3. Ek din mein kitne PYQ solve karne chahiye?

    Daily 30 PYQs minimum. Mock day pe kam kar do. Weekly target rakho — 150 PYQs + 2 Mocks. Yeh combo ka best ratio hai.

  • 4. Kya PYQ ratna chahiye?

    Bilkul nahi. PYQ se concept samjho, ratna nahi. Kyunki NTA har saal naye angles se questions deta hai. Concept clear hoga toh naya question bhi solve ho jaayega.
