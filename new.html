Syllabus

Official UPSESSC General Knowledge Syllabus — Assistant Professor (Higher Education), Compulsory for All Disciplines

6 units — कुल 30 questions.

01

Current Affairs

Major events & Current Affairs (National/International), Personalities in News, Sports News, Current Research in the field of Science.

02

Teaching and Research Aptitude

Teaching: Nature, Objectives, Need, Methods, Evaluation & Factors Affecting Teaching; Research: Meaning & Methods; Data: Source, Collection & Analysis; Role of UGC in Qualitative Development in Higher Education.

03

Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

ICT: Meaning, Advantages & Disadvantages; Basics of Internet & E-mailing; General Abbreviation and Terminology.

04

People and Environment

People & Environment Interaction, Environmental Depredation: Cause & Solution, National & International affairs for better Environment, Environmental Control.

05

Indian History and Geography

Salient Features of Indian Culture, Indian National Movement (1857-1950), Origin of Universe & Solar System, Indian Geography (General).

06

Indian Constitution and Economy

Preamble, Fundamental Rights & Directive Principles; Indian Political System: Legislative, Executive & Judiciary; Election Commission & Public Service Commission; Population, Poverty, Unemployment, Planning & Development, Income Tax; Agriculture, Industry, Trade, Money, Currency, Banking & Capital Market.
