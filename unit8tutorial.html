ICT का अर्थ

Information and Communication Technology (ICT) refers to technologies used for collecting, processing, storing, retrieving and communicating information.

सूचना एवं संचार प्रौद्योगिकी उन technologies का समूह है जिनका उपयोग information को collect, process, store, retrieve और transmit करने के लिए किया जाता है।

Master Formula
ICT = Information Processing + Digital Storage + Communication Network
  • ICT के Components
  • Computer hardware
  • Software
  • Internet and networks
  • Telecommunication
  • Audio-video technology
  • Digital platforms
  • Cloud services
  • Database systems
  • Mobile communication
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Educational Example

जब teacher Google Meet पर live class लेता है, LMS पर notes upload करता है और online quiz से assessment करता है, तो वह ICT-enabled teaching कर रहा है।

Types of Computer Networks

  • PAN — Personal Area Network

एक व्यक्ति के आसपास छोटी range का network।

Example: Smartphone और wireless earbuds का Bluetooth connection।

  • LAN — Local Area Network

छोटे geographical area में network।

Example: Computer laboratory or university department.

  • MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

City या metropolitan area तक फैला network।

  • WAN — Wide Area Network

Large geographical area covering countries or continents.

Example: Internet.

  • Range Formula
  • PAN < LAN < MAN < WAN

Internet, Intranet and Extranet

  • Internet

A global public network of interconnected networks.

Internet = Network of Networks
  • Intranet

Internet technologies पर आधारित private organisational network।

Example: University का internal faculty portal.

  • Extranet

Controlled private network in which selected outsiders are also given authorised access.

Example: University portal accessed by authorised vendors or collaborating institutions.

BasisInternetIntranetExtranet

OwnershipNo single ownerOrganisationOrganisation/partners
SecurityRelatively less controlledMore controlledControlled access
ScopeGlobalOrganisationCollaborating groups
ExamplePublic websiteEmployee portalSupplier/collaboration portal
Recall

Internet = Everyone Intranet = Employees/Internal members Extranet = Employees + Authorised outsiders

Bandwidth and Related Terms

  • Bandwidth

A communication channel की theoretical data-carrying capacity.

  • Data Transfer Rate

Actual data transferred per unit time.

  • Latency

Data को source से destination तक पहुँचने में लगने वाला delay.

  • Jitter

Packet delay में variation.

  • Video-conferencing Example
Low bandwidth → low video quality
High latency → response delay
High jitter → broken or irregular audio
Packet loss → missing audio/video segments
  • Exam Trap

High bandwidth alone does not guarantee perfect video conferencing; latency, jitter, congestion and device performance भी relevant हैं।

Common Exam Traps

Internet and WWW are not synonymous.

Internet is public; intranet is privately controlled.

Extranet allows selected external users.

SMTP sends e-mail; POP3/IMAP retrieve or access it.

BCC recipients are hidden from other recipients.

HTTPS adds encryption; it does not guarantee that every website is trustworthy.

RAM is volatile; ROM is non-volatile.

Google is a search engine; Chrome is a browser.

SWAYAM is an online course platform; SWAYAM PRABHA is DTH-based.

NDLI is a digital library; it is not the thesis-only repository.

Shodhganga contains theses; Shodhgangotri contains research synopses.

MOOCs may be credit or non-credit.

Blended learning is not merely using PowerPoint in a classroom.

Video conferencing is synchronous; recorded lecture is asynchronous.

Digital availability does not automatically ensure digital inclusion.

ICT governance includes alignment, security, accountability and resource use—not only hardware purchase.

Virus usually requires a host; worm can self-replicate over networks.

Phishing, smishing and vishing differ by communication medium.

Verified Previous Year Questions

PYQ 1: UGC NET, 30 September 2020, Shift 1

Which statements are correct?

A. Internet is larger than the intranet. B. Intranet is larger than Internet. C. Internet and intranet are the same. D. Internet is a network of networks. E. World Wide Web is an example of intranet.

  • Options
  • B and E only
  • A and D only
  • C and E only
  • B and C only
Answer: 2. A and D only

Explanation

Internet has global coverage and is a network of interconnected networks. Intranet is a restricted private network. WWW is an Internet-based service, not an intranet.

  • C and D only
  • A, C and D only

Explanation
